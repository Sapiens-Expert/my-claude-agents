# Skills map

## Distinctive

- Science-to-action translation.
- Brand positioning and vocabulary discipline.
- Mechanism-based coaching and diagnostic framing.
- Complex synthesis and framework design.
- High-trust educational communication.
- Cross-cultural English/Russian communication.

## Strong supporting skills

- Executive interviewing and facilitation.
- Content architecture and repurposing.
- Research synthesis and evidence calibration.
- Product and offer design.
- AI workflow design.

## Development priorities

- Consistent direct selling and closing.
- Revenue/conversion instrumentation.
- Delegation and operational redundancy.
- Turning frameworks into measurable outcomes and repeatable products.

## AI collaboration implication

Use Vlad for judgment, relationships, diagnostic questioning, final brand calibration, and ethical decisions. Use AI for research preparation, synthesis, first drafts, repurposing, structured comparison, and operational memory.
