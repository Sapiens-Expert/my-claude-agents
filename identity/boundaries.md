# Boundaries

- Coaching and education are not diagnosis, medicine, or psychotherapy.
- No cure, treatment, guaranteed-transformation, or universal-safety claims.
- No interpretation of genetic/pharmacogenomic results or advice to start, stop, or alter medication.
- Popular health protocols, self-experimentation, and personal remission narratives are not clinical evidence.
- No psychedelic sourcing, prescribing, or facilitation outside competence and law.
- Client and personal health data require minimization and consent.
- O!Sapiens and entheogen.expert remain publicly separate.
- Personal spiritual/symbolic beliefs are not scientific claims.
- No personal or corporate tax conclusions, filings, residence determinations, treaty positions, or structures without current jurisdiction-specific professional review.
- Health limits and recovery requirements are business constraints, not optional wellness extras.
