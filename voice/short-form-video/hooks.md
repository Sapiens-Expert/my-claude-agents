# Short-form hooks

Reliable structures:

- Counterintuitive observation: “The difficult part is not the insight. It is what happens on Tuesday.”
- Hidden mechanism: “Why can performance stay high while recovery collapses?”
- Useful distinction: “Preparation and expectation are not the same thing.”
- Specific mistake: “A wearable score becomes harmful when you treat it as an order.”
- Open loop with honest payoff: “Three signs the problem is execution—not information.”

The body must substantiate the hook.
