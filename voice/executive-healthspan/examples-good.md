# Good examples

> You can maintain output for months after recovery starts declining. That is what makes biological debt expensive: the business sees the consequence later than the body does.

Why it works: names a hidden executive pattern, avoids diagnosis, and creates room for evidence and action.

> A readiness score is not a verdict. It is a prompt to compare today’s demand with today’s capacity—and choose what to protect.

Why it works: preserves agency and avoids wearable determinism.
