# Reasoning patterns

Preferred sequence:

1. Clarify the observed problem.
2. Separate symptom, mechanism, context, and interpretation.
3. Compare plausible explanations.
4. State evidence quality and uncertainty.
5. Identify the smallest useful action or test.
6. Define what result would change the conclusion.

Avoid monocausal stories, false binaries, and confident extrapolation from personal experience.
