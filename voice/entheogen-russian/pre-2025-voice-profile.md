# Pre-2025 Telegram voice profile (imitation guide)

Derived from a frequency and phrase analysis of the "Энтеогенный Ренессанс" Telegram archive, split at 2025-01-01. Full method and numbers: [Telegram archive: structured database and voice-drift analysis](../../research/telegram-archive-voice-analysis.md). This file is the imitation guide, not the paper. Use it when drafting Telegram or channel-adjacent copy that should sound like Vlad wrote it himself, not like a summarized article. It supplements, doesn't replace, [voice.md](voice.md) and [disclaimer.md](disclaimer.md); safety and evidence rules there still apply.

## Why this exists

Posts before 2025-01-01 and posts after it are measurably different in shape. Post-2025 defaulted to a template: bolded headline, bullet list of "key findings," hashtag block, source link. That's a legitimate content type but it reads like a digest, not like a person. Pre-2025 posts read like someone narrating what he noticed, joked about, or decided. If new writing (including AI-assisted drafting) keeps defaulting to the easier template, the original voice becomes unrecoverable except by digging through the archive. This file locks in the fingerprint of the earlier voice so it can be deliberately chosen, not accidentally lost.

## Two flavors within pre-2025

- **Founding voice** (Sept-Oct 2023, roughly the channel's first month): high-energy, a rhetorical question voiced *as the skeptical reader*, stacked punctuation, occasional English slang, hashtags used inline as topic labels mid-post rather than as an end block.
- **Settled voice** (2024): calmer, still first-person and narrative, less clickbait, more willing to just tell you what he read, thought, or decided. This is the more usable everyday register; the founding voice is a spike tool for a strong hook, not a whole-post register.

Both are pre-2025 and both are legitimate; pick the one that fits the moment.

## Structural rules

1. **Shorter than the post-2025 default.** Target 100-160 words, not 200+. If it's running long, cut the hedge sentences, not the personality.
2. **Hashtags are optional, not a ritual.** Pre-2025 only 29% of posts had any hashtag at all. Don't append a 3-5 tag block by habit; use a tag only when it names something the post is actually about, and it's fine to drop them entirely.
3. **No mandatory "Источник:" callout.** Pre-2025 links are woven into a sentence ("Слушал на прогулке [эпизод]...", "Тем временем в... пишут про..."), not appended as a separate labeled line. Reserve the standalone source line for when you're explicitly citing a study, not for every post with a link.
4. **Minimal bold markdown.** Pre-2025 bolded text in only 4.3% of posts, almost always to define a term, not to shout a headline.
5. **Open mid-thought, not with an announcement.** Don't open with "Всем привет!" or "🔥 НОВЫЙ ЭПИЗОД:". Open like you're continuing a conversation: "Тем временем в...", "А что там...", "На этой неделе...", "Не могу не...", "По следам прошлого поста...".

## Voice rules

6. **First person carries a decision or observation, not a citation frame.** Write "Слушал на прогулке эпизод..." or "Мой совет: ..." rather than "я решил разобрать это исследование". The pronoun should attach to something he did, noticed, or is telling the reader to do, not to the act of summarizing.
7. **It's allowed to be unfinished, associative, and self-aware about that.** Pre-2025 example: "Как вы уже сами смогли заметить, моё повествование строится не совсем системно и линейно" (2023-10-02). Naming the messiness out loud is on-voice; a tightly organized bullet digest is off-voice for this register.
8. **A joke at his own expense beats a hedge.** "Нашёлся какой-то чел, который начал меня закидывать помидорами. Но я всё же продолжу..." (2024-03-29) is the model: acknowledge pushback, don't defend, keep going.
9. **The founding-voice hook device**, used sparingly for a strong open: voice the reader's imagined skepticism as an exaggerated question, then answer it straight. *"Что??!? Энтеогены лечат мигрень??? Серьёзно?"* then a real, sourced answer. Don't overuse it; it's a hook for a punchy post, not a tic for every post.
10. **Emoji as punctuation, not decoration.** Pre-2025 emoji sit at the end of a sentence to land a tone (🤯 🫠 😁), not scattered through a bullet list. Fewer than post-2025 (1.96 vs 2.82 per post); resist the urge to over-emoji.
11. **Never** open with a generic greeting, stack more than one hashtag-block per post, or write "важно отметить" / "стоит упомянуть" hedges: those markers cluster in the post-2025 digest register.

## Quick self-check before posting in this register

- Would this survive being read out loud as something one specific person noticed today, or does it read like a summarized abstract?
- Did a hashtag block or a bold headline sneak in out of habit?
- Is there one first-person sentence that's a decision or observation, not a citation frame?
- Word count under ~160?

If more than one answer is wrong, it's drifted into the post-2025 digest register.
