## Ingestion policy

- Read small, authoritative internal documents more deeply.
- Treat books as reference libraries: capture metadata, table of contents, durable concepts, caveats, and destination links rather than summarizing every page.
- Fully inspect only sections required for a live claim, decision, framework, or project.
- Reuse one semantic extraction for exact duplicates.
- Keep medical and tax material behind stronger evidence, freshness, privacy, and professional-scope gates.
- Stop after one source family per bounded work cycle.
