## Live claim risk — flagged in the source as "everything else can wait a week, this cannot"

Four lines were live on the public profile at audit time that read as exactly the kind of claim `identity/boundaries.md` and `brands/osapiens/banned-language.md` already forbid — coaching cannot be framed as repairing or treating biological dysfunction, and unsupported outcome promises are a rank-1 verification failure.

| Line, as published | Why it's a problem |
| --- | --- |
| "identify and **rebuild** the biological systems" (headline) | Direct violation of the standing rule against claiming coaching repairs or treats biological dysfunction. Highest-risk line on the whole profile. |
| "Deeper, more restorative sleep **within the first two weeks**, confirmed by wearable data where available" | A dated, specific outcome promise tied to a physiological measure, no stated evidence base. Same category CLM-0006 already flags as unvalidated. |
| "leaves you **60% charged**" | Invented precision — reads as measured, isn't. |
| "**almost certainly** not operating at their actual ceiling" | An unfalsifiable claim about a stranger who has never been assessed. |

Lower severity, same pass: "It is not a mindset problem. It is not a discipline problem. It is a biological problem." — diagnoses a reader who's told you nothing; the not-X-but-Y construction is a recognizable AI-writing tell.

## What's strong — do not touch

- The About section opens with the reader's reality, not Vlad's biography.
- "The cost of delivery has risen and you cannot fully explain why" — the single best sentence on the profile.
- "My background is not wellness. It is 20+ years of commercial performance" — the single most differentiating paragraph.
- The O!Sapiens Experience entry states the offer with real specifics (90 minutes, ten pillars, protocol in 48 hours) more clearly than About does.
- The Gap Partnership record (650+ executives coached, 50+ workshops) — already documented in `../../identity/credentials.md`, but currently underused in the profile's own About section.

## What's costing recognition, not creating risk

1. "Executive Healthspan Coach" occupies the headline's first three words — the least immediately relevant term available to a skeptical operator audience.
2. "Nervous System Regulation" sits second — wellness-adjacent, wrong first impression for this audience.
3. "1,000+ hours of research" is effort, not evidence, and this audience discounts effort — "650+ executives coached at multinationals" is real evidence, sitting unused.
4. No Featured section — the largest unforced omission; a convinced visitor has nowhere to go.
5. FANATIC and O!Sapiens both marked "present" with no line connecting them — reads as two unrelated businesses.

## Priority order, by commercial impact

1. Remove the four claim-risk lines.
2. Rewrite the headline.
3. Rewrite the first three lines of About; move the assessment link higher.
4. Build the Featured section.
5. Rewrite the O!Sapiens Experience entry with locked offer names.

## Voice-of-customer language bank

Patterns extracted from research whose underlying quotes couldn't be independently confirmed — **treat as phrasing to test, never as something a real client said.**

Recovery: "Weekends don't reset me anymore" · "Monday feels heavier than Friday" · "I sleep the hours and wake up as if I hadn't."
Effort inflation: "Everything takes more out of me than it used to" · "I am working harder to stand still."
Decision/cognition: "Decisions that used to be automatic now feel expensive" · "I do the actual thinking at eleven at night."
Control: "I can't switch off" · "I'm always available and I never decided to be."
Language that creates resistance (confirmed by the stigma data above): burnout, wellness, self-care, mental health (as an opener), holistic, healing, journey, unlock, transform, nervous system reset, adrenal fatigue, optimise your life, best self — consistent with `../../brands/osapiens/banned-language.md`.
