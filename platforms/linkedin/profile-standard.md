# LinkedIn profile standard

## Job of the profile

Help a relevant founder or senior operator answer quickly:

- Is this for someone like me?
- Does Vlad understand the problem at the right level?
- Is the mechanism and offer credible?
- What evidence and limits support the authority?
- What is the safest useful next step?

## Section architecture

| Section | Standard |
| --- | --- |
| Photo/banner | Professional recognition and category context; no visual clutter or unprovable claims |
| Headline | Audience + costly problem + distinctive mechanism/category + role; readable before keyword density |
| About | Recognizable situation → precise diagnosis → approach → evidence/limits → offer → restrained CTA |
| Featured | One diagnostic/offer asset, one authority piece, and one relevant proof asset; review regularly |
| Experience | Outcomes, responsibilities, and method with verifiable dates and claims |
| Credentials | Exact program/issuer wording; short courses are not degrees |
| Recommendations | Consent-based and representative; do not imply typical outcomes from selected testimonials |
| Contact | Clear route appropriate to intent and privacy |

## Trust standard

Trust combines demonstrated experience, relevant expertise, third-party or primary evidence, transparency, and accurate scope. Google E-E-A-T concepts may inform this trust audit, but its search-rater guidelines are not a LinkedIn ranking model.
