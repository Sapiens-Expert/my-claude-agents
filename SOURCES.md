## Exact duplicate policy

Thirty-seven exact-duplicate groups account for 53 redundant copies. Only one copy in each group needs semantic processing; all original paths remain preserved. See [the manifest](sources/MANIFEST.md) for the checkpoint groups and current totals.

Near-duplicates and different editions are not deducted from the unique-file count unless their hashes match. Overlapping content still requires authority, date, and provenance review.

## Authority principles

1. Current operating records and primary data outrank narrative plans.
2. Current law, official guidance, clinical guidance, and peer-reviewed primary evidence outrank books, commercial summaries, and generated reports within their domains.
3. A polished internal or AI-generated synthesis is not independent validation.
4. Plans, targets, forecasts, credentials, and outcomes remain unverified until supported by appropriate evidence.
5. Medical, psychedelic, tax, legal, privacy, and safety claims use stronger scope, freshness, and professional-referral gates.
6. Personal, client, genetic, health, financial, and tax information is private by default.

## Known evidence limitations

- The archive is rich in strategy and educational assets but sparse in confirmed revenue, conversion, retention, client outcomes, and execution data.
- Books are reference libraries, not automatic support for every claim they contain.
- Platform, medical, scientific, and tax material may become stale and requires targeted current verification when used for a live decision.
- Consumer genetic summaries, personal recovery stories, symbolic systems, and experiential reports do not establish diagnosis, causality, or general efficacy.
- Source-folder inclusion records provenance and relevance, not endorsement or professional competence.

## Maintenance

When adding a substantive source:

1. Preserve its original filename and provenance.
2. Check for exact duplicates.
3. Add it to the relevant source-family map with authority and use limits.
4. Update [the manifest](sources/MANIFEST.md), [the source-map index](research/source-maps/README.md), and [ingestion state](state/source-ingestion.md).
5. Update derived knowledge or claims only when the source changes a conclusion.
