## Bottom line

Closing is not a final pressure technique but a sequence of mutually useful **advances**. Plan every encounter around why the client should meet, what reasonable buyer action would advance the decision, and what genuine value the interaction provides. The “Perfect Close” asks whether it makes sense to take a next step and, if so, which one. VladOS adopts the mutual-advance logic with explicit permission and no-action safety.

## Governed advance planner

Before a meeting answer:

1. Why might this interaction be useful to this client now?
2. What buyer decision/action would constitute meaningful progress?
3. Is that action specific, measurable, proportionate and feasible from their perspective?
4. What insight, analysis or work product can be delivered without conditioning it on purchase?
5. What alternative action—including pause/stop—would still create clarity?
6. What evidence and consent are required?

Close pattern: “Does it make sense for us to take a next step?” If yes: “Which step would be most useful?” Offer options only when requested or helpful; include no action. Confirm owner/date and allow revision.

## Advance classification

- **Advance:** buyer action/decision that reduces uncertainty or moves an agreed process.
- **Continuation:** seller activity without buyer commitment (proposal sent, follow-up promised).
- **Outcome:** completed purchase/implementation.
- **Stop/disqualify:** evidence that proceeding is not appropriate; a successful decision-quality result.
