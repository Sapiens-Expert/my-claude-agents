## Bottom line

Good nonfiction is hard thinking expressed through simplicity, economy, unity, concrete words, human voice and extensive rewriting. The book combines durable principles with genre-specific methods and writer attitudes. ## Governed nonfiction workflow

1. Define one audience, purpose, controlling idea, scope and evidence standard.
2. Gather facts/voices with provenance and consent; distinguish memory from documented fact.
3. Draft for meaning and structure before polishing.
4. Enforce unity of pronoun, tense, tone and scope unless a deliberate shift is signaled.
5. Cut clutter, qualifiers without function, jargon, euphemism and repetitions—but retain material caveats.
6. Prefer concrete nouns and active verbs; vary rhythm and read aloud.
7. Build a lead that earns attention honestly and an ending that resolves without false certainty.
8. Fact-check, sensitivity/safety review, revise repeatedly and retain correction provenance.
