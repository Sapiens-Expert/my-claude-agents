## Bottom line

- Word of mouth depends not only on messenger reach but on properties of the idea, product and surrounding context.
- STEPPS names six transmission drivers: Social Currency, Triggers, Emotion, Public, Practical Value and Stories.
- The framework is strongest when used to diagnose why useful ideas are easy to remember, observe and retell; it becomes unsafe when used to manufacture outrage, status anxiety, deceptive scarcity or invisible advertising.
- Reach and sharing are intermediate signals. VladOS must preserve truth, brand linkage, audience benefit and downstream outcomes.

## Governed STEPPS

### Social Currency

People share things that help express identity, competence, taste or insider knowledge. Design for legitimate identity value through remarkable evidence, useful mastery and community contribution. Do not fabricate exclusivity, gamify vulnerable users, conceal sponsorship or turn clients into status props.

### Triggers

Link the idea to naturally recurring, relevant cues near the desired behavior. Record cue, context, frequency, audience, intended recall and competitive associations. Prefer durable fit over a clever but unrelated mnemonic; avoid hijacking trauma, health anxiety or sensitive moments.

### Emotion

Sharing is associated more with activating states such as awe, amusement, excitement, anger or anxiety than with low-arousal sadness/contentment. Use emotion to make consequence human and comprehensible, not to bypass judgment. High-stakes claims require evidence before emotional treatment.

### Public

Observable use and durable residue make behavior easier to imitate. Build voluntary, privacy-safe visibility—clear artifacts, signatures, rituals or shareable outputs. Do not expose health, psychedelic, coaching, employment or client status; do not publicize harmful behavior so strongly that it becomes normative.

### Practical Value

Package genuinely useful knowledge with scope, source, date, assumptions and actionable next step. Deal framing must show reference price, total cost and material restrictions truthfully. Practical value is receiver-centered; it must work without requiring a purchase or data surrender disproportionate to the benefit.

### Stories

Create a carrier narrative whose essential retelling preserves the claim, causal logic, brand/idea linkage and safe action. Test “plot stripping”: if memorable details are removed, does the intended meaning remain? Obtain consent, protect identities and distinguish representative experience from outcome promise.

## Contagion design card

1. Objective and downstream outcome—not “go viral.”
2. Defined audience and reason sharing would serve them.
3. Evidence/claim boundary and current source.
4. STEPPS present, absent and deliberately avoided.
5. Brand/message integral to retelling.
6. Privacy, dignity, safety and misinformation risks.
7. Distribution seed and owned destination.
8. Measurement: qualified reach, accurate recall, action, benefit and harm.
9. Decay/update rule and correction route.

## Evidence assessment and corrections

- Case histories demonstrate plausibility, not necessity or sufficiency. Successes such as Blendtec, hotmail-style signatures, Movember, Kit Kat/coffee and selected viral videos omit base rates and failed attempts.
- Sharing studies often measure email/social transmission in bounded contexts. Platform algorithms, format, paid support, network structure, source credibility and timing confound outcomes.
- Social Currency overlaps identity signaling; Emotion overlaps arousal; Public overlaps social proof; Stories can contain every other STEP. Treat STEPPS as lenses, not independent causal variables.
- Practical Value uses prospect-theory framing, but perceived bargains are not actual value. Verify denominator, reference price and total cost.
- Virality can accelerate falsehood faster than correction. Add a claim-risk gate, pre-bunking where appropriate, correction mechanics and monitoring.
- Public observability should not override autonomy. Private adoption can be the desired outcome.
- Avoid optimizing outrage because it shares. Track complaint, polarization, misinterpretation and vulnerable-audience effects.

## VladOS applications

- **LinkedIn:** use recurring executive-work cues, useful frameworks and evidence-bearing stories; measure accurate association and qualified conversation, not impressions alone.
- **FANATIC:** build distinctive, observable brand assets whose meaning survives retelling; do not confuse fame with strategic fit.
- **O!Sapiens:** use private progress cues and shareable education without exposing health data or turning adherence into public competition.
- **Entheogen Expert:** avoid sensational, awe-only or fear-driven content; foreground legality, contraindications, uncertainty and referral.
- **Executive coaching:** client stories require informed consent and typicality; the method, not the client's vulnerability, should carry the message.
