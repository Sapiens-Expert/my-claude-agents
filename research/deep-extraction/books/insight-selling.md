## Bottom line

Sales winners do more than discover stated needs: they **Connect**, **Convince** and **Collaborate**, bringing useful ideas that help buyers see possibilities and make a credible change decision. The durable contribution is the integration of relationship, differentiated value, trust and co-creation. VladOS must distinguish an insight from a provocative assertion: it needs evidence, relevance, uncertainty and room for rejection.

## Governed insight cycle

1. Establish context, trust and permission.
2. Diagnose the buyer's current reasoning, evidence, constraints and desired decision.
3. Form an insight: relevant pattern + credible evidence + implication + uncertainty.
4. Test it as a hypothesis: “Would it be useful to examine…?”
5. Connect it to the buyer's goals without manufacturing pain.
6. Compare status quo, internal action, alternatives and the seller's offer.
7. Co-create a feasible action or experiment with success/failure criteria.
8. Document learning and allow pause/no action.

## Insight quality contract

An insight must be specific to a decision, attributable, current enough, methodologically credible, non-obvious yet comprehensible, material, falsifiable and actionable. Disclose commercial interest, assumptions, range and counterevidence. Never repackage generic content as proprietary insight or use confidential data from another customer.

## Evidence and skills

- The study compares reported buying experiences and perceived behaviors; halo, outcome and recall bias are expected. Treat factors as hypotheses for local testing.
- “Connect–Convince–Collaborate” is not a rigid chronology; return to diagnosis and trust as evidence changes.
- Reusable D4 skills: insight-quality scorer, Connect–Convince–Collaborate planner, evidence-to-story bridge, buyer-risk map, trust audit and collaborative action experiment.
- Applications: LinkedIn thought leadership, FANATIC strategy, executive coaching and workshops; health/psychedelic insights require domain evidence and scope gates.

## D4 promotion contract
