# Solution Selling — Michael T. Bosworth

## Bottom line

The method shifts sellers from product features toward diagnosed business pain, buyer-created solution vision, access to power, mutually specified proof and process control. Its shared vocabulary remains useful, but “create buyers,” pain development, bargaining access and control letters can become manipulative. VladOS reframes the method as co-diagnosis, option design, evidence planning and mutual decision governance.

## Complete method

| Component | Contribution | Governance correction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Latent/active pain | Recognize problems/opportunities before pitching | Do not create or amplify pain beyond evidence |
| Reasons | Explore causes and impacts | Maintain competing explanations and scope limits |
| Vision processing | Help buyer articulate capabilities needed | Do not constrain vision to seller's product architecture |
| Agreement to explore | Establish a legitimate next phase | Explicit, revocable permission—not assumed momentum |
| Bargain/hooks | Exchange seller effort/proof for stakeholder access | No coercive quid pro quo or bypass of procurement/gatekeepers |
| Proof/decision vehicle | Jointly define evidence, criteria and demonstration | Include alternatives, failure criteria, implementation and risk |
| Power/buying process | Map authority, influence and approvals | Avoid reductive titles; include affected users, blockers and governance |
| Pipeline/milestones | Forecast through observable buyer progress | Remain probabilistic and allow disqualification |

## Governed solution-development protocol

1. Confirm role, purpose and permission.
2. Establish current state, problem/opportunity, evidence, impact and alternative causes.
3. Ask the buyer to define outcomes and solution capabilities without naming products.
4. Map stakeholders, decision process, procurement, implementation and people affected.
5. Compare no action, internal change and market alternatives.
6. Create a mutual evidence plan: claim, test, data, owner, threshold and failure response.
7. Match only credible capabilities; disclose gaps, dependencies and customization.
8. Agree a reversible next step; close or refer when fit is insufficient.

## Evidence and skills

- “Pain → Reasons → Vision” resembles later diagnostic methods, but buyer self-report and seller facilitation do not establish root cause.
- Proof plans improve rigor only when buyer-owned and capable of falsifying fit; a scripted demo is not proof.
- Reusable D4 skills: pain-to-evidence translator, capability-vision workshop, stakeholder/process map, mutual proof plan, decision-vehicle builder and opportunity disqualification audit.
- Applications: complex FANATIC/corporate sales; never apply commercial pain processing as clinical/health diagnosis.

## D4 promotion contract

Promote the mutual evidence plan, capability-vision workshop and decision-vehicle method after comparison with Gap, Collaborative and Challenger. Keep performance claims and 1995 channel assumptions source-bound; reject buyer creation through pressure or access bargaining.
