# The Neuroscience of Selling — John Asher

## Bottom line

The book organizes selling around six attention “activators” and 25 cognitive biases applied to prospecting, stakeholder access, rapport, listening and closing. Useful communication functions include buyer relevance, simplicity, contrast, concrete imagery, participation, note-taking and accurate summaries. VladOS must not promote the “old/reptilian brain,” emotion-versus-logic dichotomy, NLP mirroring or bias-triggering as settled neuroscience or permission to manipulate decisions.

## Scientific-claim and bias gate

For every tactic record the primary citation, study population/context, measured outcome, effect size/uncertainty, replication status, transfer distance to sales and possible harm. Label metaphors as metaphors. Never infer an individual's hidden mental state from gaze, posture, pace or “brain type.” Bias knowledge should improve decision hygiene—clear comparisons, cooling-off, base rates and reversibility—not weaponize loss aversion, liking, scarcity or consistency.

## Governed communication pattern

1. State purpose and buyer relevance without claiming to know their need.
2. Present one clear, concrete idea with truthful contrast and provenance.
3. Invite active evaluation, questions and correction.
4. Listen, ask permission to take notes, summarize and verify.
5. Provide logic, uncertainty, options and time—not emotional activation alone.
6. Ask for a proportionate next step and accept no.

## Evidence, applications and skills

- Damasio lesion findings do not establish a universal “emotion buys, logic justifies” sales law. Triune-brain/reptilian-brain framing is scientifically obsolete as a literal architecture.
- Reusable D4 skills: neuroscience-claim card, bias-to-debias translator, attention-design checklist, listening verification loop and reversible-commitment audit.
- Use in sales/LinkedIn only as communication QA. Health, coaching and psychedelic content must use domain-primary evidence and may not borrow sales neuroscience as clinical fact.
