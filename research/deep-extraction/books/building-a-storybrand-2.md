- Authority and intended audience: a commercial practitioner framework built from screen-story structure, consulting practice, client testimonials and promotional examples. Highly actionable for copy development; weak as independent causal evidence of revenue effects.

## Bottom line

- Central thesis: customers should be positioned as the hero and the brand as a guide in a seven-part narrative—character, problem, guide, plan, call to action, failure and success—expressed in clear, repeatable sound bites.
- Strongest contribution: SB7 forces a company to state what the customer wants, the problem being solved, the brand’s role, the path, next action and outcome rather than leading with company biography or abstract claims.
- Main limitation: a universal story template can flatten diverse buying journeys, exaggerate pain and transformation, and confuse copy clarity with strategy or causation. Most performance support is selected client evidence without controls.
- Safe VladOS use: treat a BrandScript as a messaging hypothesis produced after strategy, research, evidence and scope decisions; test comprehension and outcomes, and remove fear, false-guide authority or transformation claims that exceed proof.

## Argument map

People ignore messages that require too much effort to decode. A story provides a familiar structure for prioritizing information. The customer wants something but faces an external, internal and philosophical problem. The brand earns a supporting role by demonstrating empathy and relevant authority, then provides a clear process or agreement plan, calls the customer to act, names appropriate stakes and shows a desirable outcome.

The BrandScript compresses these elements into repeatable sound bites and a controlling idea. Those elements are then deployed across a one-liner, website, lead generator, email/text nurture, testimonials and referral system. Repetition is intended to improve comprehension and response.

The sound logic is narrower than the book’s growth claims: clear customer-centered language can reduce comprehension friction. It does not follow that every buyer experiences a heroic transformation, narrative messaging alone causes revenue, or the framework fits every context. Offer quality, price, distribution, category cues, evidence, reputation, timing and competitive conditions remain causal candidates.

## SB7 framework adapted for VladOS

| SB7 element | Required question | Evidence needed | Safety boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Character | Who is making which decision, and what relevant progress do they seek? | Audience/job research, category entry point, decision context | Do not reduce a heterogeneous audience to one persona |
| Problem | What observable problem exists, and how is it experienced? | Direct language, behavior, consequences, root-cause uncertainty | Never invent shame, diagnosis, villain or philosophical injustice |
| Guide | Why is this brand an appropriate resource? | Relevant qualifications, method, track record, empathy and limits | Guide is a role metaphor, not clinical, moral or outcome authority |
| Plan | What are the actual steps, prerequisites and responsibilities? | Delivery process, client effort, timelines, risks and referral paths | Keep complexity that materially affects consent or outcomes |
| Action | What is the smallest appropriate next step? | Journey data, readiness and channel expectations | Voluntary, proportionate, reversible; no false urgency |
| Failure | What realistic cost follows from no action or a poor fit? | Base rates, time horizon and alternative pathways | Do not magnify fear or imply that buying is the only safe choice |
| Success | What outcome can reasonably be expected, for whom and when? | Outcome data, variability and measurement definition | No guarantee, identity rescue or transformation beyond evidence |
| Transformation | What capability or identity may develop through participation? | Longitudinal evidence and participant language | Optional, non-shaming and never a condition of belonging |
| Controlling idea | What single meaning should organize this message? | Strategy and audience comprehension | Link to deeper evidence and boundary layers |

## BrandScript worksheet, upgraded

1. **Decision and audience:** name the buyer/user, situation, current alternative and desired progress.
2. **Problem:** record direct audience language; distinguish symptoms, causes, internal response and unsupported interpretation.
3. **Offer and guide proof:** state mechanism, qualifications, evidence, empathy, limitations, conflicts and non-fit.
4. **Plan:** state steps, responsibilities, duration, effort, price/cost range, safety gates and stop/referral conditions.
5. **Actions:** define one direct action and one genuinely useful transitional action; state data/consent implications.
6. **Stakes:** describe likely benefits and costs neutrally with base rates, uncertainty and alternatives.
7. **Outcome:** define observable success, time horizon, variation and what is not promised.
8. **Core message:** compress only after the full record is accurate; link or layer the evidence and boundaries.
9. **Test:** assess correct comprehension, recall, attribution, qualified response, conversion, outcomes, complaints and harm.

## Website guidance: retained and corrected

Retain the five practical ideas: a clear above-fold offer, visible primary action, relevant outcome/product imagery, comprehensible offer architecture and scannable copy. Add requirements the book underweights:

- tested navigation and accessibility rather than a universal “few words” rule;
- category cues and distinctive brand assets for correct recognition and attribution;
- claims evidence, qualifications, disclaimers and material limitations near relevant statements;
- privacy, security, cookie/form minimization and consent quality;
- search discoverability, page performance and multiple audience journeys;
- controlled testing of comprehension and incremental outcomes rather than testimonials alone.

## Ethical and safety guardrails

- The audience is a participant or decision-maker, not a fictional character whose emotions the brand controls.
- Do not label normal uncertainty, aging, stress, grief, illness or lack of purchase as a “hole” requiring rescue.
- External, internal and philosophical problems must come from research and be phrased without shame, diagnosis or invented injustice.
- Empathy cannot be scripted as proof of care; authority must be relevant, current, verifiable and explicitly bounded.
- Never imply that O!Sapiens, coaching or psychedelic education guarantees health, healing, transformation, agency or identity completion.
- Failure stakes must include no-action and alternative options; prohibit fear amplification, false dichotomies and urgency fabrication.
- Testimonials require informed consent, privacy protection, truthful editing, material-context disclosure and no implication of typical results.
- Referral rewards must be disclosed and must not encourage sharing another person’s contact or health information without consent.
- AI-generated copy is draft material: verify facts, claims, tone, audience fit, prohibited targeting, originality and current platform/legal requirements.

## Limitations and do-not-infer

- The book is closely tied to the author’s paid tools, certifications and AI product; commercial examples are not independent validation.
- Testimonials and before/after revenue stories lack controls, full denominators, costs, time-series context and unsuccessful implementations.
- Film-story structure is an analogy for marketing, not evidence that all decisions follow a seven-part narrative.
- Claims about survival-focused brains, attention and action simplify cognitive science and should not be treated as neuroscience authority.
- Do not infer that customers care only about themselves, never about company story, or always want a guide.
- Do not infer that every product solves an internal/philosophical problem, needs negative stakes, or creates identity transformation.
- Do not infer that fewer words always improve websites, clear copy alone drives growth, or reported conversions establish incrementality.
- Do not deploy the same BrandScript unchanged across audience, product, platform, geography or language without research and testing.
