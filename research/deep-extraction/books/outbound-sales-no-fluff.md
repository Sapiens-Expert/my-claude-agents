## Bottom line

The book reduces outbound to a workable production flow: define the company swimlane, identify people, find permitted contact paths, use phone-led outreach, move records through four lead buckets and model funnel math. Its operational clarity is useful; VladOS adds consent, data provenance, stop rules, quality balances and uncertainty.

## Governed bucket system

- **B0 excluded/suppressed:** never contact.
- **B1 unverified account:** research minimum evidence and permissible contact basis.
- **B2 verified account/person, not reached:** bounded outreach by approved cadence.
- **B3 conversation/response:** qualify problem, fit, timing and consent; agree follow-up or stop.
- **B4 mutually scheduled meeting:** confirm purpose and preparation; track show/quality.
- **B5 recycle/nurture:** only with permission and review date.

Every transition needs evidence and owner. No response cannot be upgraded to interest.

## Funnel math and skills

Model `eligible records × reach rate × positive-response rate × meeting acceptance × show rate × qualification × opportunity conversion × win × retained value`, using confidence ranges. Add negative response, opt-out, complaint, invalid-data, early churn and seller-capacity metrics.

- Reusable D4 skills: swimlane builder, bucket-state machine, daily queue generator, funnel sensitivity model and automation integrity audit.
- The Pareto framing and author ratios are heuristics; cohort results vary by market, offer, channel, brand and data quality.
- Apply to LinkedIn/social selling only with context and permission; never target sensitive health/psychedelic signals.
