## Bottom line

The writer's job is to create a superior reader experience. Stein emphasizes compelling openings, particularity, characterization through action, conflict, suspense, dialogue, scene, metaphor, nonfiction enhancement and systematic revision. VladOS should retain reader-energy and revision diagnostics while rejecting spectacle, conflict or compression when they distort evidence, exploit subjects or violate a nonfiction contract.

## Governed reader-experience edit

1. State genre contract: fiction, memoir, reported nonfiction, argument or instruction.
2. Identify the reader promise and controlling tension/question.
3. Audit opening, scene purpose, particularity, action, voice and progression.
4. Remove summary where a concise concrete example helps; do not invent detail.
5. Test dialogue/quotation against records and permissions.
6. Cut flab and redundancy while preserving evidence, nuance and accessibility.
7. Run separate structure, fact, ethics/sensitivity, line and proof passes.
8. Confirm the ending satisfies the promise without manufactured closure.

## Skills and promotion

- Reusable D4 skills: first-page diagnostic, particularity audit, scene-purpose test, tension-without-distortion gate, dialogue/quotation check, triage revision and nonfiction contract audit.
- Strong for books, scripts, case patterns and personal stories; research/health content must privilege evidence over drama.
- Promote the revision diagnostics after synthesis with Zinsser and story sources; retain aesthetic prescriptions as options, not universal rules.
