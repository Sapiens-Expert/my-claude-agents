## Bottom line

Modern buyers need sellers who collaborate as informed equals. The framework combines **situational fluency** with three roles: **Micro-Marketer**, **Visualizer** and **Value Driver**, operating through a dynamic buyer-aligned process. VladOS retains transparent collaboration, joint vision and value verification while updating dated social-platform assumptions and guarding against role-play that only simulates buyer partnership.

## Situational-fluency contract

A seller can explain the buyer's business context, relevant workflows, stakeholder constraints, problem mechanisms, available solution classes, their own capability and limits, implementation requirements, value evidence and open uncertainties. Fluency is demonstrated through accurate questions and synthesis—not jargon or confidence.

## Governed three-role cycle

1. **Micro-Marketer:** identify a relevant audience; listen only to permitted/public signals; contribute attributable value; request engagement transparently.
2. **Visualizer:** facilitate current/future-state and option design; keep buyer language; reveal trade-offs; never force the vision toward one product.
3. **Value Driver:** specify baseline, outcome, mechanism, probability, cost, adoption, risk and measurement owner; revisit after implementation.

Use roles as capabilities, not personality classifications. A single interaction may need all three or an expert handoff.

## Evidence and skills

- Buyer/seller survey findings and client cases support relevance, not causal superiority. “Collective intelligence” claims do not mean every group process beats expertise.
- Reusable D4 skills: situational-fluency assessment, three-role planner, buyer-alignment state map, collaborative vision canvas, value verification plan and enablement pilot.
- Strong fit for FANATIC and corporate workshops; sensitive domains require qualified experts and explicit consent.

## D4 promotion contract
