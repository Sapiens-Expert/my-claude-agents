# The Elements of Style: Classic Edition (2018) — William Strunk Jr., edited by Richard De A'Morelli

## Bibliographic record

- Spectrum Ink Publishing, 2018; classic Strunk text with editor's notes, new chapters and study guide.
- One 119-page English PDF; SHA-256 `8158f968d71c01edad962adcdee4f88ba9c6be18450e483cbf578dc140dfde48`; about 24,134 words; no OCR required.
- Complete coverage: eight chapters—usage, composition, capitalization, form, words/expressions, spelling, added style rules and study guide.
- Authority: historically influential prescriptive guide in a modern commercial edition; many preferences are contested, register-specific or superseded.

## Bottom line

The guide offers compact reminders about punctuation, sentence/paragraph construction, form, usage and concision. Its enduring value is disciplined revision: make the paragraph the unit, prefer definite/concrete language, use active constructions when appropriate, put emphatic words in strong positions and omit needless words. VladOS should treat every maxim as a diagnostic prompt, not a universal prohibition.

## Governed concise-style pass

1. Confirm audience, variant, genre and house style.
2. Check paragraph purpose/unity and logical sequence.
3. Prefer concrete subjects and verbs where they clarify agency.
4. Move emphasis deliberately and cut words with no semantic/rhetorical work.
5. Retain caveats, definitions, transitions and repetition required for safety/accessibility.
6. Verify disputed usage with current Garner/dictionary/domain sources.
7. Preserve intentional voice and query uncertain meaning.
