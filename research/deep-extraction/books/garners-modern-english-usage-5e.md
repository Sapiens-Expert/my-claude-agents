## Bottom line

This is a query-time authority, not a universal rulebook. It distinguishes standard/nonstandard forms, confusing words, grammar, punctuation, rhetoric, legal/professional usage, bias and stages of language change. VladOS should use it to investigate specific English-usage questions, record alternatives and register, and harmonize house style—never to flatten authorial voice, regional English or evolving inclusive usage.

## Reference architecture

- Alphabetical word/phrase entries: spelling, meaning, distinctions, idiom and illustrative citations.
- Thematic essays: grammar, syntax, punctuation, rhetoric, style, editorial practice and linguistic concepts.
- **Language-Change Index:** five-stage progression from innovation/error toward universal acceptance, with empirical ratios where available.
- Editorial guide: recurring problems such as ambiguity, chronology, cliché, jargon, parallelism, modifiers, pronouns, redundancy, tone and quotation handling.
- Cross-references and bibliography support deeper retrieval and comparison.

## Governed usage decision

1. Identify audience, jurisdiction/variant, medium, register, house style and accessibility need.
2. Retrieve the exact Garner entry plus its cross-references and stage label.
3. Check a current dictionary/corpus and relevant domain style guide; usage changes after publication.
4. Distinguish correctness, convention, clarity, tone, inclusivity and preference.
5. Choose the least disruptive edit that improves the text; preserve intentional voice.
6. Record non-obvious decisions in the style sheet with source/date.
7. Query the author when meaning or intent is uncertain.

## Limits and skills

- Corpus ratios describe printed frequency, not intrinsic correctness or fitness for every audience. Examples can reflect historical bias.
- Regional/world English, identity terminology and technology vocabulary may require newer or community-specific authorities.
- Reusable D4 skills: usage-query router, language-change classifier, confusing-word resolver, house-style decision record, register/variant check and author-query generator.
- Use as retrieval infrastructure under `voice/` and editing SOPs; do not bulk-apply every preference mechanically.
