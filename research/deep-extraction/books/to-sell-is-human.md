## Bottom line

Much modern work involves “non-sales selling”—moving others to exchange attention, effort or resources. In information-rich markets, seller advantage weakens and the governing ABC becomes **Attunement, Buoyancy, Clarity**, supported by pitching, improvisation and service. VladOS should retain perspective-taking, resilient reflection, problem finding, listening and service, while correcting strategic mimicry, universal claims and any equation of influence with entitlement to move another person.

## Governed ABC-service model

1. **Attune:** map perspectives, incentives, knowledge and power; ask rather than assume.
2. **Buoy:** prepare realistic goals, regulate emotion, interpret setbacks specifically and seek systemic causes/support.
3. **Clarify:** identify the right problem, curate credible evidence and disclose framing choices/alternatives.
4. **Pitch:** match format to context; answer what the audience should know, feel and be able to do without emotional coercion.
5. **Improvise:** listen fully, build where appropriate, disagree honestly and preserve the other party's agency.
6. **Serve:** test whether the action improves the recipient's situation and remains defensible if publicly explained.
