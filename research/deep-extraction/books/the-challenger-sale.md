## Governed commercial insight choreography

Adapt the book's sequence:

1. **Warm-up/context:** establish the buyer's situation and permission.
2. **Reframe:** offer a tentative alternative interpretation, not a surprise attack.
3. **Evidence:** show attributable data, scope, sample, uncertainty and counterexamples.
4. **Implication:** jointly estimate material consequences without emotional inflation.
5. **A better way:** describe outcome/capability criteria, including non-seller options.
6. **Seller fit:** explain distinctive capability, limitations and proof plan.
7. **Decision:** invite evaluation, pause or refusal.

An insight must lead to a buyer-useful decision even if the seller is not selected. Never engineer a “rational drowning/emotional impact” sequence around fear.

## Tailoring and process leadership

- Tailor to role-relevant goals, constraints and public/business evidence; validate rather than stereotype.
- Map economic, technical, user, procurement, security and implementation perspectives.
- “Take control” becomes: surface decisions, clarify trade-offs, name avoidance, protect mutual deadlines, and accept no.
- Negotiation concessions require a plan, reciprocity without coercion, and protection of delivery/customer outcomes.
