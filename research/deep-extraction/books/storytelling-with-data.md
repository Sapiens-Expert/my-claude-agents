## Bottom line

Effective data communication starts with context and audience action, chooses a fit-for-purpose display, removes non-informative clutter, guides attention, applies design principles and constructs an honest narrative. VladOS should promote this six-step workflow with stronger data provenance, uncertainty, accessibility and anti-misleading gates.

## Governed visualization workflow

1. Define audience, decision/question, action and evidence boundary.
2. Audit data provenance, definitions, denominators, missingness, uncertainty and comparability.
3. Write the Big Idea as a complete, falsifiable sentence with stakes and caveat.
4. Choose the simplest display that supports the actual comparison.
5. Use honest scales, ordering and baselines; show uncertainty/material exceptions.
6. Remove only non-informative clutter; use hierarchy to aid—not manipulate—attention.
7. Add title takeaway, labels, source/date, units, sample and accessible text alternative.
8. Test what viewers notice, conclude and decide; revise errors.
