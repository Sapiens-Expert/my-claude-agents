## Bottom line

Complex sales are multi-person, dynamic systems. The method's six elements—Buying Influences, Red Flags/Strengths, Response Modes, Win-Results, Ideal Customer and Sales Funnel—force opportunity and territory strategy beyond a single contact. VladOS retains stakeholder coverage, uncertainty flags, mutual outcomes and allocation discipline while replacing political manipulation, “coach” dependence and title-based role assumptions with transparent stakeholder/decision mapping.

## Governed strategic-selling blueprint

1. Define the specific sales objective and customer outcome.
2. Map **decision roles** rather than fixed titles: economic authorization, user impact, technical/procurement evaluation, implementation, governance and internal coordination.
3. Record each stakeholder's desired outcomes, concerns, evidence, influence and engagement status.
4. Identify red flags, strengths, missing information and uncertainty; never convert unknown to green.
5. Assess change posture (growth, trouble, even keel, overconfident) as a tentative situational hypothesis—not a personality type.
6. Define mutual wins and harms; prohibit win-lose designs.
7. Compare competition broadly: status quo, internal work, budget alternatives and other vendors.
8. Choose actions, owners, permission and review date; disqualify when ethical/fit conditions fail.

## Internal-guide integrity

An internal guide may explain process and facilitate introductions but must not disclose confidential information, circumvent policy or be pressured to advocate. Verify claims through appropriate stakeholders and systems. Record conflicts of interest and never hide the seller relationship.
