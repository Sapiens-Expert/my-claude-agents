## Bottom line

The book aligns sales activities with a proposed buying process: recognition, identification, assessment, decision, purchase and post-sale evaluation. It covers questions, motivation, value, competition, incremental commitments and presentation design. VladOS retains the decision-stage map and evidence-aware communication tools while rejecting “make problems hurt,” simplistic brain claims and lab-to-sales certainty.

## Evidence-governed decision map

1. **Recognition:** verify a material problem/opportunity without making it hurt.
2. **Identification:** define outcome and solution classes broadly.
3. **Assessment:** compare options, evidence, risks and constraints.
4. **Decision:** support stakeholders, criteria and informed choice.
5. **Purchase:** confirm terms, consent, expectations and implementation.
6. **Evaluation:** measure actual outcome, harm, adoption and learning.

At every stage, allow return to earlier questions, pause, referral or no action.

## Scientific-claim translation gate

For each claimed tactic record: original citation; study design/sample/context; outcome measured; effect size/uncertainty; replication/current review; distance from sales context; plausible harms; wording allowed. Ban neural labels (“reptilian brain,” subconscious switch) when they add no validated predictive value. Correlation, laboratory effect or population average cannot guarantee an individual response.

## Governed questions, value and presentation

- Move from facts → meaning → implications → options, but never intensify fear for conversion.
- Value case = verified outcome × attributable probability/time minus full cost, risk and change burden.
- Competitive claims require comparable definitions and provenance.
- Present one decision-relevant structure, remove distractions, use concrete evidence, show limitations and check comprehension.
- Incremental commitments are explicit, proportionate and reversible.

## Evidence and skills

- Popular synthesis increases accessibility but risks citation laundering, overgeneralization and replication-age problems.
- Reusable D4 skills: research-to-sales claim card, decision-stage mapper, behavioral tactic audit, evidence-led presentation planner, ethical motivation gate and post-sale evaluation loop.
- Medical/psychedelic/health applications require primary domain evidence and cannot inherit sales-level behavioral claims.

## D4 promotion contract

Promote the scientific-claim gate, decision map and post-sale loop after primary-source verification. Keep all tactic efficacy/mechanism claims source-bound; prohibit pain amplification and individual mind-reading.
