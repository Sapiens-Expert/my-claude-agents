# Oversubscription reliably creates profit, desirability and market advantage

## Claim

Building demand before release and constraining availability makes an offer more desirable, profitable and resilient because people want what others want and compete for limited access.

## Assessment

**Unsupported as a general causal claim.** Selected premium-brand, event and founder cases do not establish that restricting supply creates durable value or profit. Excess demand may reflect hype, novelty, status, underpricing, inequitable access, weak forecasting or insufficient capacity. Launch revenue omits fulfilment, refunds, working capital, retention and customer outcomes.

## Safe use

Plan demand around excellent-delivery capacity. Use only auditable capacity/time constraints, proportionate contact and transparent allocation. Diagnose demand quality, economics, delivery, exclusion and customer outcomes before expanding or repeating a campaign.

Source analysis: [Oversubscribed, Second Edition](../deep-extraction/books/oversubscribed-2e.md).
