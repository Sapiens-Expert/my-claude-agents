# Copy flow and psychological triggers reliably produce buying action

## Claim

Product knowledge, a strong organizing concept, seamless sentence-to-sentence flow and selected psychological triggers reliably compel readers to continue and purchase across offers and media.

## Assessment

**Strong craft hypotheses, unsupported as a universal causal claim.** The source rests primarily on practitioner judgment, selected direct-response cases and incomplete campaign economics. Many media prescriptions date to 2007. Continued reading does not establish comprehension, consent, customer benefit or incrementality, and emotional-versus-logical buying claims are oversimplified.

## Safe use

Use concept and flow to create a voluntary clarity path. Test entry, explanation, evidence, objections, complete offer, navigation/action and voice. Preserve defer/decline options, and evaluate qualified decisions, delivery, refunds, complaints and harm rather than response alone.

Source analysis: [The Adweek Copywriting Handbook](../deep-extraction/books/adweek-copywriting-handbook.md).
