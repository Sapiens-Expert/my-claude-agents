# Customer-interest programs reliably create deeper relationships and growth

## Claim

Building or joining programs around customers' interests reliably creates deeper brand relationships, trust and commercial growth.

## Assessment

**Rejected as a blanket inference; specific programs remain unvalidated.** The source relies primarily on selected cases and practitioner logic. Participation may reflect pre-existing affinity, and engagement does not establish incrementality, correct attribution, trust or downstream value.

## Safe use

Treat each program as a bounded hypothesis. Establish participant value before promotional value; require legitimate fit, consent, privacy, moderation, accessibility and non-extractive practice; compare build, partner, support and no-program options; and measure incremental relevant reach, attribution, behavior, cost and harm.
