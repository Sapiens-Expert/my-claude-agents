# Behavior execution gap

## Evidence position

Knowledge is frequently insufficient for sustained action. Planning, self-efficacy, context, friction, stress, feedback, recovery, and accountability affect execution. This is a synthesis, not evidence that every health failure shares one cause.

## Safe public formulation

“In preventive health, the limiting problem is often not access to information but the ability to translate intention into repeated action.”
