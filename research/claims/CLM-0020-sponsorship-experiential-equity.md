# Sponsorship and experiential programs reliably build brand equity

## Claim

Sponsorship, events, digital participation and other non-advertising experiences reliably build stronger brand equity and relationships than conventional communication.

## Assessment

**Rejected as a blanket comparative claim; specific programs remain unvalidated.** Selected historical successes do not establish typical effects. Outcomes depend on audience fit, activation, correct attribution, incremental reach, operations, opportunity cost and reputational events. Exposure equivalents and participation do not establish causality.

## Safe use

Choose channels by a defined program job. Compare alternatives, predefine incremental behavioral and brand-linked outcomes, include full costs and guardrails, govern partners as bidirectional co-brand risks, and attach stop and exit rules.
