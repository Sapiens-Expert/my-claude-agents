# Category leaders reliably capture most category value

## Claim

The company recognized as the category leader reliably captures roughly 70–76% of the category's economic value, and deliberate category design substantially improves the odds of achieving that position.

## Assessment

**Unvalidated and rejected as a portable quantitative claim.** The book describes an author-defined analysis concentrated in public technology companies but does not provide a reproducible company list, category-boundary rules, comparison design or full statistical analysis. Selection, survivorship, network effects, timing and category definition can materially change the result.

## Safe use

Describe category leadership as a possible source of reinforcing demand, ecosystem, capital or talent effects in some markets. Specify and test the local mechanism. Do not cite the percentages, promise dominance or call a VladOS brand a “category king” without independent market evidence.
