# Physiological erosion before perceived decline

## Evidence position

People can maintain visible output through compensation while sleep, recovery, stress, or physiological markers worsen. The timing and meaning of any marker vary, and subjective performance is not one construct.

## Safe public formulation

“Recovery and physiological signals can deteriorate before a high performer notices a clear drop in output.”

## Avoid

“Your body always knows before you do” or claims that one wearable metric proves hidden damage.
