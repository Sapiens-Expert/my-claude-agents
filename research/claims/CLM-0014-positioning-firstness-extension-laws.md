# Positioning firstness and line-extension rules generalize reliably

## Claim

Being first in the prospect's mind reliably produces enduring leadership, while line extension normally weakens the parent position and should generally be avoided.

## Assessment

**Rejected as a blanket inference; specific applications remain unvalidated.** The source relies on selected practitioner cases and categorical prescriptions without representative sampling, counterfactuals or contemporary base rates. Outcomes depend on product value, later entry, distribution, investment, category dynamics, portfolio fit, distinctive assets and execution.

## Safe use

Treat firstness and extension dilution as risk hypotheses. Measure actual retrieval and buyer behavior; assess category and portfolio fit, attribution, economics and delivery; compare focused-brand and extension options; and set local decision thresholds. Do not cite them as immutable laws or guaranteed share effects.
