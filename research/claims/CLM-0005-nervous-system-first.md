# Nervous-system-first sequencing

Regulation, stress load, and recovery plausibly affect execution capacity. The branded sequence has not been shown to outperform alternatives.

Allowed use: product philosophy, coaching hypothesis, and testable design lens.

Avoid: claims that all problems are nervous-system dysregulation or that regulation must always precede action.
