# Integration technology and supplement outcome claims

## Claims encountered

A file titled “Evidence-Based Best Practices in Psychedelic Preparation and Integration” gives highly specific effect sizes for guided integration, supplements, technology-assisted integration, sleep monitoring, qEEG scores, and related methods.

## Assessment

- The document appears to be a generated synthesis rather than a peer-reviewed guideline.
- Several citations are commercial/practitioner pages or do not transparently support the stated precision.
- Methods, populations, denominators, uncertainty, and reproducibility are not adequately established.
- It includes supplement dosing and interaction assertions inappropriate for autonomous coaching use.

## Safe use

Do not use its numerical claims, supplement protocols, biomarker scores, or technology efficacy statements in coaching, products, screening, or public content. Any underlying claim must be reconstructed from current primary research and clinically reviewed.
