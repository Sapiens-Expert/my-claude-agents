# LinkedIn engagement rate identifies sales success and transferable best practice

## Claim

Creators with high likes-and-comments relative to followers are the best models for LinkedIn sales success, and their posting, networking and engagement practices can be generalized.

## Assessment

**Unsupported as a sales-performance measure or causal selection method.** The anthology deliberately sampled visible high-engagement sales creators. Its metric does not adjust for reach, audience fit, pods, bots, content purpose, qualified conversations, pipeline, customer outcomes, cost or harm. The design excludes low-engagement high-performing sellers and cannot show that contributors' tactics caused their outcomes.

## Safe use

Treat contributor advice as a diverse hypothesis inventory. Measure qualified nonfollower reach, correct audience response, substantive conversation, qualification, assisted pipeline, economics, customer value, reputation and safety separately. Reject coordinated engagement and test contradictory tactics locally with stable evidence and recipient-protection controls.

Source analysis: [Finding Sales Success on LinkedIn](../deep-extraction/books/finding-sales-success-on-linkedin.md).
