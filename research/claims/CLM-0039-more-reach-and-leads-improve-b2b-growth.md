# More social reach and leads reliably improve B2B growth

## Claim

Expanding social reach, publishing frequently, placing conversion opportunities widely and optimizing lead flow reliably improve B2B customers, revenue and ROI.

## Assessment

**Unsupported as a general or causal claim.** The source offers a coherent practitioner system plus selected 2010–2011 cases and vendor studies. More reach can add irrelevant exposure; more forms can reduce trust; more leads can increase sales burden and lower fit. CRM or first/last-touch attribution does not establish incrementality, customer outcome, retention or net economics.

## Safe use

Optimize qualified category reach, useful assets, readiness-matched next steps, granular permission and customer value. Measure the full chain through retained outcomes, contribution margin, brand/reputation and harm. Label attribution confidence and use controlled incremental evidence where causal ROI matters.

Source analysis: [The B2B Social Media Book](../deep-extraction/books/the-b2b-social-media-book.md).
