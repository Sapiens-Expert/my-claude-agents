# Smallest-viable-market focus reliably drives long-term growth

## Claim

Focusing marketing permanently on the smallest viable audience, early adopters or a committed tribe reliably creates superior trust, spread and long-term growth.

## Assessment

**Rejected as a blanket inference; initial focus remains a useful design heuristic.** Selected cases and practitioner logic do not establish typical causal effects. Permanent narrowness can suppress qualified penetration, category-entry-point coverage and learning from nonbuyers. Early adopters and communities may differ from later buyers.

## Safe use

Use a bounded initial service population to validate problem, promise, delivery and economics. Predefine evidence for broadening, narrowing or stopping. After validation, build qualified reach and access across relevant entry points while preserving fit, safety, permission and capacity limits.
