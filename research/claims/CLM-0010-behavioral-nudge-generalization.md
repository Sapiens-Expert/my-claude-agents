# Behavioral nudges reliably generalize across contexts

## Claim

Defaults, framing, reminders, social norms, scarcity, habit loops, bias corrections, or other popular behavioral interventions reliably produce predictable and durable outcomes across people and settings.

## Assessment

**Rejected as a blanket inference; specific applications remain unvalidated.** Behavioral effects depend on intervention, outcome, population, implementation, context, comparison, duration, and publication/replication quality. A named bias or successful book example does not establish effect size or welfare benefit in VladOS use cases.

## Safe use

Treat each mechanism as a testable design hypothesis. Use current primary evidence, predefine welfare and guardrail outcomes, pilot in context, examine subgroup/unintended effects, and monitor persistence.
