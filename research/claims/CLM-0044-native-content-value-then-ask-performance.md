# Native value content followed by asks reliably drives social performance

## Claim

Frequent platform-native value content builds sufficient relationship and emotional response for later commercial asks to generate engagement, brand value and sales.

## Assessment

**Plausible mechanism, unsupported as a general causal formula.** The source supplies expert creative critique and selected examples, rarely with exposure denominators, comparison groups, full costs, downstream purchases or long-term effects. Engagement does not establish trust, brand equity or sales. Platform conditions and examples date mainly from 2010–2013, and no universal value-to-ask ratio is demonstrated.

## Safe use

Classify value, invitation, commercial ask and relationship-care content explicitly. Adapt execution while preserving claims, identity and disclosure. Determine sequencing through permission state, channel intent, offer maturity, fatigue and controlled learning; measure customer value, retention, complaints, harms and cost in addition to engagement.

Source analysis: [Jab, Jab, Jab, Right Hook](../deep-extraction/books/jab-jab-jab-right-hook.md).
