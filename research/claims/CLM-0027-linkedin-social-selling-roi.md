# LinkedIn social-selling playbooks reliably generate positive ROI

## Claim

Implementing a complete LinkedIn profile, fixed outreach/content routines and the LINK Method reliably converts contacts into clients and guarantees positive ROI.

## Assessment

**Unsupported as a guaranteed or causal claim.** The source uses practitioner experience, selected cases and dated vendor/industry statistics. Outcomes depend on offer, audience fit, reputation, market, reach, execution, capacity, costs and multi-touch journeys. Feature and quota advice from 2018 is volatile.

## Safe use

Treat LinkedIn as one testable channel. Use capacity-based relationship workflows; verify current platform mechanics; track cohorts, qualified outcomes, full time/cost, other touches, complaints and opportunity cost; and set stop/change thresholds. Never promise ROI.
