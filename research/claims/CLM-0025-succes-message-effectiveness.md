# SUCCESs principles reliably make messages effective

## Claim

Messages that are Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional and expressed through Stories reliably become memorable, persuasive and behavior-changing.

## Assessment

**Rejected as a sufficiency or guaranteed-effect claim.** The framework is a retrospective synthesis drawing on mixed research and selected cases, not a validated scoring equation. Attention, recall, belief, care, behavior and welfare are different outcomes. A message may be memorable because it is false, emotional or sensational.

## Safe use

Validate truth, scope, evidence, consent and safety first. Use the six principles selectively as communication diagnostics. Measure accurate comprehension, delayed recall, attribution, warranted belief, appropriate behavior, outcomes and harmful retelling separately.
