# STEPPS reliably causes virality and business outcomes

## Claim

Adding Social Currency, Triggers, Emotion, Public observability, Practical Value and Stories makes ideas contagious and reliably produces word of mouth, popularity and commercial results.

## Assessment

**Useful diagnostic, unsupported as a universal causal formula.** The source combines research with selected viral and commercial cases. The six lenses overlap, and platform distribution, network structure, paid support, source credibility, timing and selection of successes confound outcomes. Sharing does not establish accurate recall, trust, customer benefit or sales.

## Safe use

Use STEPPS selectively after truth and safety review. Measure qualified exposure, accurate recall, faithful transmission, correct attribution, appropriate action and benefit. Treat privacy exposure, misinformation, harmful imitation, polarization and correction failure as guardrails that can veto reach.
