# StoryBrand messaging reliably increases revenue across industries

## Claim

Using the seven-part customer-as-hero BrandScript reliably clarifies messages and causes substantial revenue growth regardless of industry.

## Assessment

**Rejected as a universal or causal growth claim.** The source primarily offers practitioner experience, selected client examples and testimonials without controls, complete denominators or unsuccessful implementations. Copy clarity is only one potential constraint among offer value, price, reach, access, proof, delivery and market conditions.

## Safe use

Use the adapted elements as optional message prompts after strategy and validation. Compare narrative and non-narrative versions; measure comprehension, qualified response, downstream outcomes and harms; and prohibit unsupported pain, authority, failure and transformation claims.
