# Value-innovation methods reliably create uncontested profitable growth

## Claim

Using a strategy canvas, noncustomer analysis, six paths and eliminate–reduce–raise–create reliably produces uncontested markets and profitable growth.

## Assessment

**Rejected as a blanket inference; specific applications remain unvalidated.** The framework is illustrated primarily through selected historical and company cases. The narrative does not establish a base rate, typical effect size or independent causal comparison. Demand, imitation, timing, capabilities and execution vary by context.

## Safe use

Use the methods for diagnosis-led hypothesis generation. Require buyer-utility evidence, access/price validation, unit economics, adoption feasibility, externality and ethical review, plus a bounded local test with falsification and stop rules. Do not promise an uncontested market or growth outcome.
