# LinkedIn engagement signals and SSI reveal sales readiness

## Claim

Profile views, follows, post engagement, job changes, activity alerts or a high Social Selling Index reliably identify buying windows and predict sales performance.

## Assessment

**Unsupported as intent or causal evidence.** These measures can help generate research hypotheses, but may reflect curiosity, recruitment, peer interest, platform activity or unrelated change. SSI summarizes platform behaviors; it is not qualified pipeline, incrementality or revenue. The source supplies practitioner examples and dated platform/vendor statistics rather than independent validation.

## Safe use

Record the observable fact separately from its interpretation. Exclude sensitive signals, validate relevance with a low-pressure question, coordinate contact across channels and respect suppression. Evaluate LinkedIn through qualified conversations, pipeline movement, economics, reputation and safety; never present SSI or engagement as proof of buyer intent or revenue impact.
