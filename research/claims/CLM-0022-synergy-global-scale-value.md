# Strategic synergy and global scale reliably create net value

## Claim

Related businesses, brand extensions, centralized coordination or global standardization reliably create synergy and superior value through shared assets and scale.

## Assessment

**Rejected as a blanket inference; specific synergies remain unvalidated.** Shared assets may help, but coordination, localization, cannibalization, dependency, governance and opportunity costs can exceed benefits. Case examples do not establish a portable causal effect.

## Safe use

Decompose each synergy into a mechanism, owner, required behavior, affected audiences, full cost and measurable net benefit. Compare independent, local, partner and no-action options. Pilot where possible and attach thresholds, reversal and exit rules.
