# Transparent buyer-question content reliably drives revenue

## Claim

Openly answering buyer questions—especially cost, problems, comparisons, reviews and best options—and assigning that content before sales conversations reliably increases traffic, trust, close rates, sales velocity and attributable revenue.

## Assessment

**Plausible mechanisms, unsupported as a general causal bundle.** Clear answers can reduce information friction, improve fit and make conversations more useful. The source relies heavily on selected practitioner cases, CRM/content-touch attribution and implementation stories. Buyers with high intent may both consume content and purchase; product, price, category, distribution, sales process and market timing are material confounders.

## Safe use

Use transparent question answering as decision support, not a guaranteed growth tactic. Measure comprehension, usefulness, sales/service adoption, time saved, fit, complaints and customer outcomes. Separate observed influence from controlled incrementality; never make assigned consumption a coercive qualification test.
