# Small retention increases reliably create large profit multipliers

## Claim

A small increase in customer retention, commonly framed as five percentage points, reliably produces a very large profit increase across businesses and therefore establishes loyalty as a primary independent brand-equity lever.

## Assessment

**Rejected as a portable quantitative claim.** The multiplier depends on acquisition cost, margins, cohort behavior, service cost, time horizon and assumptions about retained customers. Loyalty and defection also vary with brand size and category buying structure, so observed differences do not establish independent relationship quality or intervention effects.

## Safe use

Model retention using local cohort economics and diagnose preventable service failure, harm and avoidable churn. Benchmark repeat and defection against category and size where possible. Never cite a generic five-percent multiplier or discourage appropriate customer exit and referral.
