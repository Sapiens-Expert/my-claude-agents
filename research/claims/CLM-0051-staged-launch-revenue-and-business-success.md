# Staged launch sequences reliably create revenue and business success

## Claim

A sequenced prelaunch education campaign using anticipation, authority, reciprocity, social proof and a deadline reliably produces large online sales and can validate or create a successful business.

## Assessment

**Unsupported as a general causal claim.** The source relies on selected exceptional launch stories and revenue figures tied to a commercial training ecosystem. Gross launch revenue does not establish incrementality, profit, refund rate, retention, customer outcome or typicality. A launch may pull demand forward, cannibalize baseline sales or overwhelm delivery.

## Safe use

Use staged education and a time-bounded offer as a governed experiment. Disclose commercial intent, verify deadlines and capacity, protect consent, deliver before celebrating, and measure contribution margin, refunds, retention, customer outcomes, complaints and team cost. Continue business-model validation after the launch.
