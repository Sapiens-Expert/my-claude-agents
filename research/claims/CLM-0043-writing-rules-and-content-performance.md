# Writing rules and format practices reliably improve content performance

## Claim

Applying the source's writing, voice, story, format, email, website, social and LinkedIn practices reliably produces clearer, more engaging and commercially effective content.

## Assessment

**Strong practitioner value for process, unsupported as a universal performance claim.** The editorial workflow is coherent and useful, but examples, interviews, selected studies and campaign results do not establish portable causal effects. Genre, audience, stakes, distribution, offer quality and accessibility change which conventions work. Tool, platform, legal and benchmark guidance is time-sensitive.

## Safe use

Adopt the workflow as a quality-control and learning discipline. Test comprehension, usefulness, accuracy, recognition, accessibility, qualified action and harm in context. Treat every length, headline, format, platform and performance recommendation as a hypothesis; verify legal and platform details using current qualified or primary sources.

Source analysis: [Everybody Writes, Second Edition](../deep-extraction/books/everybody-writes-2e.md).
