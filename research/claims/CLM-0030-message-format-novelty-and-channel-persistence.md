# Novel message formats and cross-channel persistence reliably increase sales replies

## Claim

Audio/video novelty and persistent follow-up through LinkedIn, InMail, email and phone reliably improve reply and sales rates after an initial nonresponse.

## Assessment

**Unsupported as a general or causal claim.** The source relies on practitioner experience, selected cases, convenience polls and dated platform observations. Novelty decays; recipients differ in accessibility and channel preference; nonresponse may indicate timing, workload, aversion or no interest. A format or channel change does not reset consent.

## Safe use

Default to concise text unless another format is welcome. Provide accessible alternatives, coordinate every channel in one record, impose a finite ceiling and stop on refusal, wrong fit, preference, suppression or exhausted sequence. Test only with recipient-safe cohorts and measure meaningful reply, qualified progression, time cost, complaints and customer value—not novelty or delivery alone.
