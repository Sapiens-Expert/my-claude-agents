# Starting with WHY reliably produces inspiration, trust and durable performance

## Claim

Communicating from purpose through principles to products maps to human decision biology and reliably creates inspiration, loyalty, trust, adoption and durable organizational performance.

## Assessment

**Unsupported as a general causal theory.** The Golden Circle is a memorable alignment and communication heuristic, but the source relies on selected organizational histories, retrospective attribution and simplified neuroscience. Purpose competes with product quality, strategy, price, distribution, institutions, incentives, power, competence, repair and market conditions. The proposed neocortex/limbic mapping and universal adoption threshold are not adequate operational evidence.

## Safe use

Use purpose as a bounded stakeholder commitment translated into observable principles and practices. Measure contradictions, delivery, trust dimensions and stakeholder outcomes. Select communication order for the audience context; treat adoption stages and purpose effects as testable hypotheses.

Source analysis: [Start with Why, Fifteenth-Anniversary Edition](../deep-extraction/books/start-with-why-15e.md).
