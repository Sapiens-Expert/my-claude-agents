# Sleep loss and executive function

## Evidence position

Experimental and review literature broadly supports impairment across attention, working memory, inhibition, cognitive flexibility, and decision processes, with effects varying by task, dose, adaptation, and individual.

## Safe public formulation

“Sleep loss reliably impairs several dimensions of executive function.”

## Avoid

“One bad night destroys your decision-making.”

## Limitations

Do not infer an individual’s impairment from a wearable estimate or one night alone. Preserve dose, duration, task, and individual variability.
