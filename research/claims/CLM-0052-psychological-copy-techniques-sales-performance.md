# Psychological copy techniques reliably increase sales performance

## Claim

Applying classic consumer-psychology principles and direct-response copy techniques reliably increases attention, response and sales across audiences and media.

## Assessment

**Useful hypothesis inventory, unsupported as a universal causal system.** The source combines practitioner experience, classic advertising conventions, selected studies and historical response claims without systematic evidence appraisal. Many print rules, benchmarks, biological motives, color effects and digital practices are dated or overgeneralized. Marketplace response alone cannot establish truth, welfare or durable customer value.

## Safe use

Retain clear benefit, specificity, claim-proof fit, readable hierarchy, complete offers, friction diagnosis and valid testing. Require validity, decision quality, voluntariness and delivered performance. Prohibit fear inflation, status insecurity, authority laundering, false social proof/scarcity and disguised advertising.

Source analysis: [Cashvertising](../deep-extraction/books/cashvertising.md).
