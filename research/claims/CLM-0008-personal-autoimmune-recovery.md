# Personal recovery establishes treatment efficacy for ankylosing spondylitis

## Claim

Lifestyle changes or entheogen use treated, healed, cured, or reversed ankylosing spondylitis because Vlad reports improved health and being off medication.

## Assessment

**Rejected as a causal or general medical claim.** The source is a personal narrative without a comparator, independently verified disease activity, treatment attribution, or control for concurrent care and natural variation. It may support a carefully consented first-person account only.

## Safe wording

“Vlad describes a personal period of major health improvement while changing multiple aspects of his life. That experience does not establish what treated his condition and is not advice to change medical care.”

Medication decisions and disease monitoring belong with a rheumatology professional under current guidance.
