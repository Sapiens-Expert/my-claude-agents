# Value Proposition Canvas work validates customer and market fit

## Claim

Mapping customer jobs, pains and gains to products, pain relievers and gain creators—and testing the most important hypotheses—establishes problem-solution, product-market and business-model fit.

## Assessment

**The canvas organizes hypotheses; it does not validate fit.** Interviews and workshop rankings support language and prioritization hypotheses, while clicks, letters of intent, preorders and pilots support different bounded conclusions. No universal fit threshold exists. Segment construction, sampling, framing, incentives, delivery conditions, safety and externalities can all change the result.

## Safe use

Separate actors and retain provenance for every profile item. Define proposition-specific thresholds across problem-solution, product-market, business-model, responsible-delivery and stakeholder/ecosystem fit. Test critical dependencies cheaply while uncertainty is high, preserve negative findings and increase realism only as evidence strengthens.
