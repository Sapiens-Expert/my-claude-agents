# AI metabolic advisory system improves diabetes management

## Claim

The mobile/cloud system, cumulative health index, glucose forecasting, microbiome analysis, or vegan-diet component enables reliable personalized management or slows type 2 diabetes progression.

## Assessment

**Unvalidated from the archived report alone.** The report describes a preliminary system and appears to make broad conclusions from very limited participant evidence. A reported prediction “precision” is insufficient without metric definition, held-out evaluation, baseline comparison, calibration, clinical outcomes, safety analysis, and external validation.

## Safe use

Describe it as a research prototype and hypothesis-generating feasibility project. Do not call it a clinically validated advisory, diagnostic, or treatment system without independent evidence and applicable medical-device/privacy review.
