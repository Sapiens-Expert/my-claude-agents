# Branded differentiators and energizers reliably create durable growth

## Claim

Naming features, services, programs, partnerships or promotional properties as branded differentiators or energizers reliably produces durable differentiation, relevance or growth.

## Assessment

**Rejected as a blanket inference; specific applications remain unvalidated.** The framework is supported mainly by selected cases and strategic logic. Attention, novelty and internal enthusiasm do not establish incremental demand, correct attribution, durable equity or favorable economics. Extra names can fragment memory and investment.

## Safe use

Name only after the underlying value is verified. Compare a branded asset with a plain descriptor and no-addition option. Require audience relevance, fame/uniqueness or attribution evidence, bidirectional transfer analysis, maintenance economics, an owner and a sunset trigger.
