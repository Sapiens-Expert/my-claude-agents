# Traditional or natural herbal products are inherently safe and effective

## Claim

An herb, adaptogen, home remedy, or natural product is safe and effective because it is natural, traditional, holistic, widely used, available without prescription, or described in a materia medica.

## Assessment

**Rejected.** Traditional use establishes provenance, not efficacy or safety. Botanical products vary by species, part, preparation, dose, formulation, quality, and user context. They may cause adverse effects, interactions, allergy, toxicity, contamination, or treatment delay.

## Safe use

Evaluate the exact product, population, outcome, evidence, dose, quality, contraindications, interactions, and jurisdiction using current authoritative sources. Keep historical/traditional description distinct from clinical recommendation and do not replace prescribed or urgent care.
