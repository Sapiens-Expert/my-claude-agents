# Larger LinkedIn networks reliably improve career, sales and recruiting outcomes

## Claim

Uploading contacts, accepting most invitations and connecting broadly with recruiters, open networkers, industry people or competitor contacts reliably improves visibility and opportunity access.

## Assessment

**Unsupported as a general or causal claim.** The practitioner manual offers no controlled evidence that network size improves fit, interviews, clients or hires. Broad importing and acceptance can transfer third-party data, dilute relevance, increase fraud/spam exposure and create false permission assumptions. Suggested contacts and connection status do not establish relationship or consent.

## Safe use

Require a legitimate relationship, introduction, shared context, community exchange, role/account relevance or explicit learning purpose. Review identity and risk before accepting; do not upload address books by default. Measure qualified access, reciprocal relationships and downstream outcomes while maintaining permission, security and suppression controls.
