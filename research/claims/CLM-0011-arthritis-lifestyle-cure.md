# A lifestyle protocol reliably heals or reverses arthritis

## Claim

A particular diet, supplement stack, gut protocol, posture/alignment method, mobility system, mind-body approach, or exercise program reliably heals, cures, reverses, or eliminates arthritis or chronic pain and can replace medical care.

## Assessment

**Rejected as a blanket claim.** Arthritis and persistent pain include heterogeneous conditions and mechanisms. Popular books, recipes, mechanistic arguments, practitioner cases, and personal outcomes cannot establish condition-specific disease modification, comparative benefit, safety, or medication replacement.

## Safe use

Discuss evidence-supported lifestyle and self-management options only at the level supported for a defined condition and outcome, coordinated with appropriate care. Describe lived experience as experience, distinguish symptom/function change from disease modification, and never advise stopping prescribed treatment.
