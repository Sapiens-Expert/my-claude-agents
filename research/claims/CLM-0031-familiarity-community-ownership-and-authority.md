# Repeated exposure and community ownership create trust and authority

## Claim

Systematic LinkedIn exposure, nurturing and ownership of a prospect-centered group reliably create familiarity, trust, authority and predictable sales progression.

## Assessment

**Unsupported as a general or causal claim.** The source combines simplified psychology, agency cases and promotional benchmarks. Exposure may create recognition without trust; administrative ownership is not domain competence; membership and event attendance do not establish relationship, permission or purchase readiness. Concealed delegation or manufactured participation may actively damage trust.

## Safe use

Measure recognition, reciprocity, participant value, qualified progression and customer outcomes separately. Disclose owners, sponsors, actual operators, commercial purpose and data use. Require observable evidence for each relationship-stage transition, and never simulate authorship, intimacy, questions, attendance, popularity or live status.
