# Category creation and barriers reliably produce superior performance

## Claim

Creating a new category or subcategory with must-have criteria and barriers reliably makes competitors irrelevant and produces durable superior financial performance.

## Assessment

**Rejected as a blanket inference; specific category theses remain unvalidated.** Selected retrospective winners do not establish creation base rates, causal contribution or profitability. Many categories remain small, converge, fail or shift through actions of buyers, competitors, channels and institutions. Barriers can also create lock-in and public harm.

## Safe use

Treat category creation as one strategic option. Require behavioral demand, buyer utility, delivery capability, economics, brand linkage and competitor-response evidence. Review competition, interoperability, portability, privacy and welfare. Use staged tests and explicit change/kill criteria; never promise competitor irrelevance.
