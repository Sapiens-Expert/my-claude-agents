# Penetration and broad reach are universally dominant growth strategies

## Claim

Because brands usually grow through penetration, every brand should prioritize broad reach and acquisition over retention, differentiation, targeting or community.

## Assessment

**Rejected as a universal managerial inference.** Penetration-led growth and related buyer-behavior regularities are well supported in many established repertoire markets, but they are descriptive baselines. Transfer depends on category definition, buying cycle, contracts, margins, acquisition cost, capacity, eligibility, risk and market structure.

## Safe use

Measure qualified penetration, category-entry-point retrieval, action availability and buyer distributions. Diagnose the binding constraint and compare incremental economics. In health-adjacent, regulated, B2B, subscription or capacity-limited contexts, define who can appropriately benefit and preserve service, safety, privacy and retention guardrails.
