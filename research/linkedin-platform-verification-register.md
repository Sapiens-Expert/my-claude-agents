# LinkedIn platform verification register

The five-book comparison is complete. The following items are implementation candidates, not current facts, until checked against the live interface and official LinkedIn documentation. Durable relationship, evidence, permission and measurement principles do not depend on these features.

| Area | Items requiring verification | Status | Required evidence before use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Profile | Fields, limits, images, Featured, services, visibility and viewer controls | unverified | Live interface plus current official documentation |
| Connections/follow | Invitation behavior, notes, withdrawal, limits and follow/connect defaults | unverified | Current account behavior and policy |
| Messaging | Degree-based access, InMail, subject lines, credits, audio/video and limits | unverified | Current plan/interface and official help |
| Search | Syntax, filters, result limits, commercial-use limits and saved searches | unverified | Current interface, plan and policy |
| Content | Post/article/newsletter/live/document/poll/video formats, limits and analytics | unverified | Current creation interface and official help |
| Groups/events | Ownership, moderator roles, member messaging, announcements, registration and replay | unverified | Current group/event interface and terms |
| Sales Navigator | Tiers, filters, alerts, lists, CRM integrations and data availability | unverified | Current product documentation and contracted plan |
| Automation/data | Export, scraping, third-party permissions, retention and account restrictions | unverified | Current user agreement, privacy terms and security review |
| Metrics | SSI definition/access, profile views, follower/reach analytics and attribution | unverified | Current definitions; business validation remains separate |
| Vendors | Every named 2015–2023 tool, integration, price and capability | unverified | Independent security, privacy, accessibility, cost and fit review |

## Verification record

For each operational decision record the exact claim, official URL/interface evidence, account/plan, jurisdiction, check date, owner, observed behavior, limitation, decision and recheck trigger. Never infer effectiveness from feature availability.

Sources defining the candidate set include [LinkedIn Unlocked](deep-extraction/books/linkedin-unlocked.md), [The Ultimate LinkedIn Sales Guide](deep-extraction/books/the-ultimate-linkedin-sales-guide.md), [LinkedIn for Personal Branding](deep-extraction/books/linkedin-for-personal-branding.md), [The Ultimate LinkedIn Messaging Guide](deep-extraction/books/the-ultimate-linkedin-messaging-guide.md), [Connect](deep-extraction/books/connect-josh-turner.md) and the extended LinkedIn corpus. Low-authority compilations may expand the candidate list but cannot verify a feature or performance claim.
