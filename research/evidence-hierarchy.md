# Evidence hierarchy

1. Primary operating data and direct observation.
2. Systematic reviews, meta-analyses, and strong guidelines.
3. Relevant controlled and longitudinal research.
4. High-quality observational/mechanistic studies.
5. Official statistics and regulatory sources.
6. Credible expert synthesis.
7. Preprints, market estimates, vendor claims, and media.
8. Anecdote, personal experience, and symbolic interpretation.

Fit to the actual question matters as much as nominal rank.
