- `knowledge/SKILL-MAP.md`: writing, storytelling, content strategy, UX writing, and experimentation capabilities.

## Conflicts resolved

- Clarity, accessibility, truth, consent, and safe scope precede persuasion and conversion.
- Fear, scarcity, authority, belonging, status, curiosity, and social proof must be real, material, and non-coercive.
- Story and neuroscience language cannot turn inference or exceptional outcomes into general causal claims.
- A conversion uplift is rejected when it increases misunderstanding, regret, unsafe behavior, poor-fit clients, or long-term trust damage.
