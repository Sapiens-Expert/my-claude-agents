# AI coach source map

## Processing model

Twenty PDFs were mapped. Eleven are exact copies already incorporated in previous source families. The German edition of *Outlive* is a distinct file/translation of a book already mapped in AI Healer and was not treated as independent conceptual evidence. Eight sources add new coaching, focus, mindset, business-scaling, or expert-marketing material.

## Coaching relationship and change conversation

| Source | Contribution and authority limit |
| --- | --- |
| `Co-Active_Coaching_-_Kimsey_House.pdf` | Designed alliance, listening, curiosity, self-management, fulfillment, balance, process, and integration; branded practitioner model, not independent outcome proof |
| `Motivational_interviewing_helping_people_change_fourth_edition_-_William_R_Miller_and_Stephen_Rollnick.pdf` | 2023 MI foundation: spirit, engaging, focusing, evoking, planning, skills, change/sustain talk, discord, information/advice, and practice development; evidence-informed method whose competent use requires training/practice |
| `The_Solutions_Focus_-_Mark_McKergow.pdf` | Desired future, resources, exceptions, scales, signs, small steps, and solution-focused organizational/coaching conversations; practitioner method with bypass risk if applied mechanically |
| `Positive_Provocation_25_Questions_to_Elevate_Your_Coaching_Practice_-_Robert_Biswas-Diener.pdf` | Challenge and question-design prompts for experienced coaches; provocative questions require permission, timing, relationship, and impact review |

## Open gates

- Verify Vlad's current coaching credentials, supervision, MI training/fidelity, insurance, and jurisdiction-specific scope before stronger public claims.
- Measure client outcomes, alliance/fit, adverse effects, referral compliance, and sustainability rather than inferring efficacy from method adoption.
- Keep trauma, arthritis, longevity, nutrition, and mental-health material educational and referral-bound.
- Keep expert funnels, belief engineering, trial closes, fabricated urgency/scarcity, and movement-leader tactics outside coaching conversations.
- Revisit the source books chapter by chapter only during the queued deep-extraction pass.
