# Psychedelic Coach source map

## Processing model

Twenty-nine PDFs were classified by authority and mapped through contents, relevant chapters, and targeted safety/ethics extraction. General neuroscience encyclopedias and very large pharmacology/toxicology references were not treated as autonomous clinical decision tools. Two exact copies also occur in AI Healer and should not be reprocessed there.

## Clinical, pharmacology, and safety references

| Source | Contribution and authority limit |
| --- | --- |
| `Treatment recommendations  PAT 17 1 24.pdf` | Swiss professional recommendations for regulated psychedelic-assisted therapy: indication, contraindication, preparation, sessions, integration, emergencies, ethics, supervision; jurisdiction/date-specific and clinician-facing |

## Unresolved/current-verification gates

- Jurisdiction-specific legality, licensure, mandatory reporting, emergency pathways, and data rules.
- Current interaction and contraindication evidence for any specific person.
- Exact credential scope, supervision, insurance, and referral network.
- Validated screening/risk workflow and service agreement.
- Evidence for integration methods and long-term outcomes outside trials.
