## Incorporation model

Sources were not copied into one undifferentiated summary. They were mapped into identity, brands, voice, knowledge, frameworks, projects, platforms, research, content, SOPs, decisions, templates, and state according to role. Exact duplicates were processed once while retaining every original path.

Books and large manuals were incorporated as bounded reference libraries. Medical, psychedelic, tax, legal, privacy, and safety-sensitive material received stronger authority, freshness, scope, and referral gates. Personal health, genetic, client, financial, and tax data remain private by default.

## Remaining limitations

Completion means semantic coverage, not independent validation of every statement inside every source. Live use still requires:

- current verification for medical, scientific, platform, legal, and tax claims;
- primary operating data for revenue, conversion, retention, outcomes, and execution status;
- credential, consent, jurisdiction, and professional-scope confirmation where applicable;
- targeted deep reading of a large reference only when a consequential question requires it;
- conflict review when new evidence disagrees with a canonical synthesis.

## Maintenance trigger

Repeat the coverage checks when a substantive source is added, removed, renamed, or replaced. Update the family map, manifest, source register, source-map index, and any affected canonical conclusion in the same change.
