## Evidence limitations

- Practitioner success stories and vendor research are not universal causal proof.
- “Neuroscience of selling” claims require verification against primary research before scientific publication.
- Influence and language patterns may explain behavior but do not authorize manipulation.
- Frameworks conflict on seller control versus collaboration; buyer autonomy, complexity, and evidence determine the approach.
