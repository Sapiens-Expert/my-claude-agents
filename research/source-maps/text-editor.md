# Text editor source map

## Processing model

Twenty-one PDFs were mapped. Three exact copies already incorporated elsewhere were not reprocessed. The remaining books were used to build an editorial stack spanning reader/task design, developmental editing, line editing, copyediting, proofreading, data communication, narrative structure, and useful nonfiction.

Style and story books are references, not mutually consistent laws. Edition, completeness, regional English, genre, accessibility, platform, house style, and authorial voice determine application.

## Open gates

- Establish the authoritative house style by language, brand, and channel; do not mix reference-book preferences arbitrarily.
- Verify edition/completeness of shortened or repackaged files during deep extraction.
- Test comprehension, task success, qualified action, and maintenance cost rather than judging writing only by elegance.
- Preserve evidence, uncertainty, privacy, accessibility, and author voice when applying clarity or story advice.
- Do not begin the deferred book until its explicit activation gate is met.
