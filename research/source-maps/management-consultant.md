# Management Consultant source map

## Processing note

Twenty PDFs were mapped through contents, framework sections, indexes, and targeted full-text search. *Good Strategy/Bad Strategy* is an exact duplicate of the FANATIC copy and was not processed twice. These books support a knowledge library; they do not by themselves establish professional experience.

## Authority and limitations

- Research-based books, practitioner books, and company stories have different evidentiary weight.
- Case studies do not establish universal causality or guaranteed results.
- Several frameworks overlap or conflict; client evidence and context decide their use.
- Legal, employment, financial, and behavioral-intervention decisions require jurisdiction-appropriate expertise.
