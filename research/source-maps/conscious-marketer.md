# Conscious marketer source map

## Processing model

Twenty-one PDFs were mapped. Ten are exact copies already represented in earlier source families and were not reprocessed. The eleven remaining books contribute practitioner models for customer value, stakeholder purpose, category and brand framing, content-led growth, transparent customer education, platform-native communication, and reducing resistance. Inclusion records relevance, not empirical endorsement.

## Ethics, trust, purpose, and customer orientation

| Source | Contribution and authority limit |
| --- | --- |
| `This_Is_Marketing_-_Seth_Godin.pdf` | Smallest viable audience, empathy, permission, trust, status, and change; practitioner philosophy, not causal marketing evidence |
| `Uploaded/Conscious_Capitalism_With_a_New_Preface_b_-_John_Mackey.pdf` | Purpose, stakeholder orientation, leadership, and culture; normative/company-case source that does not prove universal performance superiority |
| `Uploaded/Start_with_Why_15th_Anniversary_Edition_-_Simon_Sinek.pdf` | Purpose-led narrative and organizational alignment; popular practitioner framework, not proof that a stated why causes loyalty or performance |
| `Uploaded/They_Ask_You_Answer_-_Marcus_Sheridan.pdf` | Transparent customer-question content, including price, comparison, problems, and reviews; inbound playbook whose outcomes depend on context, accuracy, and execution |

## Open gates

- Deep extraction must distinguish cited primary research from author anecdotes, company cases, metaphors, and prescriptions.
- Purpose, category, archetypes, virality, and platform tactics remain hypotheses/lenses until independently supported in the actual context.
- Verify platform mechanics and examples at time of use.
- Measure fit, retained value, welfare, complaints, regret, and trust alongside reach, leads, and conversion.
- All commercial use remains subordinate to evidence, brand firewalls, privacy, and health/psychedelic/tax scope controls.
