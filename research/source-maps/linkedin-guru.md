## Strategic conflicts resolved

- Active O!Sapiens strategy prioritizes qualified executive relationships, not generic audience growth, job search, company-page growth, or network marketing.
- Current cadence (~five targeted comments per weekday) overrides high-volume engagement advice.
- No algorithm, format, filter, limit, or “best time” claim is canonical without current verification.
- Demand creation does not permit artificial scarcity, engagement manipulation, spam, or misrepresented authority.
