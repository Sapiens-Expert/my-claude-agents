# Product Manager source map

## Processing model

Twenty-two PDFs were mapped. Seven exact copies already incorporated elsewhere were not reprocessed. Large commercial books were treated as model and tactic libraries, not evidence that their revenue claims, case studies, funnels, scarcity methods, or prescriptions generalize.

## Vlad and customer context

| Source | Contribution and authority limit |
| --- | --- |
| `Vladislav Andreev Linkedin.pdf` | Ten-page LinkedIn snapshot covering positioning, roles, experience, education, skills, and claims; point-in-time/self-authored and conflicts with newer canonical history in places |
| `Ideal Customer Profile.pdf` | Exact copy of the mapped O!Sapiens ICP hypothesis; detailed but largely AI-assisted/unvalidated buyer assumptions |
| `Building_Your_Ideal_Private_Practice_-_Lynn_Grodzki.pdf` | Service-practice design, niche, referral, operations, boundaries, and sustainability; practitioner-business reference, not a credential or guaranteed practice model |

The LinkedIn snapshot contains promotional claims such as “science-backed,” “trusted by,” numeric experience/delivery claims, current role dates, and service descriptions that require primary proof and current-status review before reuse. It does not override `PROFILE.md`, current decisions, or the Digital Vlad source map.

- Establish repeatable Diagnostic → Performance Reset acquisition, delivery, outcomes, and economics before adding product lines.
- Validate HealthSpan through behavior-bearing commitments and retained value before build/fundraising.
- Treat subscription, membership, community, webinar, launch, and one-to-many models as options requiring explicit replacement decisions.
- Do not use fabricated scarcity, health fear, unqualified guarantees, or customer identity/status pressure.
