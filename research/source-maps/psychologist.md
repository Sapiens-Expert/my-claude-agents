# Psychologist source map

## Processing model

Twenty-four PDFs were mapped. Three are exact copies already represented elsewhere and were not reprocessed. The remaining works span clinical manuals, therapy models, self-help, foundational/historical psychology, social psychology, trauma, attachment, consciousness, evolution, and popular synthesis. Clinical use requires current authoritative guidance and qualified practice; historical importance is not current validity.

## Open gates

- Check diagnostic-manual edition and all live criteria against current official guidance; never automate diagnosis.
- Deep extraction must identify book claims tied to primary studies, then check systematic reviews, replication, correction, and population/context limits.
- IFS, attachment categories, transactional scripts, trauma metaphors, evolutionary accounts, and consciousness theories remain models—not discovered facts about an individual.
- Do not infer historical abuse or validate a memory from somatic response, imagery, conviction, dream, parts work, or psychedelic material.
- Any applied trauma, diagnosis, severe-symptom, or crisis work requires appropriate qualification, jurisdiction, supervision, referral, and emergency infrastructure.
