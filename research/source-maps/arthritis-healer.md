# Arthritis Healer source map

## Processing model

Thirty-five PDFs were mapped. Five are exact copies already represented in AI Healer or AI coach and were not reprocessed. The remaining books provide clinical education, exercise/movement systems, persistent-pain approaches, diets/recipes, supplements, herbalism, and alternative protocols. Titles using “heal,” “defeat,” “fix,” “cure,” or “pain free” are not accepted as outcome evidence.

## Open gates

- Deep extraction must classify every intervention by arthritis/pain condition, population, comparator, clinical outcome, harms, and evidence date.
- Verify all live material against current rheumatology, pain, rehabilitation, nutrition, and medication/supplement guidance.
- Distinguish symptom relief, function, quality of life, disease activity, imaging, biomarkers, and structural progression.
- Treat posture, fascia, alignment, gut, inflammation-type, blood-type, detox/reset, megavitamin, and cure models as unvalidated unless independently established.
- Never use this archive to replace diagnosis, disease-modifying therapy, physiotherapy, pain medicine, dietetics, or urgent care.
