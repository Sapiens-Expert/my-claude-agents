## Key reusable concepts

- Transcript evidence needs a completeness and fidelity label before extraction.
- Automated summaries, cleaned versions, and repeated references are not independent sources.
- Client language can improve delivery when retained in the client's restricted record, but cannot become marketing proof without separate consent.
- Meeting-derived decisions must be dated; operational and financial statements decay quickly.
- Discovery calls generate hypotheses and commitments, not validated partnerships, demand, legality, or outcomes.
