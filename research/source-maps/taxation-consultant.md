# Taxation Consultant source map

## Processing model

All 32 PDFs were inventoried and classified by issuer, jurisdiction, subject, date, and authority. No exact duplicates were found. Large manuals and books were mapped from metadata and contents rather than exhaustively summarized. Rules, rates, thresholds, procedures, and eligibility are not operational until checked against current primary sources for the relevant period.
