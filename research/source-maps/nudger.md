# Nudger source map

## Processing model

Twenty-one PDFs were mapped. Three exact copies already existed in mapped families and were not reprocessed. The remaining works were treated as concept libraries whose famous effects, anecdotes, and prescriptions require construct-specific primary and replication review during the deep pass.

## Open gates

- During deep extraction, identify each book's underlying primary studies and check current replication/correction status before claim reuse.
- Validate mechanisms and outcomes in the actual population/context; do not cite a bias label as causal diagnosis.
- Measure autonomy, comprehension, welfare, regret, dependency, subgroup burden, and persistence alongside behavior/conversion.
- Prefer structural/service improvement where an individual nudge would shift burden or conceal missing resources.
- Never use scarcity, sludge, defaults, variable rewards, social pressure, or identity to impair informed choice.
