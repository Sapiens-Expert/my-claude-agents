# O!Sapiens source map

## Authority

Internal brand, coaching, product, science, buyer, roadmap, website, community, and investor materials. They cover two linked but distinct businesses: founder-led coaching and a proposed HealthSpan product.

## Files and mapping

- `OSapiens_Strategic_Operating_System_v1.0.docx` → coaching business architecture, offer ladder, diagnostic funnel, operating rules.
- `Sapiens OS Ultimate strategy doc-2.docx` → later portfolio priorities, Executive Biology, content precedence, current strategic synthesis.
- `SapiensOS_Brand_Strategy.pdf` → canonical brand foundation, positioning, messaging, vocabulary, and separation.
- `Sapiens OS Executive Search and Buyer Intelligence.docx` → executive ICP, triggers, language, and buyer research.
- `Sapiens_OS_Scientific_Review.docx` → healthspan/behavior scientific claims and limitations.
- `O!Sapiens Investor memo.docx` → product/investor thesis and assumptions.
- `SapiensOS HealthSpan - Pre-Seed Investor Deck.pdf` → fundraising narrative; not execution evidence.
- `Old Investor deck.pdf` → historical narrative and superseded framing.
- `O_Sapiens_18_month_roadmap.docx` → product/company sequencing assumptions.
- `OSapiens_Social_Brief.docx` → social content direction and brand expression.
- `OSapiens_Website_Copy_WorkWithMe.md - Google Docs.pdf` → website offer language and customer journey.
- `SKOOL. Turning a Low-Traction Healthspan Skool Community into an Active Membership Business.docx` → community-business hypothesis; not currently prioritized.
- `Mental health quizes.docx` → assessment/quiz ideas requiring clinical-scope and evidence review.

## Expanded internal material

- `OSapiens_Strategic_Operating_System_v1.0.pdf` → PDF rendition of the already mapped strategic operating system; mapped but not semantically reprocessed.
- `Brand strategy.pdf` → concise 2026 strategic pyramid: category insight, brand idea, navigator role, Expert Peer voice, desired emotional response, functional benefits, proof, and banned terms.
- `Ideal Customer Profile.pdf` → detailed AI-assisted executive buyer hypothesis, triggers, language, content preferences, and offer mappings; most specifics remain unvalidated.
- `10 pillars of mental health post.pdf` → draft content series for the ten domains; useful topic/voice archive containing claims and “no pills” language requiring review.
- `Sapiens OS: Full Curriculum Overview.pdf` → 101/Core/Deep teaching structure across all ten domains.
- `O!Sapiens product overview.pdf` → large curriculum/script/offer compilation plus historical membership advice and speculative pricing; curriculum source, not current product catalog.
- `SapiensOS – Научно обоснованные методики для ментального здоровья.pdf` → historical Russian low-cost course landing page and testimonials; rapid-reset/universal language is not current or safe positioning.
- `Conversation with Gal.pdf` → historical growth consultation advocating membership, webinars, ads, group funnels, and proof claims; superseded where it conflicts with the single-funnel decision.
- `влад ца.docx` and `влад ца.pdf` → two format versions of audience/client notes containing highly sensitive personal and mental-health information; private and quarantined from case/content use.
- `Распаковка - ЦА.pdf` → table-form rendering of three historical client/audience notes; same privacy restrictions.
- `Summary of Vladislav’s knowledge.pdf` → AI-generated composite expertise/personality briefing overlapping Digital Vlad; internal hypothesis, not credential or independent authority.

## Conflicts and gates

App build/fundraising plans are subordinate to the active validation decision. Quiz and mental-health artifacts cannot imply diagnosis or replace validated assessment.

Course, membership, webinar, community, group, and multiple-entry funnel ideas are historical/deferred. The active Diagnostic → Performance Reset funnel governs. Sensitive client notes are not approved proof and must not enter public or third-party AI workflows.
