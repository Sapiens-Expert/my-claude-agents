# Digital Vlad source map

## Role of this family

Digital Vlad is an eight-PDF briefing layer that combines a June 2026 LinkedIn export, a 2026 brand strategy, podcast/transcript synthesis, and AI-generated personality/expertise profiles. It is useful for identity and navigation, but its authority varies by document and claim.

## Incorporated knowledge and skills

- Professional chronology from commercial sales and P&L through strategy, negotiation consulting, entrepreneurship, and coaching.
- Mechanism-first executive-performance positioning and the Expert Peer relationship model.
- Russian-language psychedelic science translation, interviewing, harm reduction, and integration education.
- Cross-domain synthesis across neuroscience, behavior, trauma-aware communication, public health, meaning, and media.
- Brand architecture: Vlad is the trust vehicle; O!Sapiens carries framework/product IP.

## Conflicts and exclusions

- “Alternative to psychotherapy” is rejected; coaching is complementary and non-clinical.
- Comparisons with clinicians, psychologists, neuroscientists, or global experts are not evidence of standing.
- Anti-medication implications conflict with the stronger anti-stigma/nuance principle and are excluded.
- Short-timeline coaching outcomes are unvalidated and registered as CLM-0006.
- Psychedelic content remains separated from the executive LinkedIn/O!Sapiens brand.
- The strategy’s “comments daily, 80% of effort” is historical advice; the active LinkedIn operating decision controls current cadence.
