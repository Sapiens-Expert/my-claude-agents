# AI Healer source map

## Processing model

Thirty-four files were inventoried and authority-triaged. Two exact neuroscience duplicates already mapped in Psychedelic Coach were not reprocessed. The collection is a reading/research archive, not a clinical knowledge base: current clinical guidance outranks every book here, and personal records remain private.

## Unresolved/current-verification gates

- Current ankylosing-spondylitis status, clinician plan, monitoring, and permission to use the story publicly.
- Clinical confirmation and current interpretation of any genetic/pharmacogenomic result.
- Edition/date and primary evidence behind any specific diet, supplement, breathing, sleep, movement, autoimmune, or longevity claim.
- Study design, sample size, validation protocol, privacy architecture, and regulatory status of the metabolic advisory prototype.
- Current guideline and jurisdiction review before any disease, medication, or safety statement.
