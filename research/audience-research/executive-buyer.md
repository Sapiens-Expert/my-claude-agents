# Executive buyer

Founder/CEO/senior operator with a costly but often hidden constraint in sleep, recovery, energy, decision quality, or sustainable output. Values discretion, precision, relevance, agency, and time efficiency. Trust comes from diagnostic specificity and a practical next step, not generic wellness motivation.

## Working segment hypothesis

- Founders, executives, and senior operators approximately 32–50, not as a rigid demographic gate.
- Still functioning and responsible for consequential work, but noticing that output costs more in sleep, recovery, presence, emotional steadiness, or decision clarity.
- Science-literate and skeptical of vague wellness, motivation, maximalist biohacking, or one-size protocols.
- Has consumed information and may use wearables, but struggles with prioritization, contextual application, and sustained execution.
- Values confidentiality, time efficiency, mechanism-level explanation, personalization, and observable progress.

## Trigger hypotheses

Board or investor pressure, a visible decision error, worsening sleep/recovery, conflict or reduced presence at home, a health warning, declining creative/strategic capacity, major transition, or failed self-directed attempts. These are research prompts—not facts to infer from public behavior.

## Evidence status

The expanded ICP documents combine AI synthesis, market assumptions, and a small set of historical client notes. Income ranges, media/brand preferences, exact age, prevalence statistics, quotes, willingness to pay, and “dream solution” language are unvalidated until traced to primary research.
