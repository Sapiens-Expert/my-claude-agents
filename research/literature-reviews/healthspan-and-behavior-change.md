# Healthspan and behavior change review

Durable change requires more than information. Motivation, planning, self-efficacy, environmental design, feedback, recovery, repetition, identity, and accountability interact. Stress and cognitive load widen the intention-action gap; small contextual actions and lapse-recovery plans often matter more than richer instruction.

For products and coaching, prioritize the limiting constraint and measure behavior and meaningful outcomes. Population- and outcome-specific claims still require direct validation.
