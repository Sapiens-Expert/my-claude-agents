# Psychedelics and integration review

Psychedelic research is promising in several clinical areas, but findings depend on screening, preparation, setting, therapeutic support, integration, population, comparator, and study design. Controlled findings must not be generalized to unsupervised use.

Integration supports stabilization, meaning-making, relationships, values, and daily behavior. It is not proof that an experience was therapeutic or that its interpretations are literally true.
