## Recurring pattern across sessions, worth tracking

**The "engineer my lifestyle around the goal" identity.** First articulated by Tamaz himself in the 2026-07-01 session, in response to Vlad's attempted reframe ("someone who prioritizes self-care"), which Tamaz rejected in favor of his own version. This is the identity that reappears, essentially unchanged, as the "WiFi" framing by 2026-08-20 — his own durable formulation, not something introduced to him.

**The "WiFi" metaphor.** Tamaz's own term for his capacity to energize and lead his team when well-resourced — "раздавать вай-фай" (to broadcast/give out wifi). By 2026-08-20, sleep and recovery are framed not as self-care but as the precondition for being able to "give wifi" to the people around him — effective because it routes around his resistance to treating rest as legitimate on its own terms.

**The "two wolves" internal-parts frame.** Presented in the 2026-08-20 session as a nightly conflict between the part that wants to keep working/socializing and the part that knows he needs sleep. Not new that day — a direct evolution of a "working with boredom" technique Tamaz described already using for unrelated tasks in the 2026-07-01 session (vent the frustration, then act anyway), which he proposed extending to sleep resistance himself.

**Self-critical productivity metric — a through-line.** First appears 2026-07-01 as a self-estimated 20–30% "win rate" against his own expectations (via a "fixing a loss" trading metaphor). By 2026-08-20 this had become "3 out of 10," then revised downward again in that same session. The metric, and his discomfort with it, appears stable across at least seven weeks.

**The Whoop-vibration anchor.** Proposed 2026-07-01 (tie a physical, symbolic reminder to a device he already wears constantly). By 2026-08-20 it's referenced as an already-adopted, low-friction habit he actively likes — one of the few interventions that stuck cleanly between sessions.
