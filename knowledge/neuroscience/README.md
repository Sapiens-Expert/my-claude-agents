# Neuroscience

Applied concepts relevant to cognition, learning, stress, behavior, and integration. Avoid reductionist “brain explains everything” claims. Primary source map: `sources/Research/`, `sources/Psychedelics/`, and scientific reviews.

Standard textbooks establish durable background but do not independently support a clinical, sex-essentialist, behavioral, or intervention claim. Popular neuroscience language—“rewiring,” fixed male/female brains, nervous-system regulation, trauma stored in the body, or neurotransmitter explanations—must be translated into observable constructs and checked against current primary evidence.
