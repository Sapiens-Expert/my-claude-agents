# Content strategy

Content strategy aligns audience needs, organizational goals, evidence, voice, formats, distribution, governance, maintenance, and measurement.

## Strategy model

1. Define the audience, context, jobs/questions, risks, and desired behavior or decision.
2. Specify the legitimate business and user outcome for the content.
3. Audit existing assets for usefulness, accuracy, duplication, performance, ownership, and freshness.
4. Select pillars and journey roles: discovery, recognition, education, evaluation, action, delivery, retention.
5. Establish claim/evidence rules, voice, terminology, accessibility, privacy, and brand boundaries.
6. Design formats and distribution around audience behavior rather than channel fashion.
7. Assign workflow, owner, review gates, publication state, maintenance date, and retirement rule.
8. Measure comprehension, useful action, qualified conversion, downstream outcome, and production cost.
9. Learn from research and experiments; preserve what works, revise what does not, and remove stale content.

## Content quality hierarchy

1. Accurate and safe.
2. Useful to the intended audience.
3. Clear, findable, scannable, and accessible.
4. Distinctive and credible.
5. Persuasive toward a legitimate next step.
6. Efficient to create and maintain.

Conversion cannot compensate for harm, deception, confusion, or brand damage.
