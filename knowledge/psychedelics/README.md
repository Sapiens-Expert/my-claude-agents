# Psychedelics

Evidence, safety, context, culture, mental health, and uncertainty. Canonical synthesis: [psychedelics research](../../research/literature-reviews/psychedelics-and-integration.md).

## Knowledge layers

1. Current law, regulation, emergency guidance, product labeling, and official professional standards in the relevant jurisdiction.
2. Current systematic reviews, clinical guidance/consensus, trials, pharmacology, toxicology, and interaction references.
3. Professional training texts and harm-reduction/integration models.
4. Practitioner books, workbooks, case material, qualitative research, and memoir.
5. Traditional, spiritual, cultural, botanical, and experiential accounts.

Lower layers can inform questions, meaning, and context but cannot override medical evidence, consent, law, or scope.

## Safety invariants

- No sourcing, dosing, administration, medication adjustment, contraindication clearance, or facilitation instructions.
- No claim that a screening checklist makes use safe.
- Medication, supplement, medical-condition, pregnancy, psychiatric-history, or substance-combination questions require current qualified review.
- Personal, traditional, spiritual, or practitioner experience is not clinical efficacy evidence.
- Psychedelic-assisted therapy, legal facilitation, peer harm reduction, preparation coaching, and post-experience integration are distinct roles.
- Intense or meaningful experiences do not prove truth, healing, memory accuracy, or durable benefit.
- Legal status and professional requirements must be rechecked for place and date.
