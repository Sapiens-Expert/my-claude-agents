# Sales

Ethical B2B sales is a joint decision process: determine whether a material problem exists, whether change is valuable and feasible, whether the offer fits, and what evidence-backed next step serves both parties.

## Operating system

1. Define the ideal customer and disqualifying conditions.
2. Build a prioritized account/prospect universe from observable signals.
3. Use balanced, relevant prospecting to earn a conversation.
4. Discover current state, desired state, consequences, causes, stakeholders, and change constraints.
5. Qualify the opportunity and map the buying system.
6. Co-create value, decision criteria, implementation logic, and risk controls.
7. Obtain an explicit advance: a buyer-owned action with owner and date.
8. Negotiate interests and constraints without coercion.
9. Close only when the decision is supported; otherwise disqualify or nurture.
10. Measure conversion, cycle time, quality, loss/no-decision reasons, outcomes, and retention.

See [discovery and qualification](discovery-and-qualification.md), [pipeline and forecasting](pipeline-and-forecasting.md), and the [sales SOPs](../../sops/sales/README.md).

## Ethical standard

- Diagnose before prescribing.
- Do not manufacture urgency, scarcity, authority, social proof, or reciprocity.
- Never exploit fear, cognitive vulnerability, health anxiety, or confidential information.
- Make alternatives, uncertainty, price, scope, and opt-out conditions clear.
- Treat “no decision” and “not a fit” as legitimate outcomes.
