# Pipeline and forecasting

## Stage design

Stages must represent verified buyer progress, not seller activity.

| Stage | Required buyer evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Target | ICP fit and a defensible relevance signal |
| Engaged | Two-way interaction with a plausible stakeholder |
| Discovery | Material current/desired-state gap explored |
| Qualified | Fit, consequences, buying system, process, and resources sufficiently evidenced |
| Solution alignment | Outcome, approach, criteria, risks, and responsibilities aligned |
| Decision | Formal decision actions and dates confirmed |
| Won/lost/no decision | Explicit outcome and reason recorded |

## Metric hierarchy

- **Business results:** revenue, margin, retention, expansion, and realized client outcomes. These are lagging and not directly manageable.
- **Sales objectives:** pipeline coverage, opportunity quality, conversion, cycle time, forecast accuracy, and account penetration.
- **Sales activities:** prospecting blocks, conversations, stakeholder meetings, next steps, account plans, and coaching. Manage these only when evidence links them to objectives.

## Forecast discipline

- Forecast from buyer evidence, not seller confidence.
- Every active opportunity needs owner, stage evidence, next buyer action, date, value range, risk, and last meaningful movement.
- Track aging and time-in-stage; stale deals do not remain active by optimism.
- Record loss and no-decision separately.
- Review both quantity and quality; more pipeline cannot repair systematically poor fit.
