# Social selling

Social selling is the coordinated use of professional identity, useful public contribution, relationship context, and direct conversation to create informed commercial opportunities.

## Relationship sequence

1. Define audience, problem territory, and reputational boundaries.
2. Build a profile that makes relevance and authority legible.
3. Publish useful ideas and contribute substantive comments where the audience already pays attention.
4. Notice real triggers and relationship signals without converting them into assumptions about private pain.
5. Start or continue a conversation with genuine context and an easy response path.
6. Explore fit before proposing a call or offer.
7. Record next actions and move qualified conversations into the sales system.
8. Continue non-transactional relationships when no immediate opportunity exists.

## Trust model

- **Experience:** show what has actually been done or observed.
- **Expertise:** explain mechanisms and decisions accurately within scope.
- **Evidence:** use verifiable work, research, outcomes, or client-safe proof.
- **Consistency:** align profile, posts, comments, DMs, calls, and delivery.
- **Boundaries:** state uncertainty, non-fit, and referral limits.

## Anti-patterns

- Engagement pods, purchased audiences, automated intimacy, scraped personalization, mass connection requests, and comment spam.
- Pitching immediately after connection acceptance.
- Treating connection count, Social Selling Index, or impressions as business outcomes.
- Manufactured exclusivity or scarcity.
- Copying another creator’s voice or using personal-brand activity as performative self-promotion.
