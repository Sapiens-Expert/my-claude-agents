# Organizational operating system

## Principles synthesized from the library

- Preserve a credible core while allowing methods, products, and structures to evolve.
- Translate ambition into a few measurable objectives; key results are evidence of movement, not task inventories.
- Select and position people for relevant strengths while designing support around limitations.
- Give autonomy with context, clear outcomes, decision rights, and accountability.
- Combine personal care with direct, specific challenge; avoid both aggression and protective silence.
- Treat people operations as a product: research employee needs, segment carefully, prototype, measure, and iterate.
- Design remote culture intentionally through norms, rituals, information access, inclusion, and asynchronous work.
- Protect creative teams from avoidable chaos while maintaining standards and useful constraints.
- Use management systems to reduce founder dependence through ownership, cadence, documented processes, and appropriate automation.

## Failure modes

- Metrics become targets detached from purpose.
- “Culture fit” masks similarity bias or exclusion.
- Strengths language excuses harmful behavior or missing job requirements.
- Radical candor becomes permission for bluntness without care.
- Autonomy is announced without information, capability, or decision clarity.
- Automation scales a broken process.
- Growth mindset is used to individualize structural problems.

## Evidence rule

The library mixes research-based work, company case studies, and practitioner advice. Apply each principle as a context-dependent design hypothesis and measure local effects.
