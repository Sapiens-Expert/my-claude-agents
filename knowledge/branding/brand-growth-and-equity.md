# Brand growth and equity

## Growth system

Brand growth can be investigated through:

- category demand and penetration;
- mental availability across relevant category-entry situations;
- physical/digital availability and buying friction;
- distinctive-asset recognition and correct attribution;
- positioning, perceived value, and reasons to choose;
- product/service experience and delivery consistency;
- price, distribution, innovation, communication, and competitive response;
- retention and frequency, interpreted relative to category norms;
- portfolio reach, overlap, cannibalization, and investment allocation.

No single school is the universal model. Byron Sharp’s penetration/availability evidence challenges loyalty-heavy strategy, while Aaker, Ries, Holt, and practitioner traditions emphasize identity, differentiation, category, focus, and cultural meaning. Use the tension to generate tests.

## Equity measurement

Measure only dimensions linked to the decision:

- awareness/salience and relevant memory structures;
- distinctive-asset fame and uniqueness;
- category consideration and relevance;
- associations, perceived quality, trust, and value;
- penetration, repeat/frequency, switching, and customer-base structure;
- price sensitivity or premium where identifiable;
- experience, recommendation, distribution, search/share-of-voice, and commercial outcomes;
- brand-role clarity and cross-brand effects in a portfolio.

Attitudinal metrics do not replace behavior, and sales do not isolate brand causality. Define baselines, segments, time horizons, comparison sets, and plausible confounders.

## Relevance threats

- The category/subcategory declines or customer criteria shift.
- The brand loses energy, visibility, or cultural/customer relevance.
- A material negative association excludes the brand from consideration.

Responses may include innovation, a new subcategory, repositioning, experience repair, stronger availability, a relevant signature program/story, architecture change, or deliberate exit. Diagnose before choosing.
