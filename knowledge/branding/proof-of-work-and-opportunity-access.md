# Proof of work and opportunity access

Projects and work samples let an audience inspect capability more directly than titles, adjectives, endorsements or activity metrics. They are especially valuable where formal seniority is limited, a person is changing domains, work is multidisciplinary or the contribution would otherwise be invisible.

Proof is not merely uploading an artifact. A usable proof record explains:

- the problem, context and intended user;
- the person’s exact role and contribution;
- constraints, collaborators and dependencies;
- the method and consequential decisions;
- the artifact or observable work;
- results, measurement limits and what was learned;
- provenance, date, rights, confidentiality and consent;
- the capability and authority the evidence supports;
- what it does not establish;
- the appropriate audience decision or next step.

## Proof hierarchy

| Evidence | Useful for | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Self-described skill | Discoverability and hypothesis formation | No demonstrated performance |
| Learning exercise | Showing process and development | May not represent real-world constraints |
| Independent project | Demonstrating initiative and method | Outcomes and comparability may be limited |
| Work sample with clear contribution | Inspecting applied capability | Rights/confidentiality may restrict detail |
| Verified outcome or adoption | Evidence of delivered value | Attribution and selection still need context |
| Independent reference/repeat demand | Corroborating capability and trust | Must be scoped to the actual work/domain |

Certificates document learning; endorsements indicate perception; neither substitutes for applied proof. Early-career evidence should be labeled honestly rather than inflated to resemble senior authority.

## Opportunity routing

Different opportunities require different evidence. Hiring emphasizes role readiness, contribution and working constraints; consulting emphasizes diagnosis, method and client outcome; speaking emphasizes teachable insight and audience value; partnerships emphasize complementary capability, reliability and governance. Select proof for the decision rather than displaying every artifact.

Track qualified discovery, interviews, referrals, invitations, pipeline, fit, time cost and wellbeing. Application, connection, posting or portfolio volume is not the objective.
