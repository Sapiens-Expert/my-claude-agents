# Longevity

Population, preventive, lifestyle, clinical, and commercial longevity knowledge. Distinguish lifespan, healthspan, risk reduction, biomarkers, and speculative interventions.

Popular longevity books are idea maps, not consensus statements. Separate broadly supported prevention from extrapolation based on animal models, molecular mechanisms, biomarkers, self-experimentation, or unvalidated “biological age” measures. Any intervention claim requires current benefit–harm review and clinical referral where appropriate.
