# Healthspan

Years lived with functional capacity and well-being. Treat it as multidomain and longitudinal, not one score or anti-aging promise.

## Working model

Healthspan content may address sleep, movement, nutrition, stress, social connection, function, prevention, and adherence. It must distinguish:

- education and coaching from diagnosis or treatment;
- population associations from individual effects;
- symptoms, validated clinical outcomes, surrogate markers, and proprietary composite scores;
- established prevention from plausible mechanisms and experimental longevity interventions;
- lived experience from transferable evidence.

No single diet, supplement, breathing method, mobility system, microbiome profile, genetic variant, psychedelic experience, or wearable metric is a general-purpose health intervention.
