# Medical evidence and referral

## Authority ladder

1. Current clinical guidelines and regulator communications for the relevant jurisdiction.
2. Systematic reviews and well-designed controlled studies with clinically meaningful outcomes.
3. Standard physiology and clinical textbooks for durable background, subject to edition age.
4. Cohort, mechanistic, microbiome, wearable, genetic, and early feasibility studies for qualified hypotheses.
5. Clinician-authored popular books and disease guides for questions and communication—not treatment authority.
6. Commercial protocols, alternative-health books, memoirs, and personal outcomes as low-authority or narrative evidence.

Books in `sources/AI Healer/` span this entire ladder. Their inclusion records Vlad's reading archive; it does not validate every claim in them.

Herbal and supplement evidence additionally requires exact species, plant part, preparation/extract, dose, formulation, product quality, interactions, vulnerable-population risks, and current safety communications. Traditional use and natural-product chemistry do not establish clinical outcomes.

## Medical-claim gate

Before public or client use, record the population, intervention/exposure, comparator, outcome, time horizon, effect size, uncertainty, harms, study design, publication date, and applicability. Recheck current guidance for medication, disease-management, genetic, or safety claims.

Use “may,” “is associated with,” or “is being studied” only when those words accurately reflect the evidence. Mechanism is not outcome; biomarker change is not cure; an individual response is not general efficacy.

## Referral boundary

Refer to a licensed clinician when a person requests diagnosis, interpretation of symptoms or tests, medication initiation/cessation/dose changes, disease treatment, contraindication clearance, or management of significant deterioration. Urgent or severe symptoms go to local emergency services, not a coaching workflow.

Genomic and pharmacogenomic results require confirmation, current clinical interpretation, medication context, ancestry/population limits, and an appropriate clinician or genetic counselor. Never convert a consumer report directly into prescribing advice.

## Personal-data boundary

Personal genetic reports and health narratives are private by default. Store only the minimum derived constraint needed for planning. Do not publish variants, medication-response predictions, diagnoses, sample identifiers, or detailed history without explicit, item-specific approval.
