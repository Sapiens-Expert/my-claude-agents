# Taxation

Tax knowledge in VladOS exists to frame questions, organize evidence, and prepare work with qualified advisers. It does not establish authority to give tax or legal advice.

## Domain map

- Individual residence, source, domicile where relevant, and treaty tie-breakers.
- Spain: IRPF, non-resident income tax, VAT, wealth/succession context, inbound-worker regime, filing and dispute procedures.
- Cyprus: personal and corporate tax, residence, domicile-related rules, VAT, transfer pricing, holding-company context, and compliance.
- International: treaty interpretation, permanent establishment, beneficial ownership, withholding, foreign tax relief, mutual agreement procedures, transfer pricing, anti-abuse rules, exchange of information, Pillar Two, and UN/OECD model differences.
- Tax behavior and policy: compliance, incentives, harmful practices, investment, and distributional/institutional context.

## Authority order

1. Law, treaty text, official gazette, binding decisions, and current tax-authority guidance.
2. Current OECD, UN, EU, and other applicable institutional instruments, with implementation status checked.
3. Current professional manuals and major-firm summaries for orientation.
4. Academic research and textbooks for durable concepts and policy analysis.
5. Law-firm articles, commercial guides, and help-center pages as leads only.

The controlling rule depends on taxpayer, period, jurisdiction, income/asset/entity facts, treaty, elections, deadlines, and later amendments. A publication labeled 2024 or 2025 is historical unless reconfirmed for the relevant tax year.
