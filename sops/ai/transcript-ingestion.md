# Transcript ingestion

## Source classes

- Raw audio-derived transcript: primary evidence of what the recording captured, subject to ASR and speaker-label errors.
- Cleaned transcript: derived text; retain a link to the raw version and record material edits.
- Automated meeting summary: AI-generated interpretation, not primary evidence.
- Partial transcript or transfer artifact: evidence only for the text actually present; bracketed descriptions of omitted material do not establish its contents.
- Session note: operational synthesis that must preserve uncertainty, privacy, and provenance.

## Workflow

1. Record date, participants, recording/transcription consent if known, language, source media, completeness, and transcription quality.
2. Separate what was said from whether it is true. Verify consequential medical, legal, financial, personnel, credential, and market claims independently.
3. Correct speaker-label errors only when the conversation makes identity clear; record the correction.
4. Extract decisions, commitments, open questions, and reusable patterns into the narrowest appropriate destination.
5. Keep named client, health, employment, compensation, financial, legal, and psychedelic details in restricted operational files. Do not convert them into public case patterns without explicit separate consent.
6. Treat recording consent as consent to record/transcribe only, not consent to publish, train, quote, market, or share.
7. Never fill gaps in a partial transcript. Mark missing segments and unresolved dates explicitly.
8. Link the synthesis to the raw source and run a contradiction check against current state and active decisions.

## Public-use gate

Public use requires verified rights/consent, minimum necessary detail, de-identification tested for re-identification risk, claim review, brand/scope review, and human approval. Paraphrase by default; direct quotations require permission and context review.
