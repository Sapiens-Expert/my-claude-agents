# AI quality layer

Read current state, profile, active decisions, and the relevant brand/voice/project/evidence files. Separate fact, source claim, inference, estimate, recommendation, decision, and unknown. Prefer primary evidence, surface contradictions, protect sensitive data, and never infer execution from a plan.

Draft in plain language with traceable sources, then check scope, safety, brand, privacy, and whether the result creates unnecessary complexity. Governance: [AI-GOVERNANCE.md](../../AI-GOVERNANCE.md).
