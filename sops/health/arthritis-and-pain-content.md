# Arthritis and pain content review

1. Name the exact condition/population or state that the content is general; never collapse all arthritis or pain into one mechanism.
2. Classify each statement as education, association, mechanism, symptom management, functional support, prevention, treatment, disease modification, or cure.
3. Check current condition-specific guidelines and systematic evidence for outcomes and harms; books and personal stories cannot pass this gate alone.
4. Separate pain, function, quality of life, inflammation markers, imaging, and disease activity. Do not substitute one for another.
5. For movement content, state prerequisites, adaptations, dose/progression logic, stop/modify criteria, and referral needs. Avoid universal alignment or posture claims.
6. For diet/supplement content, check adequacy, restriction burden, interactions, contraindications, dosing, product quality, and prescribed-care displacement.
7. Include urgent/timely assessment language when the topic could be mistaken for self-triage.
8. Remove cure, reversal, healing, detox, reset, joint-repair, pain-free, and medication-independence language unless a narrowly defined claim passes formal medical review.
9. Protect personal health information and label lived experience as non-generalizable.
10. Assign a clinical review date and owner; update or retire stale material.
