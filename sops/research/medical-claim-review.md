# Medical claim review

1. Classify the statement: education, association, mechanism, risk, diagnosis, prevention, treatment, or cure.
2. Identify whether it concerns a disease, drug, supplement, genetic result, contraindication, or vulnerable population.
3. Locate current authoritative guidance and the strongest applicable evidence; a book or generated report cannot pass this step alone.
4. Extract population, comparator, outcomes, harms, effect size, uncertainty, date, and conflicts of interest.
5. Check jurisdiction, credential scope, and whether clinician referral is required.
6. Rewrite at the supported level or reject the claim. Log consequential claims in the claim register.
7. Keep personal health information out of the evidence argument unless its use is explicitly approved and clearly labeled as experience.

Automatic rejection triggers include cure/reversal guarantees, medication-change advice, single-cause disease stories, precise outcomes without traceable evidence, and proprietary health scores presented as clinically validated without validation data.
