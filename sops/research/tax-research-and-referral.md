# Tax research and referral

1. Write the exact fact pattern and unresolved facts; do not infer residence or entity substance from a mailing address.
2. Fix the tax period, jurisdictions, taxes, transaction, filing deadline, and decision deadline.
3. Build an issue tree: domestic law, treaty/EU overlay, characterization, calculation, reporting, anti-abuse, procedure, and evidence.
4. Retrieve current primary law and official guidance. Record effective dates, amendments, language, and retrieval date.
5. Use institutional, professional, and academic sources to interpret or challenge the primary reading; label their authority and age.
6. Record alternatives, uncertainty, missing evidence, and consequences. Never output a single definitive number from incomplete facts.
7. Escalate material conclusions to a qualified adviser in each relevant jurisdiction. Request written confirmation of assumptions, scope, deadlines, fees, and professional responsibility.
8. Preserve the final adviser memorandum and decision separately from exploratory AI research. Minimize passports, tax IDs, addresses, account details, and other personal data.

AI may organize documents, compare stated rules, create question lists, and flag conflicts. It must not submit returns, make elections, contact authorities, or implement structures without explicit authorization and professional review.
