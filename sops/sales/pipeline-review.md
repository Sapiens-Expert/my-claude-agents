# Opportunity and pipeline review

For each material opportunity:

1. Verify stage against buyer evidence.
2. Restate the problem, consequences, desired outcome, and offer fit.
3. Review stakeholder coverage and buying-process evidence.
4. Name the largest uncertainty and red flag.
5. Confirm the next buyer-owned action, owner, and date.
6. Update value range, probability logic, timing, and forecast category.
7. Advance, hold with a defined trigger, nurture, or close lost/no decision.

At pipeline level, review coverage, conversion, aging, cycle time, source quality, no-decision rate, forecast accuracy, and capacity. Coaching should improve decisions and behaviors, not pressure sellers to preserve fictional pipeline.
