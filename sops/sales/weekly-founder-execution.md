# Weekly founder execution

Every weekday: health minimums, protected sales block, CRM next actions, and a shutdown/logging ritual.

Friday review: substantive conversations, diagnostics, proposals, revenue, hours by work type, energy/health flag, what worked, what broke, what was avoided, and next week’s three calendar commitments.

Stop rules: new ideas wait 72 hours; work over four hours/week names what it replaces; ordinary outputs receive two revision passes; HealthSpan stays in its block; two amber health weeks trigger automatic workload reduction.
