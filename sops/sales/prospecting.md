# Prospecting

1. Select a small ICP/account segment and define exclusions.
2. Identify a real trigger, relevant problem hypothesis, or relationship path.
3. Research only enough to make relevance specific; do not infer private pain.
4. Choose the least intrusive suitable channel and write a concise, human message.
5. Offer a useful reason to respond and one easy question or next step.
6. Use a bounded follow-up sequence across appropriate channels.
7. Stop on refusal, unsubscribe, lack of fit, or exhausted cadence.
8. Record source, trigger, touch, response, disposition, and next-action date.

Measure positive replies and qualified conversations alongside volume. Personalization must be truthful; unusual “contact marketing” should be proportionate, professional, and respectful of privacy.
