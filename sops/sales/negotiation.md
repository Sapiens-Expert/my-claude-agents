# Negotiation

1. Prepare interests, constraints, alternatives, authority, variables, risks, and walk-away conditions.
2. Listen for the other side’s perspective; summarize it until they confirm it is accurate.
3. Use labels, mirrors, silence, and calibrated open questions to clarify—not to manufacture rapport.
4. Separate positions from underlying interests and implementation constraints.
5. Trade variables conditionally; do not concede without reciprocal value.
6. Test feasibility: who does what, how, by when, and what could prevent execution?
7. Document agreements, unresolved items, owners, approvals, and next steps.

No false deadlines, fake alternatives, invented scarcity, unauthorized commitments, or exploitation of emotional vulnerability. Tactical empathy means understanding another perspective; it does not mean agreement or psychological manipulation.
