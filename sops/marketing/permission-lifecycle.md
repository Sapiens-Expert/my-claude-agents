# Marketing permission lifecycle

## Purpose

Ensure marketing contact remains specific, welcome, proportionate and revocable across LinkedIn, email, communities and direct outreach.

## Procedure

1. Record how permission or legitimate contact context was established, including source, date, purpose and channel.
2. State the value exchange and sender identity clearly. Do not bundle marketing consent into coaching, health support, event access or community belonging where choice is not genuinely free.
3. Collect only data needed for the stated purpose and set access, retention and review rules.
4. Set a bounded frequency and content scope proportionate to the relationship and signal.
5. Provide easy preferences, opt-out and suppression. Honor refusal across related systems without requiring explanation.
6. Never infer sensitive health, trauma, psychedelic, financial or identity attributes for targeting without explicit lawful justification.
7. Review inactivity, complaints, nonresponse, changes of purpose and expired context. Reconfirm, reduce contact or stop.
8. When permission ends, suppress future outreach and delete or retain data according to documented legal/operational requirements.
9. Audit platform automation and integrations so a local opt-out is not overwritten by another list or workflow.
10. Measure permission quality through engagement in context, complaints, opt-out, regret and downstream value—not list size alone.

## Immediate stop

Stop after refusal, non-fit, a safety or boundary concern, deceptive acquisition, unclear consent provenance or exhausted follow-up sequence.
