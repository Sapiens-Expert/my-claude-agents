# Profile audit

Use the [personal-brand evidence ledger](../../templates/personal-brand-evidence-ledger.md) as the source of truth. The profile may select and clarify verified associations, but it must not create credentials, capability, authority or reputation claims that the ledger cannot support.

1. Confirm the profile's primary job, active audience, decision, category, offer and brand firewall using the [objective and role router](../../frameworks/linkedin/objective-and-role-router.md). Identify secondary-job promise or CTA collisions.
2. Review the first-screen experience: image, banner, headline, location, and current role.
3. Test whether the About section names a recognizable problem, distinctive mechanism, evidence, limits, and next step.
4. Verify dates, credentials, client names, numeric claims, and outcome language against primary records and permission.
   Treat recommendations as independently authored evidence: factual prompts are acceptable, but do not ghostwrite praise for approval. Record relationship, scope, consent and conflicts; endorsements alone do not verify competence.
5. Align Featured and Experience sections with the current opportunity route. For each major project or sample, verify the problem, Vlad's exact contribution, artifact, outcome limits, rights, confidentiality and supported capability through a [proof-of-work record](../../templates/proof-of-work-record.md).
6. Remove stale offers, generic claims, unsupported authority signals, and psychedelic brand bleed.
7. Test links and contact routes.
8. Record profile views, relevant inbound, and conversion to qualified conversation before and after material revisions.

Use the person's chosen professional name and actual location/availability; distinguish a market served from residence. Minimize exposed contact and résumé data. Photos, age cues, luxury/status imagery, political conformity and public-profile completeness must never be treated as competence or fit evidence.

Do not optimize for a platform completion score at the expense of positioning clarity or truth.
