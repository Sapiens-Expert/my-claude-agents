# Content production

1. Select one audience problem, question, or decision from research, conversations, or the idea bank.
2. Classify the claim and retrieve the required evidence.
3. Choose one job: recognition, reframe, mechanism, proof, objection, or invitation.
   Also classify the relationship state as value contribution, invitation, commercial ask or relationship care; use the [context-native creative card](../../templates/context-native-creative-card.md) for campaigns or material asks.
4. Draft around one core idea using concrete language and a recognizable executive context.
5. Add only the evidence, story, or personal data needed; distinguish experience from general evidence.
   For project breakdowns, state the original problem, exact contribution, constraints, method, artifact, outcome limits and learning. Never imply sole authorship, client permission or causal impact that the record does not support.
6. Apply executive-healthspan voice, brand firewall, clinical limits, and claim rules.
7. Use a restrained close or CTA proportional to reader intent.
8. Publish at a sustainable cadence and log source idea, format, CTA, and funnel response.
9. Review unique qualified nonfollower reach, category-entry-point coverage, brand attribution, qualified conversations, profile movement, repeat exposure, and attributable next steps. Do not mistake engagement from existing followers for market penetration.

For roundups, interviews or multi-contributor assets, use the [content-based networking framework](../../frameworks/linkedin/content-based-networking.md). Select for relevant evidence and perspective coverage, disclose commercial purpose, obtain granular permissions, preserve disagreement and make promotion optional.

Contradictory advice about length, format, frequency, tone or connection breadth is an experiment prompt, not a mandate. Define the audience and outcome, change one material variable where practical, keep evidence/safety controls fixed, and compare qualified outcomes plus cost and harm signals. Triage criticism by evidence, affected stakeholder, severity and good faith; never ignore it merely because engagement is high.

Preserve invariant purpose, claims, evidence, identity, scope and disclosure across LinkedIn formats. Adapt hooks, pacing, tone, interaction and CTA to the current context only after verifying platform-controlled features and policies. Prior value never creates permission to sell, and there is no fixed value-to-ask posting ratio.

“Simple, unexpected, concrete, credible, emotional, stories” is an editing lens from *Made to Stick*, not a requirement to sensationalize. Never trade accuracy for stickiness.

If sharing or word of mouth is an explicit objective, use the [responsible transmission audit](../../frameworks/content/responsible-transmission-audit.md). Connect ideas to recurring professional contexts and practical value without manufacturing status, outrage or public exposure. Measure accurate recall, attribution and qualified benefit—not reposts alone.

When LinkedIn supports a capacity-constrained campaign, distinguish exposure, engagement, explicit interest, qualified conversation/application, commitment and delivered value. A reaction is not a waitlist signal, and a waitlist is not consent for repeated outreach. Route cohort or release planning through the [capacity-first campaign framework](../../frameworks/marketing/capacity-first-campaign.md).

For a staged launch, use the [evidence-led launch framework](../../frameworks/marketing/evidence-led-launch.md). Make the forthcoming offer explicit throughout the education sequence, let each unit stand alone as useful and never use accumulated attention, authority or anticipation as pressure to buy.
