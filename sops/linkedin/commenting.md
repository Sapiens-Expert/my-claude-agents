# Commenting

1. Select posts by relevant people or conversations, not expected reach alone.
2. Read the full post and material context before responding.
3. Add at least one mechanism, executive consequence, useful distinction, relevant experience, or precise question.
4. Keep the contribution self-contained and proportionate.
5. Do not summarize, flatter generically, diagnose the author, hijack the thread, or pitch.
6. Reply naturally when a real conversation develops; do not force a DM transition.
7. Track repeated relevant interaction and qualified conversation, not comment count alone.

The active cadence is approximately five targeted comments per weekday. Older library recommendations for high-volume daily commenting do not supersede that decision.
