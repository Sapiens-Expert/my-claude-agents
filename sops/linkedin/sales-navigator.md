# Sales Navigator research

1. Start from an account/role hypothesis and a concrete weekly action goal.
2. Build account lists before broad lead lists when the sale involves organizations.
3. Filter using stable concepts: geography, company attributes, function, seniority, relationship proximity, activity, and material change signals.
4. Inspect profiles and company context manually before saving or contacting.
5. Tag the source signal and uncertainty; never label health status or private pain from public activity.
6. Keep lists small enough for human research and follow-through.
7. Review saved accounts/leads for changes and remove stale/non-fit records.
8. Measure qualified conversations and opportunities created, not saved-lead volume.

The source library includes a July 2025 guide describing specific lead/account filters and counts. Treat every name, count, AI feature, buyer-intent field, limit, and workflow as stale until confirmed in the live product.
