# Medication and interaction referral

When a person asks whether a psychedelic is compatible with a medication, supplement, medical condition, pregnancy/breastfeeding, or another substance:

1. Do not answer from memory, a book table, online anecdote, or AI synthesis.
2. Do not recommend starting, stopping, tapering, pausing, substituting, or timing medication.
3. Record the question accurately without interpreting safety.
4. Refer to a qualified prescriber, pharmacist, toxicology/poison service, or clinician with relevant expertise and access to current references.
5. If exposure has occurred and concerning symptoms or uncertainty exist, direct the person to appropriate local urgent/emergency or poison-control support.
6. Resume coaching only when the question is within scope and the person has appropriate support.

The repository’s pharmacology, toxicology, and interaction textbooks are background references, not a self-service clearance system; several are dated editions.
