# Psychedelic coaching intake and scope

1. Confirm identity, age/legal capacity, location/jurisdiction, requested service, and whether an experience has already occurred.
2. Explain exclusions: no medical/psychiatric clearance, psychotherapy, emergency care, sourcing, dosing, prescribing, medication change, or illegal facilitation.
3. Ask about immediate safety, sleep, functioning, suicidality, psychosis/mania history or signs, dissociation, substance use, medication/supplements, medical conditions, pregnancy, and current professional support only to determine scope/referral—not to diagnose.
4. Determine whether qualified medical/mental-health review is required before coaching can proceed.
5. Review confidentiality, data minimization, communication, fees, cancellation, boundaries, consent, supervision/consultation, and crisis limitations.
6. Agree goals, safe contact/support plan, referral pathway, and review points.
7. Document accept, conditional accept, defer, decline, or urgent referral with rationale.

A completed intake never constitutes medical clearance or a guarantee of safety.
