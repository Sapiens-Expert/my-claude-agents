# Psychedelic content safety

Check legal/platform context, evidence calibration, clinical boundaries, contraindication/risk framing, non-glamorizing language, and crisis/referral direction. Do not include sourcing or procedural drug-use instructions.

- Do not reproduce dose charts, combination advice, medication-taper instructions, extraction/growing/identification procedures, or “safe-use” recipes.
- Do not frame difficult experiences, ego dissolution, trauma activation, or spiritual emergency as inherently healing.
- Do not imply that music, touch, a sitter, ritual, preparation, or integration eliminates risk.
- Do not use medicalized “therapy” language for unlicensed coaching or illegal contexts.
- Separate clinical-trial findings from naturalistic use and product marketing.
- Name uncertainty, adverse events, non-response, prolonged effects, and the limits of screening.
- Avoid anti-medication, anti-psychiatry, recovered-memory certainty, Indigenous appropriation, and facilitator hero narratives.
