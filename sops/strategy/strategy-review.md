# Strategy review SOP

## Purpose

Test whether a proposed strategy is a coherent, evidence-linked response to a real challenge before committing or expanding resources.

## Inputs

- completed [strategy kernel template](../../templates/strategy-kernel.md);
- relevant research and decision records;
- implementation owner and affected stakeholder representation.

## Review sequence

1. Read the diagnosis without the proposal. Ask whether it explains the difficulty and separates evidence from inference.
2. Present the strongest competing diagnosis. Identify information that would distinguish them.
3. Verify that the guiding policy directly addresses the selected causal structure.
4. Identify choices avoided: non-priorities, resource shifts and activities stopped.
5. Map action reinforcement, dependencies and the binding weak link.
6. Test buyer/customer value, organizational feasibility, market perception and likely responses.
7. Run a premortem covering evidence error, execution, ethics, stakeholder harm and environmental change.
8. Verify measures observe the causal chain and outcome—not only activity.
9. Set decision thresholds, owner and review date.
10. Approve, revise, run a bounded test or reject. Record the reasoning.

## Reject or revise when

- the “diagnosis” restates a goal;
- the policy is a slogan or generic best practice;
- actions are an unprioritized project list;
- nothing stops and resources do not shift;
- no evidence could disconfirm the strategy;
- stakeholder harm or legal/ethical risk is ungoverned;
- no accountable owner or review trigger exists.

## Value-innovation module

Use the [value-innovation framework](../../frameworks/strategy/value-innovation.md) only when the diagnosis supports market reconstruction. Review whether canvas factors and alternative levels are evidenced, ERRC choices create real trade-offs and resource shifts, and the proposal passes utility, access/price, cost/quality, adoption and public/ethical gates. A divergent curve without local validation is not approval evidence.

## Output

A decision-ready strategy kernel and linked decision record, including unresolved uncertainty and explicit follow-up.
