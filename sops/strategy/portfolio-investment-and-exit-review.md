# Portfolio investment and exit review

## Purpose

Assign explicit investment states to businesses, brands and major offers while preserving customer, legal, safety and knowledge obligations.

## Procedure

1. Define portfolio scope, owner, horizon, available resources and strategic constraints.
2. For each asset, assemble comparable evidence on strategic fit, demand, category/brand position, qualified penetration/access, economics, capability, dependencies, risk and stakeholder value.
3. Identify strategic uncertainties and the smallest information action that could change allocation.
4. Compare grow/enter, maintain, harvest, consolidate/migrate, divest and retire states. Include no additional investment.
5. Decompose claimed synergy into mechanism, owner, required coordination, cost and measurable net benefit.
6. Model opportunity cost and the resources actually released or required by each option.
7. Assign a state, owner, investment, milestone, disconfirming evidence and review date. Record dissent and dependencies.
8. For harvest, divest or retirement, preserve quality, support, records, corrections, contracts, privacy, referrals and transparent customer communication.
9. Update strategy, project state, portfolio register and deprecation records consistently.
10. Review when signposts cross thresholds; do not delay exit solely because prior investment is sunk.

## Escalate when

- responsibilities or data cannot transfer safely;
- customers may lose access, support, safety information or paid commitments;
- the decision changes clinical, psychedelic, tax or professional boundaries;
- claimed synergy or valuation lacks an identifiable evidence path.
