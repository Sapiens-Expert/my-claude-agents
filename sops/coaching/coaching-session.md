# Coaching session

## Before

Review the client's goals, last commitments, consent/scope, relevant measures, unresolved risks, and current context. Prepare questions lightly; do not pre-decide the client's diagnosis or solution.

## Open

1. Check current state and any urgent issue.
2. Confirm available time and confidentiality/context limitations.
3. Ask what would make the conversation useful and agree the focus.

## Work

1. Listen and reflect until the problem, desired change, ambivalence, and context are sufficiently understood.
2. Explore resources, exceptions, previous attempts, constraints, consequences, and competing commitments.
3. Offer observations, information, challenge, or a framework with permission and calibrated certainty.
4. Watch for medical/psychological risk, dependency, coercion, scope drift, and impaired decision capacity; pause and refer where necessary.
5. Let insight remain exploratory until translated into a decision or test the client genuinely owns.

## Close

1. Ask the client to summarize what matters.
2. Record any decision or experiment: action, cue/context, minimum version, support, obstacle response, measure, and review date.
3. Confirm coach follow-ups, client follow-ups, boundaries, and next meeting.
4. Store only the minimum necessary confidential note; separate direct observation, client report, coach interpretation, and open question.

## Review

At the next session, examine what occurred without equating completion with virtue or non-completion with resistance. Decide whether to retain, adapt, replace, stop, or refer.
