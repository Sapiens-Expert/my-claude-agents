# Brand partnership and sponsorship review

## Purpose

Approve only partnerships that create reciprocal audience value and manageable bidirectional brand risk.

## Procedure

1. Define the identity, category-entry-point or customer-value objective and compare non-partnership alternatives.
2. Audit audience overlap, partner identity, conduct, evidence, ownership, conflicts, legal status, accessibility and adverse history.
3. Specify each party's role, prominence, accountability and the value neither can create as effectively alone.
4. Model positive and negative association, authority and claim transfer in both directions, including plausible incidents.
5. Price total cost: rights, activation, delivery, accessibility, staff, measurement, opportunity cost and exit.
6. Contract IP, trademarks, approvals, substantiated claims, data/consent, quality, safeguarding, incidents, public statements, termination and post-exit use.
7. Define baseline, incremental qualified reach, attribution, behavior/customer value, cost and reputation/harm guardrails.
8. Pilot or stage commitment where practical. Do not value exposure equivalents as outcomes.
9. Monitor delivery and incidents; pause when claims, safety or consent conditions fail.
10. Renew, redesign or exit according to thresholds; update the portfolio register and archive evidence.

## Mandatory escalation

Escalate medical/scientific endorsement, psychedelic or vulnerable-audience exposure, sensitive data, minors, regulated claims, unclear rights, cross-domain authority transfer or absent termination rights.
