# Brand portfolio consolidation and retirement

## Purpose

Reduce portfolio complexity without destroying useful equity, customer access, records, commitments or safety information.

## Procedure

1. Open a portfolio decision record with owner, business rationale, affected audiences and alternatives: maintain, invest, merge, migrate, demote to descriptor, pause/dormant, license/sell or retire.
2. Document current equity, retrieval, traffic, contracts, revenue, obligations, archives, URLs, trademarks, data, partners, complaints and safety dependencies.
3. Test whether the asset has independent audience meaning or performs a necessary authority/risk-separation role.
4. Model customer confusion, lost demand, search/navigation loss, reputation transfer, operational savings, legal/tax effects and reversibility.
5. Define the destination brand/descriptor and exact equivalence limits. Never imply credentials, evidence or service continuity that do not transfer.
6. Create the migration plan: audience and partner communication, redirects, navigation, contract changes, record/archive retention, consent/data handling, support and incident ownership.
7. Set a transition period, baseline, guardrails, rollback conditions and named owners.
8. Execute in stages where practical; monitor findability, comprehension, conversion, complaints, service continuity and harmful authority transfer.
9. Close only after obligations, redirects, ownership, archival access and safety/correction paths are verified.
10. Record outcome and review whether the surviving portfolio received the promised investment and clarity.

## Stop or escalate when

- legal ownership, contracts or data responsibility are unclear;
- customers could lose access to records, corrections, referrals or paid services;
- the move collapses a necessary clinical, psychedelic, tax or professional boundary;
- no accountable destination owner or rollback path exists.
