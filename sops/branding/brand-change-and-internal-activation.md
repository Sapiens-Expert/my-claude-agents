# Brand change and internal activation

## Trigger

Use before changing core identity, positioning, major distinctive assets or portfolio presentation, and before asking employees or partners to activate a revised promise.

## Procedure

1. Open a decision record identifying the strategic, audience, legal, ethical, portfolio or delivery evidence requiring change.
2. Compare preserve, correct within the current system, evolve, migrate and retire options. Explicitly test executive boredom and novelty bias.
3. Inventory affected claims, assets, channels, audiences, contracts, partners, records, domains and safety/authority boundaries.
4. Test proposed meaning and execution for comprehension, attribution, accessibility, credibility and harmful interpretation.
5. Define what remains stable, what changes, versions, owners, migration steps, transition period, metrics and reversal conditions.
6. Translate the approved identity into role-specific learning: promise, evidence, behaviors, boundaries, nonclaims and escalation.
7. Establish belief through leadership behavior, resources and transparent proof; create a protected path for reporting contradictions.
8. Embed delivery in workflows, incentives, product/service standards, partner requirements and incident handling.
9. Pilot where feasible; monitor audience understanding, service continuity, employee behavior, complaints and boundary failures.
10. Scale, revise or reverse according to predefined evidence. Archive superseded guidance without erasing provenance.

## Stop or escalate when

- the new identity outruns current capability or evidence;
- clinical, scientific, psychedelic, tax or professional authority could transfer incorrectly;
- material assets or customer access lack a migration plan;
- leadership asks for advocacy while unresolved delivery contradictions persist.
