# UX copy review

1. State the user, task, context, emotional/risk state, and desired outcome.
2. Walk the journey from entry through completion, recovery, cancellation, and help.
3. Check information order, headings, labels, commands, help, errors, empty states, confirmations, permissions, and notifications.
4. Verify terminology consistency and alignment between words, controls, and actual system behavior.
5. Test scanning, plain language, accessibility, localization, and screen-reader meaning.
6. Check privacy, consent, price, commitment, reversibility, and safety consequences.
7. Remove blame, ambiguity, unnecessary personality, persuasion pressure, and dead ends.
8. Test with representative users or a documented heuristic review.
9. Measure completion, comprehension, errors/recovery, confidence, support demand, and unintended outcomes.

Never approve UX copy based on conversion alone when users misunderstand the action or its consequences.
