# Content production

For consequential long-form, campaign, sales, executive or knowledge-base work, use the [governed writing workflow](../../frameworks/content/governed-writing-workflow.md) and its [writing record](../../templates/writing-workflow-record.md). The steps below remain the compact production route.

For commercial copy, build a [copy fact pack](../../templates/copy-fact-pack.md) before drafting. Preserve the claim trail through writing, design, approval, publication and correction; a handoff cannot erase qualifiers or change the offer contract.

1. Start from an audience problem and existing research, transcript, coaching pattern, search/support signal, or approved personal data.
2. Define the user and business job, one controlling idea, journey role, intended next step, and success measure.
   Decide distribution and promotion before production; channel choice must follow the audience job, not automatic cross-posting.
   Classify the unit as value contribution, invitation, commercial ask or relationship care. For channel adaptation, use the [context-native value-and-ask framework](../../frameworks/content/context-native-value-and-ask.md).
3. Select each material claim and verify it to the required evidence level.
4. Outline in the reader’s question order; choose only the story, proof, or example needed.
5. Draft: recognizable context → useful distinction/mechanism → implication → proportionate next step.
6. Revise in passes: meaning/evidence → structure → voice/clarity → mechanics/format. Use the [editorial-review SOP](editorial-review.md) for consequential or externally reviewed work.
   Separate the thinking draft from the logic, reader and voice passes; fluent first drafts are not publication-ready evidence.
7. Apply brand, platform, accessibility, privacy, consent, rights, clinical, and safety rules.
8. Confirm that headline, preview, CTA, links, and metadata accurately represent the content.
9. Ship after at most two ordinary revision cycles unless risk or evidence requires more.
10. Log source, claims, owner, publication state, maintenance/review date, and meaningful outcomes.

When adapting an existing asset, use the [provenance-preserving transformation framework](../../frameworks/content/provenance-preserving-transformation.md). Record the parent and atomic units, preserve claim scope/evidence/uncertainty, clear rights and consent, add distinct channel value and propagate corrections to every derivative.

Reach and clicks are diagnostic. Measure production/cost, qualified reach, useful consumption, retained permission, qualified decision, delivered customer value and trust guardrails. A before/after analytics change or attributed touch is not causal incrementality.
