# Conversion content

1. Define the audience, intent, offer fit, legitimate desired action, and non-fit cases.
2. Research actual questions, objections, alternatives, language, and sources of anxiety. For recurring material questions, complete the [customer-question content brief](../../templates/customer-question-content-brief.md), including evidence, price/cost, drawbacks, fair comparisons, non-fit and freshness.
3. Diagnose the conversion barrier using qualitative and behavioral evidence before changing copy.
4. Clarify the problem, outcome, mechanism, scope, price/commitment, proof, limitations, and next step.
   For landing-page, offer, campaign or direct-response copy, use the [claim-to-proof copy framework](../../frameworks/content/claim-to-proof-copy.md). Map each feature through immediate function to supported customer progress, and stop the benefit ladder where evidence stops.
5. Remove avoidable usability friction and disclose material consequences before action.
6. Form a falsifiable experiment hypothesis and define primary, guardrail, and downstream metrics.
7. Validate instrumentation and run an appropriately designed test where volume permits.
8. Examine segment effects, complaints, cancellations, regret, support demand, and client quality.
9. Adopt, iterate, or revert based on the full evidence—not uplift alone.

Release only after validity, decision quality, voluntariness and delivered-performance review. Copy that raises response by weakening comprehension, consent, fit or customer welfare fails the test.

For content-led B2B journeys, complete the [content-to-outcome brief](../../templates/b2b-content-to-outcome-brief.md). Do not require a form, phone number or sales contact where a self-service/no-form path matches readiness. Treat platform exposure, observed touch, self-report, multi-touch contribution and controlled incrementality as different evidence levels.

When content is sent before a meeting, state why it helps and how long it takes, send only what is relevant, provide an accessible or live alternative and invite questions. Never make consumption a commitment test, replace discovery with homework, or treat page views as permission to infer sensitive intent.

When social proof, authority, scarcity, reciprocity, commitment or belonging cues appear, run the [persuasion-cue review](persuasion-cue-review.md). Test cumulative pressure, not only literal accuracy.

For a bounded cohort, application window, event or capacity-constrained service, use the [capacity-first campaign framework](../../frameworks/marketing/capacity-first-campaign.md). Verify the real service capacity and every scarcity claim, distinguish signal strength, publish allocation rules and include fulfilment, exclusion and team recovery in the outcome review.

When using a customer-as-hero or brand-as-guide structure, apply the [customer-centered message framework](../../frameworks/content/customer-centered-message.md). Keep the problem observable, authority bounded, plan complete, stakes proportionate and outcome evidence-calibrated.

For health, coaching, and psychedelic contexts, no test may use unvalidated outcome promises, clinical implication, fear of deterioration, or impaired consent as a conversion lever.
