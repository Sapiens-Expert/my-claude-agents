# Persuasion-cue review

## Trigger

Required for material using testimonials, popularity, credentials, limited availability, gifts, concessions, public commitments, community identity or high-pressure sequencing.

## Procedure

1. Inventory all explicit and implicit persuasion cues using the [persuasion-cue audit](../../templates/persuasion-cue-audit.md).
2. Verify social-proof source, denominator, selection, outcome and period; authority scope, currency and conflicts; scarcity cause, quantity/time and stability.
3. Remove false similarity, flattery, obligation engineering, fake consensus, reset deadlines, identity threats, loyalty tests and sunk-cost pressure.
4. Read the material with cues removed. Confirm that value, price, alternatives, proof, limitations, risk and non-fit remain clear.
5. Assess stacked pressure, time to reflect, accessibility, privacy, relationship dependence and vulnerable audiences.
6. Add easy refusal, revision, cancellation, complaint and referral. Use private, self-authored commitments for behavior support.
7. Obtain domain review for health, clinical, psychedelic, tax, financial or credential implications.
8. Approve, revise or prohibit; record evidence expiry and the owner who must revalidate changing proof or scarcity.
9. Monitor comprehension, fit, regret, cancellations, complaints and harms alongside response.

Literal truth is necessary but insufficient: irrelevant or stacked cues can still impair informed choice.
