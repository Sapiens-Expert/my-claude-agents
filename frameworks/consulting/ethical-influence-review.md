# Ethical influence review

Use before publishing or deploying material that uses testimonials, credentials, scarcity, gifts, commitments, community identity or other persuasion cues.

## 1. Define welfare and action

State the audience, requested action, stakes, reversibility, intended recipient benefit, sponsor benefit, conflicts and who may be harmed.

## 2. Inventory every cue

Record reciprocity, liking/similarity, social proof, authority, scarcity, commitment/consistency and unity cues—including cues created unintentionally by interface, sequencing, status or relationship.

For sales scripts and sequences, also scan for false consensus, instrumental compliments, pain induction, leading micro-yes questions, premature commitment, false dilemmas, prior disclosures reused as leverage, objection neutralization, strategic silence and post-refusal extraction. Complete the [sales-script autonomy audit](../../templates/sales-script-autonomy-audit.md) when any pattern appears.

## 3. Verify cue integrity

For each cue test accuracy, materiality, recency, relevance and provenance. Verify denominators/selection for proof, domain scope/conflicts for authority, and factual stability for scarcity.

## 4. Separate cues from merits

Confirm that value, alternatives, price, effort, evidence, limitations, risks and non-fit remain understandable without the cue. Remove cues that substitute confidence for decision-quality evidence.

## 5. Test autonomy and cumulative pressure

Assess consent, reflection time, opt-out/exit, privacy, accessibility, vulnerability, relationship dependence and the cumulative effect of stacked cues. Increase safeguards with stakes.

## 6. Design recipient protection

Add cooling-off, private/revisable commitments, easy refusal, proof context, credential boundaries, stable deadlines, complaint route and referral where appropriate.

## 7. Measure outcomes

Predefine comprehension, voluntariness, qualified action, benefit, regret, complaint, cancellation, adverse event and subgroup guardrails. Conversion alone cannot approve the design.

## 8. Decide and govern

Approve, revise, remove, pilot or prohibit. Record owner, rationale, evidence, expiry and monitoring. Re-review when context, proof, authority or scarcity changes.

## Output contract

Complete the [persuasion-cue audit](../../templates/persuasion-cue-audit.md) and link it to the content, offer, sales or intervention decision.
