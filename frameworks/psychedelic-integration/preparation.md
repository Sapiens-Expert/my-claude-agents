# Preparation

Preparation coaching clarifies intention, expectations, uncertainty, support, scope, and aftercare. It is not facilitation, medical screening, approval, or proof that proceeding is appropriate.

## Preparation conversation

- Clarify the person’s goal, context, prior experiences, expectations, fears, and alternatives including not proceeding.
- Explain the coaching role, confidentiality limits, records, fees, conflicts, communication, and termination/referral conditions.
- Identify medical, psychiatric, medication/supplement, pregnancy, substance-use, legal, and functional questions requiring qualified review.
- Discuss psychological variability, difficult/prolonged effects, impaired judgment, memory uncertainty, and lack of guaranteed benefit.
- Plan ordinary support: safe contacts, reduced post-experience demands, sleep, follow-up, and access to professional help.
- Discuss boundaries and consent in advance, including touch, recording, confidentiality, money, spirituality, and relationships.
- Define signs that change the plan or require escalation.

Do not advise tapering/stopping medication, provide substance-specific dosing, optimize combinations, recommend illegal activity, or convert preparation into covert facilitation.
