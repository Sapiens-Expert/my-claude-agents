# Integration

Prioritize safety and daily stability, then explore narrative, emotions, relationships, values, meaning, and behavior. Convert only well-considered insights into small experiments. Avoid urgent life decisions driven solely by post-experience intensity.

Integration should be collaborative and non-directive. The client owns meaning; the coach supports reflection, distinguishes observation from interpretation, and does not certify metaphysical claims, recovered memories, diagnoses, or treatment success.

Track sleep, orientation, daily functioning, relationships, substance use, risk, and the practical effects of any action. Increased intensity, certainty, or catharsis is not automatically progress.
