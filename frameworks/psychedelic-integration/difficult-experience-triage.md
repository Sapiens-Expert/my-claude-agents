# Difficult-experience triage

## First question: routine integration or a safety/clinical issue?

Assess timing, orientation, sleep, basic needs, current substances/medication changes, medical symptoms, suicidal/violent risk, reality testing, agitation, support, and functional impairment. If urgent or outside scope, connect to local emergency/crisis or qualified clinical care.

## If stable and within coaching scope

1. Slow the pace and reduce stimulation and major demands.
2. Support sleep, food, hydration, routine, trusted connection, and ordinary grounding without promising treatment.
3. Let the person describe what happened; avoid forced retelling or interpretation.
4. Normalize that difficult reactions can occur without declaring them beneficial or pathological.
5. Separate sensory/event memory, emotion, meaning, belief, and proposed action.
6. Delay high-consequence decisions and further psychedelic use.
7. Agree check-in timing, deterioration signs, and escalation path.

Do not label psychosis or mania away as awakening, spiritual emergency, ego death, trauma release, or integration difficulty. Those narratives may be explored only after safety and qualified assessment.
