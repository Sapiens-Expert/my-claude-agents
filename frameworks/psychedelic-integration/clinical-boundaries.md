# Clinical boundaries

Integration coaching is not psychiatry, psychotherapy, emergency care, prescribing, or substance facilitation. Screen for instability, suicidality, psychosis/mania risk, severe dissociation, medication concerns, and functional deterioration; refer promptly to qualified care.

## Mandatory escalation domains

- Current suicidal or homicidal thoughts, intent, plan, inability to remain safe, severe self-neglect, or acute medical symptoms.
- Loss of reality testing, hallucinations or delusional conviction outside the expected acute context, mania/hypomania signs, severe agitation, confusion, or dangerous impulsivity.
- Persistent inability to sleep, eat, work, care for dependents, or manage basic daily life.
- Severe or worsening panic, dissociation, trauma symptoms, depression, substance-use escalation, or prolonged perceptual disturbance.
- Medication withdrawal/change, possible interaction/toxicity, pregnancy/breastfeeding, cardiovascular/neurological risk, or other medical complexity.
- Abuse, exploitation, boundary violation, coercion, or unsafe facilitator/community dynamics.

This list is not exhaustive and does not establish medical clearance. When urgent risk may be present, prioritize immediate local emergency/crisis or qualified clinical support rather than continued routine coaching.
