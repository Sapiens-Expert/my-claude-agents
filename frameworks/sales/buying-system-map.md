# Buying-system map

## Stakeholder roles

- Economic buyer: accountable for the resources and business result.
- User: experiences the solution and implementation burden.
- Technical/functional evaluator: assesses requirements and risk.
- Champion: actively advocates, has influence, and helps navigate the process.
- Coach: provides credible information and guidance; may or may not advocate.
- Blocker or risk owner: can slow or stop the decision for legitimate or political reasons.

For each stakeholder, record desired results, personal/professional wins, concerns, influence, stance, evidence, access, and next action. Labels are provisional and must be updated from behavior.

## Opportunity red flags

- Only one contact in a multi-party decision.
- No access to the economic buyer or decision logic.
- Problem and consequences are asserted only by the seller.
- Demo/proposal requested before diagnosis.
- Decision criteria appear written for a competitor.
- Dates are seller-created rather than linked to a buyer event.
- Repeated meetings without reciprocal action.
- Champion language without observable advocacy.
- Material implementation, procurement, legal, security, or clinical risk remains unmapped.
