# LinkedIn operating loop

Use to design or review a LinkedIn social-selling system without relying on fixed activity quotas or dated features.

## 1. Contract the role of LinkedIn

Define the diagnosed growth/sales problem, offers, qualified audiences, non-fit, brand boundaries, business outcomes, capacity and LinkedIn's role relative to other channels.

Use the [objective and role router](objective-and-role-router.md) to select employment, independent service, company presence, social selling or recruiting as the primary job. Document secondary jobs and resolve audience, proof, authority, privacy and CTA collisions before continuing.

## 2. Align the public evidence surface

Audit profile identification, category relevance, proof, audience value, authority boundaries, distinctive attribution and next step. Remove claims or offers that delivery cannot support.

Then diagnose the five LinkedIn functions—profile, network, messaging, content and personal brand. Name the weakest constraint and its evidence; do not increase activity elsewhere merely to mask it.

## 3. Build the content-to-conversation map

Cover relevant entry points across recognition, understanding, evaluation, readiness and post-engagement. Attach evidence, CTA, maintenance and outcome measures.

## 4. Define prospect relevance

Specify account/person criteria, lawful information sources, legitimate contact signals, uncertainty and exclusions. Prohibit sensitive inference, scraping and volume-first list building.

Classify each material signal as observable fact, inference or unknown. Record sensitivity, confidence and the least-intrusive validation step.

## 5. Run the relationship sequence

Move through relevant connection, permission, dialogue, value and qualified next step. Let the recipient determine whether and where the conversation continues.

Apply the [message decision and progression framework](message-decision-and-progression.md): verified context, relevance hypothesis, bounded proof, proportionate ask and easy exit. A response permits clarification; only documented fit and mutual purpose justify qualification or channel handoff.

When more than one channel is used, maintain one coordinated contact history, one frequency boundary and one suppression state. Multi-touch means adaptive channel choice, not maximum touch.

## 6. Set capacity-based routines

Allocate time for inbound response, relationship maintenance, research, thoughtful commenting, publishing, outreach, follow-up and evidence logging. Set volume from quality and follow-through capacity.

When a community or event is used, define member/participant value, transparent roles, granular permission, accessible delivery, truthful replay and follow-up before promotion. Assess relationship stage from observable reciprocity, not exposure.

## 7. Measure the full chain

Separate activity, quality, qualified availability, pipeline, economics, reputation and safety. Use multi-touch contribution and record counterfactual uncertainty.

## 8. Reverify platform state

Before operational changes, verify current fields, features, search limits, messaging rules, tool permissions and platform policies. Version the check date and source.

## 9. Decide and learn

Continue, revise, narrow, pause or stop based on qualified outcomes, costs, relationship quality, complaints and platform risk—not effort alone.

## Output contract

Complete the [LinkedIn operating brief](../../templates/linkedin-operating-brief.md), update relevant SOPs and record current platform verification separately from durable principles.
