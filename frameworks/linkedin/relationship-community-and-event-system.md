# Relationship, community and event system

Use when LinkedIn content, a professional community or an educational event is intended to support relationships and legitimate commercial outcomes.

## Relationship ladder

| Stage | Meaning | Evidence required to advance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relevant stranger | Plausible professional fit; no relationship claimed | Verified nonsensitive relevance |
| Recognized source | The person has encountered useful attributed work | Observable exposure only; not consent |
| Voluntary participant | They explicitly join, register, subscribe, reply or engage | Purpose-specific action and disclosures |
| Reciprocal contact | A real exchange establishes interest or shared purpose | Substantive two-way interaction |
| Qualified conversation | Both agree exploration may be useful | Mutual purpose, fit hypothesis and channel consent |
| Evaluation relationship | Stakeholders, evidence, risks and next steps are defined | Documented buyer/partner evidence |
| Customer/partner relationship | Delivery and outcomes sustain trust | Real value, governance and ongoing consent |

Time, repetition, connection status, group co-membership, attendance and content engagement do not advance the stage by themselves.

## Community contract

Define the member purpose, inclusion/non-fit, public owner, sponsor, actual operators, moderator powers, evidence standard, commercial role, privacy expectations, data use, accessibility, complaint path, exit and suppression. Convening creates responsibility, not expertise. Membership never authorizes unrelated sales targeting or off-platform enrichment.

Assistance with an executive or brand account requires authorization, editorial control and accurate authorship. Never simulate a personal message or participation by someone who did not actually send, review or approve it.

## Educational-event lifecycle

1. **Purpose:** define the audience outcome, organizational outcome and whether an offer or sales follow-up is involved.
2. **Topic:** validate a real audience question and evidence standard.
3. **Registration:** disclose host, sponsor, data use, recording, follow-up choices and commercial purpose; collect granular permission.
4. **Production:** assign actual speakers/operators, rehearse, test technology and provide captions, transcript and accessible materials.
5. **Promotion:** use only authorized audiences and channels with frequency and suppression controls.
6. **Delivery:** teach accurately; never manufacture attendance, social proof, questions or participation.
7. **Replay:** label recorded, on-demand or automated status, original date and material updates.
8. **Follow-up:** deliver what was promised; segment by explicit choices rather than attendance or watch time alone.
9. **Evaluation:** measure learning, accessibility, qualified progression, attribution, economics, complaints and customer value.

## Data and contact record

Maintain canonical person/account ID, professional role, provenance, relevance hypothesis, lawful basis or permission by purpose/channel, observed relationship stage, actual sender, content/event/claim version, contact history, preferences, suppression, retention review, qualified outcome and attribution confidence. The queue must never schedule a suppressed contact.

## Decision

Continue, revise, narrow, separate, pause or stop based on participant value, reciprocity, qualified progression, full cost, customer outcomes and harm signals—not community size or attendance alone.
