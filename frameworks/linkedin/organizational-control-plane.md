# Organizational LinkedIn control plane

Use to govern company pages, career surfaces, showcases, organizational content, employee participation, paid distribution and stakeholder response. A page is an operated organizational asset, not a mandatory badge or substitute for a website.

## 1. Define purpose and stakeholders

Choose the primary decision the surface supports: organizational validation, learning, demand, service, employment, recruiting or corporate communication. Define audiences, outcomes, alternatives, non-goals and maintenance capacity.

Map customers/users, employees/candidates, partners, media/community and service stakeholders separately. A single page may serve several audiences only when official identity remains coherent and each audience can find a clear, non-conflicting path.

## 2. Select the surface

| Surface | Proper job | Create/use when | Never use as |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Personal profile | Individual identity, contribution and relationships | A real person participates professionally | Company account or compulsory ad inventory |
| Main company page | Verified organizational reference and activity hub | Stakeholders need a maintained corporate surface | Substitute for employee voice or owned infrastructure |
| Community/group | Member exchange around a real shared purpose | Dialogue and moderation resources exist | Broadcast list or extraction pool |
| Career/employer surface | Employer proposition and job discovery | Recurring hiring and candidate value justify it | Culture theater or discriminatory filter |
| Showcase/sub-page | Distinct durable audience and content stream | The proliferation gate passes | Temporary landing page without ongoing value |
| Related page | Separate entity inside a visible relationship | Independent identity and governance are necessary | Workaround for unclear architecture |

## 3. Establish identity, access and continuity

Verify organization, domain, legal/brand relationship, name and accountable owner. Separate administration, publishing, recruiting, advertising, billing, analytics and technical-tag permissions using least privilege. Maintain two authorized organizational stewards, an access register, recovery test, periodic recertification and immediate offboarding.

## 4. Govern message and content

Maintain truthful company facts, positioning, proof, disclosures, language/locale, accessible visuals and a secure audience-relevant CTA. Assign claim, publishing, correction, response and deprecation ownership. Content cadence follows value and response capacity.

Use this flow where individuals choose to interpret organizational material:

`verified organizational evidence/event → official page source → voluntary role-specific interpretation → audience dialogue → learning returned to the organization`

The official page owns company facts and formal corrections; individuals own their commentary and must not be made to copy it. Log derivatives of material claims so a correction or withdrawal can propagate to known posts, ads, pages, sales assets and event materials.

## 5. Protect employee autonomy

Personal-account participation is voluntary unless it is an explicit lawful job responsibility agreed in advance. Provide paid capacity, evidence/claim guidance, training and safe refusal. Preserve individual voice. Prohibit prescribed profiles, identical photos, prewritten personal posts, quotas, points, rewards for engagement and coordinated reactions.

## 6. Control distribution and data

Before paid or matched-audience activity, document objective, lawful audience basis, exclusions/fairness, creative, landing experience, consent, tags/forms, frequency, budget ceiling, attribution, retention, security and stop rules. Feature availability never proves necessity or effectiveness.

## 7. Route response and complaints

Assign owners and response standards for questions, service issues, misinformation, privacy requests, job/candidate issues and high-severity complaints. Preserve evidence, correct publicly where appropriate and escalate legal/safety issues without retaliating against criticism or whistleblowing.

## 8. Govern the page portfolio

A new sub-page requires a distinct durable audience, unique decision/value, recurring content, owner, response capacity, maintenance budget, clearer architecture than alternatives, measurable outcome and retirement plan. Otherwise use the main page, a post, website path or campaign landing page.

Maintain an owned-channel fallback for page access loss, feature retirement or platform outage: authoritative website destination, contact/support route, content/evidence archive, administrator recovery path and stakeholder migration plan.

## 9. Measure and decide

Track integrity, audience fit, content usefulness, community/service response, talent outcomes, qualified commercial contribution, total cost, accessibility/privacy/employee burden and learning. Maintain, scale, redesign, consolidate or retire based on evidence—not followers or engagement alone.
