# Content-based networking

Content-based networking invites relevant practitioners, customers or experts to help create a genuinely useful asset or conversation. The asset must have independent audience value; participation is neither an endorsement nor sales consent.

## 1. Contract the asset

Define a real audience question, why multiple perspectives improve the answer, intended format, organizer, commercial purpose, evidence standard, accessibility, distribution, maintenance and success criteria.

## 2. Select contributors responsibly

Select for relevant experience, evidence and perspective diversity—not follower count or engagement rank. Record material commercial relationships, conflicts, missing perspectives and whose absence could bias the result.

## 3. Invite transparently

Disclose organizer, purpose, audience, workload, deadline, editing, biography/metric/link use, recording, reuse, commercial context and whether contributor review is available. Participation, promotion and future contact are separate optional choices.

## 4. Ask bounded questions

Ask questions contributors can answer from experience or evidence. Do not use flattery, prestige or “expert inclusion” to manufacture obligation. Never disguise prospecting, market research or lead generation as editorial interest.

## 5. Edit and verify

Preserve meaning, fact-check claims, distinguish experience from general evidence, include disagreement and limitations, and return material for review when an edit could change meaning. Obtain permission for quotations, biography, image, metrics and reuse.

## 6. Credit without implying endorsement

Make authorship and organizer/editor roles clear. Do not imply that contributors endorse the organizer, product, each other or every claim in the asset.

## 7. Publish and distribute ethically

Produce an accessible useful artifact. Contributors are not required to promote it, coordinate engagement or provide early ranking signals. No pods, reciprocal commenting or coerced employee advocacy.

## 8. Continue relationships by evidence

Measure audience usefulness, contributor experience, perspective coverage, qualified voluntary dialogue, learning, corrections and complaints. A contribution does not authorize a pitch. Any commercial next step follows the normal relationship and permission sequence.
