# LinkedIn message decision and progression

Use before initiating or advancing a LinkedIn conversation. The first message opens a possible dialogue; it does not perform discovery, qualification and closing at once.

## 1. Decide whether to message

Confirm legitimate person/account fit, a truthful contact context, non-sensitive evidence and a plausible recipient benefit. Separate observable facts from inferred need. Choose comment, connection request, message, another permitted channel or no action based on relationship and recipient preference.

Do not message when relevance depends on health, trauma, psychedelic interest or another sensitive inference; when the claimed referral was not authorized; when the contact is suppressed; or when the only objective is meeting an activity quota.

## 2. Select the opening

| Situation | Appropriate basis | Required evidence | Main risk |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Authorized referral | Name the person and shared purpose | Referrer approved the introduction | Borrowed trust without permission |
| Public business event | Ask whether it makes the topic relevant | Current professional source | Treating change as confirmed pain |
| Content interaction | Discuss the idea substantively | Real interaction/content | Inferring purchase intent |
| Comparable result | Offer bounded relevant proof | Provenance, scope, timeframe and comparability | False typicality or causal inflation |
| Expert contribution | Explain the real project and use | Genuine output and data-use clarity | Disguised research or ego bait |
| Resource/audit | Offer without obligation | Honest commercial purpose and delivery terms | Reciprocity pressure or hidden capture |
| Direct offer | State fit hypothesis and invite correction | Strong fit evidence and modest claim | Premature pitch |

## 3. Compose the minimum viable message

Use only the elements required:

1. verified context;
2. relevance hypothesis stated without diagnosis or certainty;
3. bounded value or proof, if useful;
4. one small optional next step;
5. an easy exit.

Personalization must be material, accurate and non-invasive. Templates are prompts, never evidence that research occurred.

Automatically reject messages or sequences that induce pain, assert false consensus, use generic ego strokes, build micro-commitment ladders, force binary choices, neutralize objections, create reciprocal debt or append a referral/alternate ask after refusal. Review high-pressure scripts with the [sales-script autonomy audit](../../templates/sales-script-autonomy-audit.md).

## 4. Choose an accessible format

Default to concise text unless the relationship or recipient has indicated another preference. Audio and video require context, a text alternative or captions/transcript, low-bandwidth access and no reliance on appearance, accent or production quality as credibility cues.

## 5. Advance only on evidence

| Stage | Purpose | Advancement evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Contact | Test whether dialogue is welcome and relevant | Substantive reply or explicit permission |
| Clarification | Understand context without free diagnosis | Confirmed problem/goal, role and interest |
| Qualification | Test fit, authority, timing and constraints | Mutual recognition that further exploration is worthwhile |
| Handoff | Move to a richer channel if helpful | Recipient agrees to purpose, channel and next step |
| Opportunity | Conduct discovery and commercial process | Documented fit and buyer evidence in the pipeline |

A reply is not automatically qualification; a meeting is not automatically an opportunity.

## 6. Govern follow-up

Nonresponse reduces the permission budget. Rewriting the same ask, changing text to audio/video or moving to email/phone does not create new consent. Use one coordinated cross-channel record and a predefined follow-up ceiling. Stop on refusal, wrong fit, channel preference, suppression, sensitive context or exhausted sequence.

## 7. Learn from outcomes

Track delivery, meaningful reply, disqualification reason, qualified progression, time to next step, assisted pipeline, customer value, time cost, complaints/blocks and suppression. Test message hypotheses against a baseline; never generalize from a selected large win.
