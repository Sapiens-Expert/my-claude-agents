# Nervous-system-first

A product and coaching hypothesis that regulation, recovery, and perceived capacity often need attention before complex behavior plans can be executed reliably.

Use as a mechanism or design lens, not as a universal causal claim or proven market category. “Nervous system” must not become a vague substitute for stress, trauma, emotion, or disease.
