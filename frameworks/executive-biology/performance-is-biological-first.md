# Performance is biological first

Sustained executive performance is constrained by biological capacity. Sleep, recovery, stress load, health, and behavior shape decision quality and output. The principle corrects the misattribution that every performance problem is primarily motivational or strategic.

It does not mean biology is the only cause, nor does it authorize medical diagnosis.
