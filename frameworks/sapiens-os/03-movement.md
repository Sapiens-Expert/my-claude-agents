# 03 — Movement

Role: strength, aerobic capacity, mobility, mood, cognition, and long-term function. Applied focus: reduce sedentary time and build a tolerable mix of daily movement, resistance, aerobic work, and mobility suited to capacity and medical context. For pain or arthritis, use graded, function-led activity with symptom-response and referral gates; do not diagnose from posture or promise pain-free correction.
