# SapiensOS curriculum map

## Foundations

| Domain | Learning job | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sleep | Understand schedule, duration/quality, circadian cues, stimulants, and practical routine experiments | Persistent sleep problems, suspected disorders, medication changes, and safety risks require qualified care |
| Nutrition | Notice patterns affecting energy and routine; design feasible food/environment behaviors | No individualized medical nutrition, disease treatment, restrictive protocol, or supplement prescription outside scope |
| Movement | Build sustainable aerobic, strength, mobility, and everyday-movement behaviors appropriate to capacity | Medical clearance and qualified programming where symptoms, injury, disease, pregnancy, or risk requires it |

## Regulation and context

| Domain | Learning job | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stress | Map demands, recovery, appraisal, triggers, and coping behaviors | Do not reduce trauma, anxiety, depression, burnout, or workplace harm to cortisol or self-regulation |
| Social connection | Examine support, belonging, boundaries, repair, and relationship behaviors | Coaching is not couples therapy, trauma treatment, or crisis support |
| Light/dark | Understand circadian timing and shape feasible environmental cues | Avoid rigid exposure prescriptions and medical-device claims without individual risk review |
| Agency | Create small choices, feedback, and self-trust without blame | Structural constraints and illness are not failures of agency |

## Coherence

| Domain | Learning job | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meaning | Clarify values, roles, contribution, and trade-offs | No promise that purpose protects against illness or resolves clinical symptoms |
| Rest | Distinguish sleep, pause, leisure, recovery, and demand reduction | Rest advice must not individualize organizational overload or unsafe conditions |
| Curiosity | Use questions, novelty, learning, and perspective-taking to expand behavioral options | Neuroscience claims about curiosity require primary verification; curiosity does not replace treatment |

## Delivery rule

Begin from the client’s goal, context, safety, evidence, and limiting constraint. Select the minimum useful experiment, define what would count as benefit or harm, and review rather than prescribing all ten domains.
