# Evidence-preserving message

Use after audience, purpose, claim and legitimate action are defined.

## 1. Pass validity and safety

Verify claims, evidence class, date, scope, uncertainty, privacy, authority, risk, alternatives, consent and domain-specific requirements. Stop before rhetorical optimization if a material issue remains.

## 2. Extract the core

Write one accurate sentence the audience should understand or use. Specify what can be omitted from the opening and what must remain salient.

## 3. Diagnose the communication job

Identify which stage is failing: attention, comprehension/recall, warranted belief, care or action. Select only the message tools that address that stage.

## 4. Apply the six lenses selectively

Test simplicity, relevant unexpectedness, concreteness, inspectable credibility, respectful emotion and truthful story. Do not force all six.

## 5. Build the evidence layers

Add concrete explanation, evidence, boundary and proportionate action. Preserve denominator, comparison, uncertainty and non-fit. Link deeper evidence without hiding decision-critical limitations.

## 6. Test novice reconstruction

Ask representative readers to paraphrase the core, applicability, evidence class, key limitation and next step. Record misunderstandings rather than prompting the desired answer.

## 7. Audit influence and story risk

Run the persuasion-cue audit when credibility, emotion, identity or stories may pressure choice. Verify story provenance, consent, typicality and causal language.

## 8. Measure and learn

Predefine attention, comprehension, delayed recall, attribution, belief, behavior, outcome and harm measures. Review mutation in retelling. Revise or retire when accuracy degrades.

When voluntary sharing or word of mouth is material to the objective, apply the [responsible transmission audit](responsible-transmission-audit.md). Use transmission lenses selectively and require plot-stripping, privacy/dignity review, faithful retelling and correction reach; virality is not the outcome.

## Output contract

Complete the [layered message brief](../../templates/layered-message-brief.md) and link claim evidence, source/story provenance and publication owner.
