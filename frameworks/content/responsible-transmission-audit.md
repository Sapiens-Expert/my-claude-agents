# Responsible transmission audit

Use when accurate recall, sharing or word of mouth materially contributes to the objective. Optimize useful transmission—not virality itself.

## 1. Contract the outcome

Define the audience, reason sharing would serve them or others, intended downstream outcome, evidence boundary, responsible owner, distribution seed, owned destination, decay/update rule and correction path.

## 2. Apply STEPPS selectively

| Lens | Constructive design | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Social currency | Help people share useful mastery, contribution or verified discovery | No fabricated exclusivity, status anxiety, vulnerable-user games or undisclosed promotion |
| Triggers | Connect the idea to a naturally recurring cue near the relevant decision/behavior | Record context, frequency and competing associations; do not hijack trauma or health anxiety |
| Emotion | Make real consequence human and comprehensible | Evidence precedes arousal; do not optimize anger, fear, awe or shame at the expense of judgment |
| Public | Make useful behavior/artifacts voluntarily observable | Private adoption may be preferable; never expose health, psychedelic, coaching, employment or client status |
| Practical value | Give accurate, scoped, dated and actionable help | Show assumptions, total cost and restrictions; do not require disproportionate purchase or data surrender |
| Stories | Carry the idea through a memorable narrative | Preserve claim, causal logic, source/brand linkage and safe action; secure consent and disclose typicality |

The lenses overlap and are neither necessary nor sufficient. Use only those that fit the audience, claim and risk.

## 3. Trigger map

Record cue, audience/context, natural frequency, intended recall/action, competing associations, privacy risk and expiry. Prefer a durable, relevant connection over an attention-grabbing but unrelated mnemonic.

## 4. Plot-stripping test

Remove the entertaining plot details and ask whether a receiver can still reconstruct:

- the core claim and scope;
- the mechanism or causal boundary;
- the source/brand responsible for it;
- the important limitation or non-fit;
- the appropriate next action.

If the story travels while these disappear, redesign it. Retelling must not strengthen certainty or turn one case into prevalence.

## 5. Misinformation and dignity gate

Check source credibility, privacy, consent, vulnerable audiences, stigmatizing identity signals, polarization, harmful imitation and correction latency. Pre-bunk predictable misunderstanding where proportionate. Never make a dangerous behavior more observable merely to warn against it.

## 6. Measure the outcome chain

`qualified exposure -> accurate recall -> correct source/brand attribution -> faithful transmission -> appropriate action -> audience/customer benefit`

Track misinterpretation, complaint, polarization, privacy exposure, unsafe behavior and correction reach as veto-capable guardrails. Shares and reach are intermediate signals; they do not establish belief, trust, revenue or welfare.

## 7. Decide

Preserve, revise, reseed, narrow, correct or stop according to accuracy, useful outcome, cost and harm. A low-sharing but high-benefit resource may be successful; high transmission with degraded truth is failure.
