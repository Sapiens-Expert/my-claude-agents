# Claim-to-proof copy

Use for direct-response, landing-page, offer, campaign and sales copy. Make legitimate value easy to understand and act on while preserving evidence, material detail and an easy no.

Begin with the [copy fact pack](../../templates/copy-fact-pack.md). Copy is governed by five interacting controls: human context, evidence, measurement, compliance/safety and named accountability. No apparent response gain may trade away the other controls.

## Copy architecture

1. **Audience situation:** the actual decision, context and current alternative.
2. **Verified problem/opportunity:** bounded significance, frequency/severity and uncertainty without amplification.
3. **Desired outcome:** immediate and downstream progress in the audience's language.
4. **Mechanism:** what the offer does, how it may help and which assumptions connect action to outcome.
5. **Benefits:** link features to relevant outcomes without inventing remote life transformation.
6. **Proof:** attach each material claim to the appropriate demonstration, data, credential, customer evidence or source.
7. **Boundary:** limitations, uncertainty, conditions, non-fit, risks and conflicts.
8. **Offer:** scope, delivery, total price/renewal, data/privacy and remedy.
9. **Options:** credible alternatives, referral, pause or no-action where relevant.
10. **Action:** a clear, accessible next step with easy refusal, reversal or cancellation.

Specificity improves copy only when it is true and decision-relevant. Visual polish, length, testimonial volume or numerical detail cannot substitute for valid proof.

## Benefit-ladder test

For every important feature, map:

`feature -> immediate function -> customer progress -> evidence -> conditions/limits`

Stop the ladder where evidence stops. Do not jump from a product characteristic to wealth, health, identity, status, love, safety or transformation without substantiation.

## Voluntary clarity path

1. **Orient:** identify source and relevance without bait-and-switch.
2. **Explain:** state offer, outcome and mechanism in plain language.
3. **Substantiate:** provide representative proof, specificity and material conditions.
4. **Answer:** address objections and alternatives honestly; change the offer when an objection exposes a real defect.
5. **Summarize:** restate value, price, terms, risks and next steps.
6. **Enable choice:** support learn, compare, contact, defer, decline or buy without obstruction.

Every section earns continued attention through useful decision information. Curiosity may delay minor narrative details, never price, identity, sponsorship, risk, eligibility, evidence quality or material terms.

## Seven-component audit

| Component | Required test |
| --- | --- |
| Entry | Headline, subject, image and caption signal genuine relevance and accurate expectations |
| Explanation | Product, use, mechanism, specifications and novel features are understandable |
| Evidence | Demonstrations, comparisons, testimonials, credentials and numbers are representative, sourced and current |
| Objections | Material doubts are surfaced, answered with evidence and preserved when valid |
| Offer | Price, inclusions, service, remedy, delivery, renewal, cancellation and total commitment are clear |
| Navigation/action | People can scan, obtain detail, act, defer or exit accessibly |
| Voice/flow | Sequence is coherent and human without false intimacy, discriminatory assumptions or pressure |

## Format router

| Format family | Durable design question | Volatile layer to reverify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Print/direct mail/catalog/PDF | Can people scan, understand, retain and respond with all terms available? | Postal economics, response devices, print conventions and benchmarks |
| PR | Is it genuinely newsworthy, attributable and distinguishable from promotion? | Outlet format, pitching/contact practice and distribution services |
| Audio/video | Does it work in time, demonstrate clearly and remain accessible through alternatives? | Duration, placement, production specifications and platform norms |
| Website/landing page | Does each page support one user task in a coherent decision journey? | Search, browser/device and conversion conventions |
| Email | Is contact permissioned, recognizable, independently valuable and easy to stop? | Deliverability, subject-line practice, privacy and provider policy |
| Online ads/social | Are targeting and sponsorship appropriate and is the placement claim sufficient? | Formats, algorithms, targeting controls and policies |
| Content marketing | Does it deliver independent informational value and disclose commercial interest? | Distribution, ranking and lead-capture tactics |

Revalidate the volatile layer using current primary or qualified sources. Medium constraints may change expression, never the evidence or material decision contract.

## Four release gates

### 1. Validity

Are the problem, promise, comparison, number, testimonial, credential, price and constraint accurate, current, traceable and appropriately scoped?

### 2. Decision quality

Can the intended person understand fit, mechanism, total commitment, limitations, alternatives and likely effort? Does the hierarchy support comprehension rather than overwhelm with proof theatre?

### 3. Voluntariness

Are urgency, authority, proof and friction truthful and non-coercive? Can a person decline, pause, unsubscribe, reverse or seek remedy without penalty beyond clearly agreed terms?

### 4. Delivered performance

Does the copy improve qualified action and delivered customer outcomes—not merely clicks or sales? Monitor cancellation, refund, complaint, regret, support burden, harm and longer-term trust.

## Direct-response experiment

Define audience, decision, value hypothesis, tested element/mechanism, baseline/comparison, primary measure, comprehension/consent/outcome guardrails, minimum meaningful effect, sample/duration, attribution window and stop rule. Keep material variables stable or document the design. Preserve null and negative results.

Do not target inferred vulnerability or protected/sensitive traits. Stop tests that increase conversion by degrading comprehension, voluntariness or welfare.

## Mandatory persuasion review

Run the [ethical influence review](../consulting/ethical-influence-review.md) for fear, identity/status, authority transfer, social proof, scarcity, reciprocity, commitment or belonging. Editorial camouflage, survey-selling hybrids, fake consensus, fabricated scarcity and borrowed authority are prohibited.
