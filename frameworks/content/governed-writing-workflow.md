# Governed writing workflow

Use for consequential long-form, campaign, sales, executive or knowledge-base writing. It converts the Writing GPS idea into an evidence-, accessibility- and AI-aware operating skill.

## Go — purpose, reader and evidence

1. **Goal:** define the audience decision/job, useful outcome, business role, accountable owner and primary/guardrail measures.
2. **Why it matters:** specify the reader's context, question, burden, alternatives and reason to spend attention now.
3. **Claim map:** classify factual, causal, comparative, predictive and action claims; attach appropriate evidence, counterevidence, confidence and conflicts.
4. **One sentence and structure:** write the controlling takeaway, then order the material around the reader's task and risk.

Before drafting, route health, legal, financial, safety, identity, reputation, privacy, third-party and other high-consequence material to the required review path.

## Push — think, separate and revise

5. **Thinking draft:** draft freely; mark missing evidence and never treat fluency as publication readiness.
6. **Distance:** use a proportionate cooling interval or an independent reader. Urgency does not remove factual or safety review.
7. **Logic draft:** delete the running start, repair reasoning, remove redundancy and align each section with the controlling takeaway.
8. **Reader draft:** test comprehension, relevance, effort, language, culture, emotional effect and accessibility without claiming to inhabit the reader's experience.
9. **Voice draft:** add recognizable style, specificity, analogy, warmth or humor only where they preserve dignity, evidence and context.
10. **Promise:** make the title, opening and preview clear, accurate and independently understandable. Do not create curiosity debt or risk inflation.

For high-consideration copy, review the whole decision path after a cooling interval. Sentence-to-sentence flow is useful only when each transition adds honest information; “cannot stop reading” is not a quality objective.

## Shine — verify, publish and learn

11. **Automated review:** use spelling, grammar, readability or AI tools as fallible suggestions. Protect data and record material assistance.
12. **Human review:** assign developmental, line, copy/fact, accessibility and specialist checks proportionate to risk; one editorial owner resolves conflicts.
13. **Read aloud:** find ambiguity, cadence, pronunciation and cognitive-load problems.
14. **Scan/render:** verify hierarchy, progressive disclosure, links, contrast, alt text, captions, devices and the final rendered artifact.
15. **Publish and guide:** fulfill the promise before a proportionate CTA; preserve an easy no, alternative and safe referral where relevant.
16. **Version:** freeze the released artifact while retaining correction, update, withdrawal and derivative-propagation capability.
17. **Review:** record outcomes, feedback, errors, corrections, harms, learning and the maintain/reuse/retire decision.

## Voice and tone gate

Voice is the stable expression of values and point of view; tone adapts to audience state, channel, relationship and risk. Define observable language behaviors, not adjectives alone. Humor, irreverence and enthusiasm yield to clarity, care and accountability during complaints, crises, illness, loss, addiction, psychological vulnerability and regulated decisions.

Symbolic or archetypal patterns may generate story options only through the [meaning-pattern creative lens](../branding/meaning-pattern-creative-lens.md). Preserve audience agency and prohibit stereotypes, cultural appropriation, savior roles, miracle transformation and inferred personality types.

## AI authorship gate

AI may support ideation, structure, variants, transcription and mechanical review. The accountable human must:

- use an approved tool and minimum authorized context;
- verify every material fact, source, quotation and inference;
- prevent fabricated lived experience, expertise, identity or customer voice;
- check privacy, confidentiality, copyright, bias and accessibility;
- preserve the intended voice rather than accepting homogenized output;
- record material assistance when provenance or disclosure requires it;
- make and approve the final editorial decision.

AI fluency, repetition across outputs and apparent citation detail never upgrade evidence.

## Release conditions

Release only when the content is useful, accurate, appropriately evidenced, original or properly attributed, readable, accessible, within authority, privacy/rights cleared and paired with a correction owner. Style cannot compensate for weak substance, and “done” cannot override unresolved material risk.
