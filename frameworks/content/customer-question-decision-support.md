# Customer-question decision support

Use this framework to turn recurring audience and buyer questions into accurate, reusable decision support across content, sales and service.

## 1. Register the real question

Capture the person's wording, underlying job or concern, audience, decision stage, frequency, consequence, evidence available, accountable expert, risk class, current answer and review date. Sources may include interviews, comments, consented messages, sales/service conversations, search data, lost decisions and product use.

Prioritize customer importance, materiality, underserved need, answerability and risk—not search volume or closing potential alone.

## 2. Classify the decision need

| Question type | Minimum useful answer | Integrity control |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cost/price | Model or range, inclusions, drivers, ongoing cost, alternatives and date | No teaser range, hidden mandatory charge or false precision |
| Problems/drawbacks | Failure modes, severity/likelihood where known, mitigation and poor fit | Do not minimize harm or use candor as reverse psychology |
| Comparisons | Symmetric criteria, use cases, tradeoffs, sources and uncertainty | Apply the same standard to own and competing options; disclose interest |
| Reviews | Method, period, sample, verification, positives, negatives and conflicts | No fabrication, suppression, deceptive selection or implied typicality |
| Best options | Defined category, criteria, considered set, date and exclusions | No self-award, undisclosed placement or universal “best” claim |

Add safety, scope, process, effort, privacy, evidence, alternatives and referral questions when the decision requires them.

## 3. Build the canonical answer

Every material answer states:

1. the direct answer, including what is not known;
2. scope, audience and definitions;
3. factors, ranges and decision criteria;
4. sources, evidence date and confidence;
5. tradeoffs, drawbacks, alternatives and non-fit;
6. commercial interests and other conflicts;
7. proportionate next step or referral;
8. owner, review date and correction path.

Internal expertise can supply practice evidence, but it cannot replace appropriate scientific, clinical, legal, financial or market authority.

## 4. Publish and adapt

Maintain one governed answer and adapt it into articles, posts, video, audio, FAQs, tools, email or sales aids without changing the claim. Preserve authorship, evidence, accessibility, privacy, disclosures and update state. Search, social and AI discovery are distribution surfaces, not authorities.

## 5. Use before or during a conversation

- Explain why the resource is relevant and its time/effort.
- Send the smallest useful set.
- Offer text, captions/transcript, accessible formats or live explanation.
- Invite disagreement and unresolved questions.
- Never treat consumption as proof of commitment, qualification or consent.
- Never infer sensitive intent from covert behavioral tracking.
- Retain discovery for context, constraints, stakeholders and emotion.

Failure to consume assigned material is not grounds for pressure or exclusion.

## 6. Close the learning loop

Record unresolved questions, misunderstandings, missing evidence, delivery feedback and changed circumstances. Measure:

`coverage/freshness -> qualified discovery -> comprehension/use -> conversation/service use -> behavior -> customer/business outcome`

Label attribution strength. Content-influenced pipeline or revenue is not incremental impact.

## Ownership

A content owner maintains the register and lifecycle. Subject experts validate substance. Sales and service return unanswered questions. Research/analytics validates evidence and measurement. Legal, privacy or clinical reviewers enter according to risk. A cross-functional question council prioritizes the system without becoming an approval bottleneck for routine low-risk updates.
