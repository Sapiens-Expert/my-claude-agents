# Context-native value and ask

Use to design and review channel content that preserves one truthful identity while adapting execution to audience context. Giving value never creates a debt to buy.

## 1. Classify the content state

| State | Audience value | Organizational role | Appropriate response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Value contribution | Learn, solve, feel, connect or participate | Earn attention, understanding or permission | Benefit, save, reply, share or opt in voluntarily |
| Invitation | Consider a relevant next step | Test qualified interest | Reply, register, explore or request details |
| Commercial ask | Evaluate a clearly described offer | Purchase, booking, application, donation or renewal | Accept, decline, defer or choose an alternative |
| Relationship care | Receive support, recognition or continued value | Delivery, retention, learning or advocacy | Use, ask, give feedback or refer voluntarily |

Choose the state explicitly. Do not disguise a commercial ask as neutral education or treat prior value as reciprocity leverage.

## 2. Separate invariant, adaptable and controlled layers

| Layer | Contents | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Invariant | Purpose, material claim, evidence, identity, scope, safety and disclosure | Must survive every adaptation |
| Adaptable | Hook, length, composition, pacing, tone, format, interaction and CTA | Adapt to audience job and context |
| Channel-controlled | Ranking, placement, targeting, interface, analytics, moderation and policy | Reverify before execution; record dependence |

Native execution reduces interaction friction. It never permits hidden advertising, false personhood, inaccessible design or removal of material qualifications.

## 3. Audit the content unit

Ask whether it is:

- context-fit and clearly attributable;
- expected/chosen, frequency-controlled and easy to dismiss;
- independently useful without bait-and-switch;
- concise without losing accuracy, nuance or safety;
- coherent with brand behavior and evidence;
- truthful, accessible and consent-respecting;
- lawful and rights-cleared;
- sustainable to produce and moderate.

Trend or cultural references require relevance, knowledge, permission where applicable and sensitivity. Do not join tragedy, crisis or vulnerable-community conversations merely for reach.

## 4. Contract the ask

A commercial ask needs one clear action, accurate offer and eligibility, price/material terms, evidence and limitations, message-to-destination continuity, accessible completion, commercial/sponsor disclosure, privacy and follow-up expectations, easy refusal and relevant alternatives. Never use fabricated scarcity, shame, fear, confusion or obstructed exit.

## 5. Publish and participate

Respond accurately, correct errors, answer material questions, respect nonresponse and route support/safety issues. Define coverage hours and escalation; sustainable service replaces permanent creator availability.

## 6. Learn and stop

Measure qualified reach, comprehension, saves/replies, consented next steps, click quality, conversion, retention, customer outcome, complaints, negative feedback, harms and full production/moderation cost. Engagement is not trust, sentiment or causal sales evidence.

There is no universal contribution-to-ask ratio. Set the portfolio using relationship/permission state, channel intent, decision complexity, offer maturity, recent frequency, fatigue, runway and response evidence. Revise or stop when the work is ignored, attracts the wrong audience, damages trust, requires manipulation or costs more than its customer/business learning value.
