# Provenance-preserving content transformation

Use to adapt, excerpt, summarize, expand, compile or translate a governed source asset without losing meaning, evidence, rights or accountability.

## Transformation record

| Field | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Parent asset | Stable identifier, owner, canonical location, version and date |
| Atomic unit | Claim, method, example, quotation, story, visual or data point |
| Audience job | Teach, answer, compare, demonstrate, reassure, invite or retain |
| Audience/stage | Specific situation and decision need |
| Target surface | Format/channel selected for audience fit and accessibility |
| Operation | Excerpt, summary, adaptation, expansion, compilation or translation |
| Fidelity invariant | Meaning, scope, uncertainty, evidence and attribution that must remain |
| Rights/consent | Copyright, license, client, testimonial, image and participant permission |
| Freshness | Claims, links, prices, people, law, platform and safety details rechecked |
| Outcome | Intended useful response, business role, measure and guardrails |

## Workflow

1. Select a canonical parent with sufficient quality and provenance.
2. Identify the smallest coherent units that can travel without context collapse.
3. Choose an audience job and surface before transforming the material.
4. State the fidelity invariant and prohibited claim changes.
5. Resolve rights, consent, confidentiality and brand separation.
6. Reverify time-sensitive facts and route high-risk claims to the required reviewer.
7. Add channel-native value through framing, navigation, explanation or interaction—not cosmetic duplication.
8. Test headline, preview, excerpt, CTA and metadata against the full asset.
9. Publish with traceable parentage, accountable owner and review/retirement date.
10. Propagate material corrections to every live derivative or retire it.

## Composition and decomposition

- A long asset may become a transcript, article, post, FAQ, clip, email or sales aid when each derivative remains independently useful and accurate.
- Several validated assets may become a guide, course or knowledge page only after conflicts, duplication, currency and evidence levels are reconciled.
- Translation includes cultural and contextual adaptation; it cannot silently strengthen certainty, alter professional scope or erase disclosures.

## Release gate

Do not publish when the derivative:

- cannot be traced to an accountable source;
- removes qualifications needed to interpret a claim;
- distorts a quotation, example or representative case;
- lacks necessary rights or participant consent;
- republishes stale platform, price, legal, medical or scientific detail;
- exposes confidential or identifying information;
- multiplies volume without a distinct audience job;
- uses a stronger CTA or commercial promise than the evidence permits.

## Measurement

Evaluate useful consumption, comprehension, qualified next action, correction burden, rights/privacy incidents, downstream customer value and production/maintenance cost. Reach and asset count are diagnostic only.
