# Referral boundaries

## Stay within coaching only when

- the goal is appropriate to the coaching contract;
- the client can participate, choose, and use coaching without material destabilization;
- risk, symptoms, impairment, and medical/clinical needs do not require another level of care;
- the coach has relevant competence and supervision; and
- coaching complements rather than delays necessary assessment or treatment.

## Pause and refer

Refer for diagnostic questions, significant or worsening symptoms, trauma processing, severe dissociation, suspected psychosis or mania, eating pathology, substance dependence/withdrawal, abuse/coercive danger, medication decisions, unexplained somatic or cognitive changes, marked functional impairment, or any need beyond competence.

## Escalate urgently

Imminent risk of harm, inability to maintain basic safety, severe disorganization, dangerous intoxication/withdrawal, or a medical emergency requires the applicable local emergency or crisis pathway. Do not attempt extended coaching, interpretation, or unsupported containment first.

## Practice controls

State the limit clearly, avoid diagnosis, document observations and actions, support a warm handoff where possible, maintain privacy, consult supervision within consent/legal limits, and define whether coaching pauses, ends, or resumes alongside care. Referral is a scope decision, not a judgment or failure.
