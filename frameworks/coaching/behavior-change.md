# Coaching behavior change

Translate intention into execution through specific actions, contextual cues, environmental design, self-efficacy, feedback, accountability, and lapse recovery. Match change to readiness and reduce load rather than relying on more information.

## Constraint map

Check whether the limiting factor is clarity, capability/skill, physical or social opportunity, motivation/value, prompt/cue, friction, competing reinforcement, energy/recovery, emotional safety, or structural constraint. Select one primary constraint to test rather than stacking techniques.

## Experiment design

1. Define the smallest meaningful observable action.
2. Attach it to a reliable context or prompt.
3. Reduce avoidable friction and prepare for the likely obstacle.
4. Preserve client choice and make support/accountability explicit.
5. Record a measure and review date.
6. Treat a lapse as data: retain, adapt, replace, pause, or refer.

Habits and identity are possible outcomes/supports, not moral requirements. Do not use streaks, public commitments, loss framing, or social pressure when they increase shame, conceal risk, or impair consent.
