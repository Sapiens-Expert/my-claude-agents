# Diagnostic method

“Diagnostic” refers to a structured coaching assessment, not medical diagnosis.

1. Clarify the performance problem and consequences.
2. Map sleep, energy, recovery, stress, movement, nutrition, environment, and meaning.
3. Identify patterns, constraints, readiness, and uncertainty.
4. Select one or two high-leverage behavioral tests.
5. Define measures, review timing, and referral boundaries.
