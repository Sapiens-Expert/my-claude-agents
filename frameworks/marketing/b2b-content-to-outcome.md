# B2B content-to-outcome architecture

Use to connect educational content and social distribution to qualified customer and business outcomes without reducing marketing to lead volume.

When the content system is itself the venture-learning and monetization engine, govern stage sequencing with the [content-led venture system](content-led-venture-system.md). This framework governs individual asset-to-outcome chains inside it.

## 1. Define the audience decision

Specify the audience, situation, consequential question, current alternative, intended customer progress, commercial role, non-fit and evidence burden. Choose an outcome appropriate to recognition, understanding, evaluation, action or post-purchase value.

For recurring consequential questions, apply the [customer-question decision-support framework](../content/customer-question-decision-support.md). Maintain the buyer's wording, evidence burden, answer owner and freshness rather than treating keyword demand as the question itself.

## 2. Build a useful owned asset

The asset must create value even if the audience never buys. Define promise, format, scope, authority, evidence, limitations, accessibility, localization, update owner, expiry and connection to the wider body of work.

For derivatives, record the parent asset and apply the [provenance-preserving transformation framework](../content/provenance-preserving-transformation.md). Reuse must preserve meaning, uncertainty, attribution, rights, consent and freshness while adding a distinct audience/channel job.

## 3. Design qualified discovery

Compare search, social, direct, partner, community, event, paid and referral distribution. Optimize qualified category reach and correct attribution rather than raw traffic. Record audience overlap, frequency, cost and platform dependence.

## 4. Match the next step to readiness

Offer no-form/self-service, optional subscription, resource, assessment, conversation, demonstration or purchase paths proportionate to intent. State value, data exchange and commercial consequence. Do not force every asset into a lead form or place a conversion CTA on every interaction.

Classify each unit as value contribution, invitation, commercial ask or relationship care through the [context-native value-and-ask framework](../content/context-native-value-and-ask.md). Giving useful content is not a psychological deposit that makes a later purchase obligatory.

## 5. Preserve conversion integrity

Collect only necessary data; separate asset delivery, marketing, tracking and sales permissions; retain privacy, price, terms, alternatives, evidence and exit. Test accessibility, error recovery, device use and language. Gating must not withhold public-interest safety information.

## 6. Qualify and hand off

Define lead/relationship stage, fit evidence, owner, response time, information shown, consent and next action. Marketing and sales share one definition and feedback loop. A form completion is not an opportunity; an opportunity is not delivered value.

Pre-meeting education is optional decision support: explain its relevance and effort, send the minimum useful set, provide accessible/live alternatives and use unresolved questions to improve the canonical answer. Consumption does not prove commitment or fit, and behavioral data does not silently grant permission for sales inference.

## 7. Manage the asset lifecycle

| State | Required action | Decision evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| New | Validate usefulness, evidence, discovery and next step | Qualified comprehension and action |
| Growing | Earn reuse/links, connect pathways and expand distribution | Incremental reach and assisted progression |
| Mature | Maintain accuracy, accessibility, links and conversion paths | Ongoing contribution versus maintenance cost |
| Decaying | Detect stale claims, broken paths and intent/format drift | Error, decline and risk signals |
| Retire/consolidate | Redirect, archive, merge or date clearly | Less duplication/confusion with provenance preserved |

## 8. Measure the full chain

Track inputs/cost; qualified reach; comprehension/use; permission and relationship quality; qualified demand; pipeline; customer outcome/retention; contribution margin; brand/reputation; complaints/privacy/accessibility and opportunity cost.

Use an attribution-confidence ladder:

1. platform-reported exposure;
2. observed touch;
3. self-reported influence;
4. multi-touch contribution;
5. controlled incremental estimate;
6. replicated economic effect.

Do not upgrade a lower rung into causal ROI. Predefine hypothesis, population, baseline, primary/guardrail measures, stopping rule and confounders.

## 9. Decide

Maintain, improve, redistribute, ungate, narrow, scale, consolidate or retire according to customer value, incrementality, economics, brand contribution and harms—not traffic or leads alone.
