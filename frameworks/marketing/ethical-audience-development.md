# Ethical audience development

Use this framework to choose an initial service population, create a truthful promise and expand discovery without coercion.

## 1. Define the beneficial change

State the diagnosed problem, intended customer change, mechanism, evidence, non-fit, risks, commercial interest and who may bear costs. Ethical intent does not establish beneficial outcomes.

## 2. Choose the initial service population

Define shared need, situation, readiness, accessibility and viable economics—not a demographic stereotype. Test whether the group is large enough to sustain learning and delivery. State legitimate exclusions and the evidence for them.

## 3. Map worldview and choice context

Research actual language, alternatives, category entry points, status or affiliation considerations, trust requirements and barriers. Treat empathy maps and motive interpretations as hypotheses.

## 4. Build the promise and experience

Specify outcome, scope, mechanism, proof, effort, price, limitations, alternatives and next step. Align delivery before promotion. Use symbols and language that aid recognition without false implication.

## 5. Design permission

Define the value exchange, consent source, purpose, data minimum, channel, frequency, preference controls, expiry/review and easy withdrawal. Separate marketing permission from service, coaching or community participation.

## 6. Enable discovery and qualified spread

Combine public brand building, permission-based follow-up, referrals and accessible action paths. Use early adopters for learning. After value is validated, broaden qualified reach and entry-point coverage rather than overfitting to insiders.

## 7. Review tension, status and belonging

Remove manufactured fear, shame, scarcity, urgency, inadequacy and identity pressure. Preserve informed refusal, dissent, nonparticipation and exit.

## 8. Measure and decide

Separate brand and direct-response measures. Track qualified reach, permission quality, comprehension, enrollment, delivery, outcomes, complaints, regret, unsubscribe, retention/referral, full economics and harms. Expand, revise, narrow or stop using predefined thresholds.

## Output contract

Complete an [audience and permission brief](../../templates/audience-and-permission-brief.md) linked to product-validation evidence and the governing strategy.
