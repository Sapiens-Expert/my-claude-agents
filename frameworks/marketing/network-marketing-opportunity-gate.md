# Network-marketing opportunity gate

Apply before any VladOS brand evaluates, promotes or communicates a network-marketing or recruitment-dependent commercial opportunity. Incomplete evidence results in no promotion.

## Required diligence

1. Verify legal entity, ownership, jurisdictions, licenses and regulatory/enforcement history.
2. Establish retail customer demand and repeat value independent of participant recruitment.
3. Map compensation, qualification, rank, recruitment incentives and whether economics depend materially on new entrants.
4. Obtain representative gross and net participant outcomes with denominator, time period, attrition and zero/negative-result distribution.
5. Include inventory, subscription, samples, travel, events, training, tools, advertising, tax and time costs.
6. Review cancellation, refund, inventory buyback, disputes, data use and account termination.
7. Verify product composition, evidence, labeling, contraindications, adverse effects and jurisdiction-specific claims.
8. Disclose promoter compensation, conflicts, testimonials, atypical results and platform/channel rules.
9. Review vulnerable audiences, relationship pressure, privacy, reputation and independent alternatives.
10. Reject if evidence is withheld, health/income claims are unsubstantiated, retail demand is weak or economics rely materially on recruitment.

## Communication standard

State commercial/recruitment intent early. Present no-change and alternative options. Never amplify pain, promise lifestyle/income/health outcomes, extract preliminary commitments, use friendship or milestones as funnel camouflage, or request referrals after refusal. A no ends the attempt.
