# Brand operating review

Run quarterly or before a material brand, portfolio, program or platform change.

## 1. Reconfirm the strategic contract

State the current business challenge, brand role, intended identity, active position, priority audiences and decision authority. Identify material changes since the previous review.

## 2. Review layered evidence

Examine memory/meaning, market behavior, strategic option value, economics and integrity/harm separately. Note baselines, category/size effects, confidence and missing evidence.

## 3. Test promise and execution

Compare external claims and identity commitments with product, service, content, sales, partner and leadership behavior. Correct delivery before amplification.

## 4. Diagnose relevance threats

Use the [relevance diagnostic](relevance-diagnostic.md) to distinguish category selection, brand retrieval, qualification, conditional preference, action availability, delivery and economics. Do not prescribe an energizer until the failure stage and cause are established.

## 5. Review programs and participation

For customer-interest, community, digital or partnership programs, test participant value, legitimate fit, consent/privacy, correct attribution, incremental qualified reach, behavior, cost, accessibility and harm. Continue only with evidence.

## 6. Apply the change gate

Separate strategic, audience, legal, ethical and delivery needs from executive boredom. Compare preserve, evolve, migrate and retire options. Protect useful distinctive assets and define tests and reversal rules.

For a new or materially changed expression, route the concept through [brand-concept validation](brand-concept-validation.md) before rollout. Preserve institutional memory and explicitly classify protected versus adaptable elements.

## 7. Audit internal and cross-silo delivery

Check role-specific learning, leadership credibility, workflow/incentive alignment, escalation, shared evidence and local adaptation. Surface unresolved ownership and domain-boundary conflicts.

## 8. Review portfolio and resources

Confirm roles, scope, investment, dependencies and sunset triggers in the portfolio register. Stop unfunded or duplicative activity.

## 9. Decide and assign

Record preserve, repair, test, invest, migrate or stop decisions with owners, thresholds and dates. Link the evidence and decision record.

## Output contract

Complete the [quarterly brand audit](../../templates/quarterly-brand-audit.md). Every red or amber issue must have an owner, action and review trigger.
