# Personal-brand evidence system

Use this framework before writing a profile, biography, speaker page, personal-brand strategy or thought-leadership plan.

## 1. Define the association

State the audience/context, useful association, strategic purpose, relevant alternative and observable evidence that the association is understood. Keep it narrow enough to remember but broad enough to remain truthful.

## 2. Build the six-layer record

Document identity, capability, authority, position, expression and reputation separately. For each entry record source, scope, confidence, review date and nonclaim.

## 3. Reconcile evidence

Flag unsupported aspiration, outdated skills, ambiguous contribution, confidential evidence, conflicting audience perceptions and authority transfer. Decide whether to prove, qualify, remove or investigate each item.

Convert important projects and work samples into [proof-of-work records](../../templates/proof-of-work-record.md). Make the problem, exact contribution, method, artifact, outcome limits, rights and nonclaim inspectable. Select proof for the audience decision rather than displaying everything.

## 4. Design the body of work

Map questions and category entry points to explanation, evidence, practice, point of view, credited community contribution and boundary language. Choose assets and channels based on audience value and maintenance capacity.

## 5. Govern affiliations

Specify personal and organizational claim ownership, voluntary advocacy, confidentiality, asset rights, endorsement architecture and exit portability. Separate sensitive or regulated authority domains visibly.

## 6. Publish and behave consistently

Translate the evidence record into the smallest necessary profile, biography, portfolio, content and interaction changes. Conduct must substantiate the description.

## 7. Measure reputation externally

Track correct unaided/aided association, direct retrieval, qualified invitations/referrals, trust language, independent evidence, misunderstanding, complaints and opportunity quality. Platform activity is a diagnostic only.

## Decision

Continue, strengthen proof, narrow, reposition, separate or stop an association. Record owner, review date and the evidence that would reverse the decision.

Output: complete the [personal-brand evidence ledger](../../templates/personal-brand-evidence-ledger.md) and apply channel-specific changes through the relevant SOP.
