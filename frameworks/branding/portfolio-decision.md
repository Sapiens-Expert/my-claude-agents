# Brand portfolio decision

Use for a proposed brand, subbrand, endorsement, extension, alliance, branded differentiator, consolidation or retirement.

## 1. Contract the decision

State the customer/business problem, governing strategy, decision owner, horizon and implementation authority. Do not use architecture to compensate for weak value or internal politics.

## 2. Build the governed inventory

Record every public brand, offer name, framework, program, differentiator and external partner in the [portfolio register](../../templates/brand-portfolio-register.md). Capture legal ownership, current role, scope, evidence domain, status, cost and dependencies.

## 3. Establish audience and asset evidence

Identify category entry points, audiences, associations, behavioral overlap, distinctive assets, reputation, access, economics and channel/geography overlap. Test whether audiences understand the current hierarchy.

## 4. Assign provisional roles

Specify master, subbrand, descriptor, endorser, differentiator or alliance roles for each offer. Assign portfolio roles—current power, future power, linchpin, silver bullet, flanker, energizer or cash generator—with evidence and review dates.

## 5. Compare architecture options

Include branded house, subbrand, endorsed, independent, alliance, descriptor and no-new-brand options. Evaluate relevance, verified difference, correct energy/attribution, leverage, clarity, synergy, investment and harm.

## 6. Model bidirectional transfer

Map positive and negative associations, claims, authority, data, reputation and failure moving both ways. For extensions, ask whether parent helps extension, extension helps parent, a new brand is necessary and the system can support it.

## 7. Model economics and operations

Estimate establishment and maintenance investment, opportunity cost, cannibalization, access effects, capabilities, governance burden and exit cost. No orphan brands or unfunded promises.

## 8. Test comprehension and behavior

Test recognition, offer qualification, navigation, driver/endorser understanding, correct attribution, trust, choice and harmful confusion with relevant audiences and stakeholders.

## 9. Decide and govern

Approve only when customer choice and strategic execution improve enough to justify complexity. Define prominence, naming, ownership, budget, claims, metrics, review date, migration and sunset conditions.

## 10. Consolidate safely

When reducing the portfolio, use the [consolidation and retirement SOP](../../sops/branding/portfolio-consolidation-and-retirement.md). Preserve customer access, records, commitments, corrections, search equity and an appropriate reversibility window.
