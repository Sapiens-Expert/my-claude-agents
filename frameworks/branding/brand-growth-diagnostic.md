# Brand-growth diagnostic

Use this framework to determine whether growth is constrained by qualified penetration, mental availability, action availability, value/delivery, retention or another factor.

## 1. Define the market and transfer conditions

Set category, buyer unit, geography and observation window. Classify the buying system: repertoire, subscription, contract, episodic, high-risk or capacity constrained. Record eligibility, safety, margin, service and regulatory constraints.

## 2. Decompose growth

Measure buyers, frequency/use, repeat, retention/defection, revenue and contribution. Compare periods and category/size baselines. Inspect acquisition cohorts and preventable service failures without treating every loyalty difference as causal.

## 3. Audit buyer distributions and overlap

Plot the actual contribution curve, buyer moderation across periods and behavioral overlap with alternatives. Test whether assumed heavy-buyer, demographic or competitor segments survive longitudinal evidence.

## 4. Audit mental availability

Map category entry points, their frequency/importance, brands retrieved and the focal brand's coverage. Separate unaided from prompted measures and buyers from nonbuyers.

## 5. Audit action availability

Assess discovery, comprehension, search, channel, scheduling, capacity, price, payment, accessibility, eligibility and next-step friction across relevant situations.

## 6. Audit distinctive assets and communication

Measure asset fame, uniqueness, attribution and confusion. Review incremental qualified reach, continuity, brand linkage, entry-point coverage, creative/proof effects and overexposure or safety risks.

## 7. Select the governing constraint

Compare penetration, retrieval, access, value, proof, delivery, retention and economics diagnoses. Choose the constraint that best explains evidence and changes action; name the strongest alternative and disconfirming observation.

## 8. Design and test interventions

Select coherent actions with owners, baselines, predictions, guardrails and review thresholds. Measure incremental behavior and contribution—not activity or engagement alone. Scale, revise or stop according to evidence.

## Output contract

Produce a [brand-growth evidence brief](../../templates/brand-growth-evidence-brief.md), linked analysis, chosen diagnosis, intervention plan and decision record.
