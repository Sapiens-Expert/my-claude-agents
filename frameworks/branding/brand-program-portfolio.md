# Brand-program portfolio

Use this framework to choose, coordinate and evaluate brand-building programs across channels, markets and VladOS projects.

## 1. Start from the governing system

Link business strategy, brand identity, position, priority category entry points, portfolio role and growth diagnosis. Do not begin with a channel or event.

## 2. Define the program job

Choose one primary job: correct recognition, competence proof, participation, support, relevant energy or enabled action. State the audience and causal chain to customer and business outcomes.

## 3. Generate channel-neutral options

Compare product/service experience, tools, research, diagnostics, workshops, content, PR, partnerships, sponsorships, community, events, distribution and direct response. Include no-program and lower-cost options.

## 4. Screen readiness and integrity

Test identity fit, audience value, proof, attribution, operational capability, accessibility, privacy/consent, safety, reputation, partner dependence, cost and opportunity cost.

## 5. Assign adaptation rights

Mark imperatives, adaptables and discretionaries. Define who may approve local changes and what triggers escalation. Preserve evidence and authority boundaries while enabling culturally and platform-appropriate execution.

## 6. Specify the experiment

Set baseline, population, exposure/participation definition, incremental outcome, guardrails, owner, budget, duration and stop rule. Avoid exposure-equivalent valuations and engagement-only criteria.

## 7. Govern partners and sponsorships

Define roles, prominence, reciprocal value, rights, claims, IP, data, approvals, activation duties, incidents, termination and post-exit use. Model association transfer both ways.

## 8. Review the portfolio

Assess duplication, fatigue, entry-point coverage, reach, attribution, operating load, learning and resource concentration. Maintain, scale, redesign, combine or retire using evidence.

## Output contract

Produce a [brand-program brief](../../templates/brand-program-brief.md) and update the quarterly audit and portfolio register when a partner or program becomes a durable asset.
