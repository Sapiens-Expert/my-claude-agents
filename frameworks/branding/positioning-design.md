# Evidence-led positioning design

Use this framework when the governing diagnosis shows that audience comprehension, retrieval, attribution or comparative meaning is a material constraint.

## 1. Contract and qualify the decision

Define the owner, audience, use/buying situation, geography, horizon and business outcome. State the diagnosed obstacle and why positioning—not product, proof, access, distribution or delivery—is the relevant intervention.

## 2. Map current audience reality

Research unaided category and brand retrieval, alternatives including nonconsumption, existing associations, decision criteria, category language, rejection patterns and unintended interpretations. Separate measured evidence from internal belief.

## 3. Map occupied and available frames

For each credible reference frame, identify the alternatives associated with it, entry cues, expected benefits, proof norms and current owner if any. Treat whitespace as a hypothesis: an “empty” position may be irrelevant or uneconomic.

## 4. Generate positioning options

Develop alternatives using established-category membership, comparative relationship, narrower focus, use occasion, audience, outcome, mechanism or a genuinely useful new category. Include a non-repositioning option.

## 5. Screen and choose

Score each option for truth, relevance, comprehension, distinction, attribution, credibility, demand, economics, reach, accessibility, ethical acceptability, operational fit and future flexibility. Record the primary association and explicit sacrifices.

## 6. Write the internal position

Complete audience/situation, reference frame, progress, mechanism/difference, relevant alternative and substantiated reasons to believe. Translate it into plain audience language without forcing the template into public copy.

## 7. Align delivery and expression

Map required changes across product, offer, experience, price, distribution, name, identity, proof, vocabulary, content, sales, service, people and incentives. Resolve contradictions before increasing repetition.

## 8. Test and govern

Test comprehension, retrieval, attribution, credibility, qualified behavior and harmful interpretations against alternatives and baselines. Set guardrails, decision thresholds, owner and review date. Persist, revise or stop according to evidence.

## Output contract

Produce a completed [positioning brief](../../templates/positioning-brief.md), linked evidence, delivery-alignment actions and a decision record. A slogan alone is not an output.
