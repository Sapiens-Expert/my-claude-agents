# Category and brand relevance diagnostic

Use before prescribing repositioning, category creation, rebranding, reach investment or an “energizer.”

## 1. Define the situation and decision

Set buyer/user, entry situation, geography, category, horizon, outcome and owner. Record the observed decline or opportunity and its baseline.

## 2. Locate the failure stage

Measure separately: category selection, brand retrieval, qualification against required criteria, preference among qualifiers, action availability, delivery and economics/public value. State the strongest competing diagnosis.

## 3. Map emerging criteria

Identify changing problems, alternatives, technologies, regulation, workarounds and screening criteria. Test whether a proposed must-have changes behavior and creates value or is merely novel language.

## 4. Evaluate concept options

Compare maintain, access/delivery repair, parity, reposition/reframe, value leap, category/subcategory design, new/endorsed/acquired brand and exit. For each assess demand, capability, brand range, architecture, economics, timing and public value.

## 5. Test persistence and defensibility

Estimate competitor qualification, substitute response, learning speed and convergence. Review capability, system, ecosystem, IP and brand linkage alongside interoperability, lock-in, competition, privacy and welfare.

## 6. Select the response

Choose the smallest coherent response to the diagnosed stage. Specify non-priorities, owners, dependencies, required investment and evidence that would reverse the choice.

## 7. Experiment and review

Predefine predictions from category selection through behavior and economics. Use staged tests, guardrails and stop rules. Do not infer relevance from attention or prompted enthusiasm.

## Output contract

Complete a [category-relevance thesis](../../templates/category-relevance-thesis.md), linked evidence and decision record.
