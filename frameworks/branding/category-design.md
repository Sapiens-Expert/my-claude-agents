# Evidence-led category design

Use this module only when the governing diagnosis shows that the existing problem–solution frame materially blocks comprehension, adoption or value creation.

## 1. Pass the eligibility gate

State the diagnosed obstacle, evidence and competing explanations. Compare improving the existing category, focused positioning, value reconstruction, creating a new category and doing nothing. Define evidence that would rule category design out.

## 2. Validate the problem

Identify who experiences it, when, severity/frequency, consequences, alternatives, switching barriers and demonstrated commitment. Mark whether the initiative began from observed demand or a capability seeking an application. Both require problem evidence.

## 3. Test category legitimacy

Evaluate behavioral distinction, buyer usefulness, substantive value, comprehensibility, addressable demand, economics, delivery capability, regulatory fit and ethical integrity. Test whether the frame remains useful when the sponsoring brand is removed.

## 4. Design the transition and landscape

Create a fair from/to map and locate established categories, substitutes, nonconsumption and adjacent frames. Define category boundaries, inclusion/exclusion rules, contrary evidence and the proposed name. Test naming for clarity, prior use, search, translation, accessibility and legal risk.

## 5. Write the point of view

Connect problem, stakes, inadequacy of current approaches in defined situations, enabling insight, desired future, category, mechanism, proof, limitations and action. Label unverified claims.

## 6. Align product, company and ecosystem

Specify required roadmap, capabilities, business model, taxonomy, use cases, evidence, vocabulary, sales and service changes. Map ecosystem actors, incentives, dependencies, conflicts, interoperability, governance and reciprocal value.

## 7. Run a bounded activation

Choose a concentrated learning and communication milestone only after readiness gates pass. Define audience, causal hypothesis, measures, resources, owners, risks, rollback and post-event conversion. Preserve principled dissent.

## 8. Measure and decide

Track comprehension, problem recognition, category-language adoption, association, qualified demand, adoption, retention, outcomes, ecosystem participation and reputation. Separate category growth from company performance. Continue, revise, revert to an established frame or stop using predefined thresholds.

## Output contract

Produce a completed [category-design brief](../../templates/category-design-brief.md), supporting research, governing strategy link, operating alignment plan, activation experiment and decision record.
