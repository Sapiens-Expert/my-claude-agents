# Coherent strategy

Use this framework to turn an aspiration, problem or initiative list into a testable strategy.

## 1. Contract the decision

State the decision owner, scope, horizon, stakeholders, outcome and constraints. Identify who can commit resources and stop existing work.

## 2. Build the evidence frame

Collect only evidence needed to distinguish plausible diagnoses: customer behavior, alternatives, economics, capabilities, competitors, environmental change and stakeholder effects. Label facts, interpretations and unknowns.

For material market or portfolio decisions, use the [issue-led market strategy](issue-led-market-strategy.md) to scope external analysis, uncertainties, signposts and allocation alternatives without turning the process into exhaustive planning.

## 3. Write competing diagnoses

Use this form:

> Progress toward **[outcome]** is constrained primarily by **[challenge]**, because **[causal logic]**. We believe this because **[evidence]**. This diagnosis would weaken if **[disconfirming observation]**.

Create at least one credible alternative. Select the diagnosis that best explains the evidence and changes action materially.

## 4. Choose the guiding policy

Complete:

> We will address **[diagnosed challenge]** by **[overall approach]**, concentrating on **[leverage point/audience/capability]**. We will prioritize **[choices]**, will not **[non-priorities]**, and will adapt when **[trigger]**.

Test the policy for value, feasibility, differentiation, likely response, ethics and resilience.

## 5. Design coherent actions

Choose three to seven mutually reinforcing actions. For each record owner, sequence, dependency, resource, expected observation and stop/review rule. Explicitly list work that stops or receives less.

## 6. Stress-test

- strongest competing diagnosis;
- base rate and historical analogue;
- stakeholder harm and distribution;
- competitor/system response;
- weakest link and failure cascade;
- rigidity created by tight fit;
- evidence that would reverse the decision.

## 7. Run the learning cycle

Precommit predictions and thresholds. Review observations against the causal chain, not against activity completion alone. Update diagnosis, policy and actions in that order.

## Output contract

The final strategy must fit on one decision brief while linking to supporting evidence. It includes diagnosis, policy, actions, trade-offs, assumptions, measures, owners and review date.
