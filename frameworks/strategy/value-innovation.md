# Value-innovation design

Use this module only after a strategy diagnosis identifies competitive convergence, commoditization or a demand barrier that market reconstruction could plausibly address.

## 1. Pass the eligibility gate

State the diagnosed obstacle, evidence, competing explanations and why reconstruction is preferable to focus, execution improvement, capability building, direct competition or exit. Stop if the diagnosis does not change action.

## 2. Build the current strategy canvas

Define the buyer/user, use situation and alternatives—including substitutes and non-consumption. Identify factors in buyer language, evidence their importance and offered levels, and mark estimates explicitly.

## 3. Explore boundaries

Search across alternative industries, strategic groups, buyer roles, complements, functional/emotional orientation and time. Record observations rather than brainstorming claims.

## 4. Research noncustomers

Examine reluctant users, active rejecters and people who have never considered the category. Distinguish removable barriers from durable preferences, exclusion, safety concerns and principled refusal.

## 5. Design the ERRC choices

Specify what to eliminate, reduce, raise and create. For each choice record buyer utility, cost, quality, stakeholder effects, evidence and dependencies. Reject additive designs with no resource shift.

## 6. Draft the proposed value curve

Require focus, meaningful divergence and a truthful tagline. Compare it with current alternatives and test whether the curve expresses a coherent guiding policy rather than a feature bundle.

## 7. Pass five gates

Evaluate buyer utility; access and strategic price; target cost and quality; organizational/ecosystem adoption; and public/ethical value. Failure at any gate triggers redesign, a bounded experiment or rejection.

## 8. Pilot and review

State the riskiest assumptions, predictions, guardrails, disconfirming evidence, owner and review date. Pilot before scaling. Monitor imitation, substitutes, cost drift and stakeholder effects.

## Output contract

Produce one [value-innovation brief](../../templates/value-innovation-brief.md) linked to the governing strategy kernel, evidence, financial model, decision record and pilot results.
