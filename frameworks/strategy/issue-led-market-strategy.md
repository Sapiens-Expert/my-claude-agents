# Issue-led market strategy

Use this framework to focus market sensing and resource allocation on a consequential strategic decision.

## 1. Define the issue portfolio

Select three to six issues with decision, alternatives, owner, deadline, impact and disconfirming evidence. Rank them by decision importance, urgency and uncertainty.

## 2. Build only the needed analysis

For each issue, specify which customer, competitor, market, environment and internal evidence can distinguish options. Label fact, interpretation, assumption, forecast and unknown.

## 3. Register strategic uncertainties

Record mechanism, horizon, signposts, impact range, evidence, outside-view base rate, smallest useful information action and trigger. Build only scenarios that change a decision.

## 4. Generate full strategy alternatives

For every option specify product–market investment, customer value proposition, required assets/competencies and functional programs. Include continuation, maintenance, no-entry, waiting, partnering, harvest and exit where credible.

## 5. Select the strategy style

State what is committed, adaptable, opportunistic, experimental and prohibited. Set investment caps, milestones, option-preservation and stop rules.

## 6. Compare growth and allocation choices

Compare core improvement, usage, leverage, new business, geography and acquisition/alliance routes. Decompose every synergy claim into mechanism, owner, cost, dependency and net outcome.

## 7. Decide and implement

Choose a diagnosis, guiding policy and coherent actions. Assign each business/brand/offer a grow, maintain, harvest, consolidate, divest or retire state. Make resource movement and non-priorities visible.

## 8. Monitor and update

Track assumptions, signposts, outcomes, harms and environmental response. Update diagnosis and allocation when thresholds cross; do not maintain a plan because the form is complete.

## Output contract

Produce a [strategic market decision brief](../../templates/strategic-market-decision-brief.md) and linked uncertainty register, strategy kernel and decision record.
