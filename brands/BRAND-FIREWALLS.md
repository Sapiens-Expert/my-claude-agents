# Brand firewalls

## O!Sapiens and Executive Healthspan

Allowed: executive performance, neuroscience translation, sleep, stress physiology, leadership, behavior, healthspan, recovery, decision quality, SapiensOS, and Executive Biology.

Normally excluded: psychedelics, entheogens, ceremonies, altered states, integration, drug policy, and psychedelic CTAs.

Reason: reputational safety and category coherence.

## entheogen.expert

Allowed: psychedelics, integration, mental-health education, behavior change, neuroscience, risk literacy, meaning, identity, expectation, and psychological flexibility.

Do not import automatically: C-suite sales language, Executive Biology category language, corporate-funnel CTAs, or O!Sapiens proof.

## Shared intellectual layer

Neuroscience, behavioral psychology, agency, identity, habit, stress, sleep, meaning, decision-making, evidence literacy, and behavior change may inform both brands. Shared knowledge does not authorize shared public vocabulary or customer journeys.

## Enforcement

Before publishing, select one brand and voice register. If a draft crosses the firewall, either remove the crossover or route it to the correct brand. Exceptions require an explicit active decision.
