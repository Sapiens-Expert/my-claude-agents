## Authority ladder

1. Primary records, certificates, published work, and consented outcome data.
2. Direct professional practice and clearly labeled lived experience.
3. Evidence-calibrated synthesis of peer-reviewed research.
4. AI-generated profile or positioning analysis, usable only as an internal hypothesis.

Public outcome claims and comparisons with clinicians require evidence beyond a profile, testimonial, or AI synthesis.
