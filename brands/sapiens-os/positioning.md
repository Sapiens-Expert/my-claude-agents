# SapiensOS positioning

SapiensOS is a behavior operating system that connects biological signals, human context, and prioritized execution. For the proposed HealthSpan product, the strongest category statement is: prevention still fails at execution.

Do not position it as another dashboard, general AI health assistant, or clinically validated product before evidence exists.
