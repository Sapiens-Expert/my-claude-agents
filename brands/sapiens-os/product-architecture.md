# Product architecture

Candidate loop:

1. Ingest consented user context and relevant signals.
2. Interpret trends within safe, non-medical boundaries.
3. Prioritize the highest-leverage next behavior.
4. Explain the reason in plain language.
5. Capture completion, friction, and subjective response.
6. Adapt recommendations and escalate when coaching or clinical support is appropriate.

Product architecture remains a hypothesis pending prototype and safety validation.
