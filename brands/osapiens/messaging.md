# O!Sapiens messaging

## Core messages

- High output can conceal accumulating biological debt.
- Sleep, recovery, stress physiology, and behavior shape strategic performance.
- More discipline is not always the answer; better diagnosis and system design may be.
- Sustainable performance is measurable, trainable, and context-specific.
- Orientation is more useful than indiscriminate optimization.
- Biology is the mechanism; agency is the intended outcome.

## Message response

Good messaging should help a reader say: “This describes a real pattern,” “there may be a mechanism I can examine,” and “I can take a proportionate next step.” It must not imply that biology fully explains the person, that the reader has been clinically diagnosed, or that coaching replaces healthcare.
