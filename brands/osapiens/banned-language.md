# O!Sapiens banned language

Avoid: cure, heal your disease, reverse aging, guaranteed results, unlimited performance, hack your biology, superhuman, unlock your full potential, optimize everything, detox, trauma release, psychedelic performance, clinical diagnosis, patient, treatment plan.

Also avoid shaming language and vague motivational slogans.

Also banned or restricted:

- “Reset your mental health in 7 days,” “ideal for anyone,” or similar universal/rapid transformation claims.
- “No pills” or any language that stigmatizes medication.
- “The explanation is the intervention” when it could be mistaken for diagnosis or treatment; use as a communication principle only.
- “Biology first” as an absolute explanation of every executive, mental-health, relationship, or performance problem.
- Unverified research-hour counts, institutional logos, participant totals, testimonials, client outcomes, or “primary research” wording.
- “Privacy-first” or “EU-native” as proof of compliance before product controls and review exist.
