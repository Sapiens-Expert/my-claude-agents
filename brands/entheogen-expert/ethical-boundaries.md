# Ethical boundaries

- Education and coaching only within training and law.
- No sourcing, dosing, prescribing, diagnosis, psychotherapy, or emergency care.
- Screen for scope and refer when risk exceeds coaching competence.
- Do not promise healing or transformation.
- Prioritize stabilization, consent, and harm reduction.
- No medication or interaction clearance; refer to qualified prescribers/pharmacists — confirmed directly by Vlad, 2026-08-31, not something he does under any circumstance.
- **No engagement with active suicidal ideation or self-harm risk.** Confirmed directly by Vlad, 2026-08-31: he does not deal with these cases. Refer immediately and end the coaching relationship for the duration of that risk — not something to hold, monitor, or co-manage alongside clinical care. See `../../brands/entheogen-expert/audience-segmentation.md` Segment 9 ("The Wrong Room") for the full disqualification protocol.
- **Facilitation is permitted only within a legal jurisdiction/environment — never underground or illegal, regardless of client request.** Confirmed directly by Vlad, 2026-08-31. This is a conditional allowance, not a blanket exclusion — see `../../projects/entheogen-expert/coaching.md`.
- No touch-based, trauma-processing, crisis, or spiritual-authority role beyond documented competence and lawful scope.
- No sexual/romantic relationships, exploitation, secrecy, induced dependency, or coercive community dynamics.
- Personal psychedelic experience is not professional competence.
- Do not call distress healing, awakening, ego death, or spiritual emergency in place of safety assessment.
