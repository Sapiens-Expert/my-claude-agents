# Writing workflow record

## Go

- Audience, context and decision/job:
- Useful outcome and business role:
- Accountable owner and intended measures:
- Reader question, burden, alternatives and “why now”:
- One-sentence takeaway:
- Claim/evidence/counterevidence map:
- Risk class and required reviewers:
- Reader-task outline:

## Push

- Thinking-draft unknowns and missing inputs:
- Logic/redundancy corrections:
- Reader comprehension, effort and accessibility findings:
- Stable voice behaviors:
- Contextual tone adjustment:
- Title/opening promise and curiosity-debt check:

## Shine

- Automated tools used and material AI assistance:
- Data/privacy/IP restrictions observed:
- Human and specialist reviewers:
- Fact/source/quotation verification:
- Read-aloud and rendered-accessibility findings:
- CTA, easy no, alternative or referral:
- Release version, owner and review date:
- Correction/withdrawal/derivative propagation path:

## Post-publication learning

- Useful outcome and guardrail results:
- Feedback, errors, corrections or harms:
- What changed in the writing system:
- Maintain, adapt, consolidate or retire:
