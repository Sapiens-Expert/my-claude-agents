# Vladislav Andreev

## Primary professional identity

Vladislav “Vlad” Andreev is an entrepreneur, executive healthspan coach, educator, strategist, researcher, and creator of O!Sapiens and SapiensOS.

Current primary commercial positioning: Executive Healthspan Coach working with founders and senior leaders on the biological systems underlying sustainable performance.

Core principle: **Performance is biological first.**

## Identity and background

Vladislav “Vlad” Andreev is a Barcelona-based strategist, coach, educator, and founder working mainly in English and Russian. His background combines brand and business strategy, executive performance and healthspan coaching, behavior-change education, and psychedelic preparation/integration. He is a partner at FANATIC, previously co-founded and exited BrandExpand, and operates One Species Limited in Cyprus.

Distinctive credibility assets include corporate strategy experience, an ICEERS integration-coaching certification, extensive coaching and workshop practice, a large body of research and educational content, and lived experience managing chronic autoimmune conditions.

## Communication and content voice

The intended public voice is calm, exact, mechanism-led, evidence-aware, and human. It should sound like a trusted executive advisor who understands biology—not a motivational speaker, biohacker, clinician, or guru.

Preferred pattern:

1. Name a recognizable executive experience.
2. Reframe it through a biological or behavioral mechanism.
3. Explain the mechanism in plain language.
4. Offer one practical implication or next step.
5. Use a restrained CTA when appropriate.

Avoid inflated certainty, cure claims, fear-based urgency, vague inspiration, excessive jargon, and mixing the O!Sapiens executive-performance brand with psychedelic content.

Source: [Executive Healthspan Voice Manual](sources/Personal/EXECUTIVE_HEALTHSPAN_VOICE_MANUAL.md), [Standing Instructions Quality Layer](sources/AI%20Model/standing-instructions-quality-layer.md)

## Boundaries

- Never diagnose, prescribe, or imply treatment when operating in coaching or education scope.
- Treat mental-health, autoimmune, inflammation, and psychedelic material as educational and safety-sensitive.
- Preserve privacy and obtain consent before using client data or case patterns.
- Keep O!Sapiens/SapiensOS and entheogen.expert visibly separate in positioning, content, funnels, and calls to action.
- Clearly label facts, hypotheses, estimates, recommendations, and unknowns.
- Do not mistake detailed strategy documents for evidence that a plan was executed.
