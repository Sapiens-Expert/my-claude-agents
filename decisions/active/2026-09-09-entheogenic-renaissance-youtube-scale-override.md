# Scale Entheogenic Renaissance's YouTube channel, English-first

## Decision

- Entheogenic Renaissance's YouTube channel moves from `maintain` / secondary / "opportunistic cadence" to an active, sustained production push. Confirmed by Vlad directly, 2026-09-09.
- Primary content language for the YouTube channel becomes **English**, not Russian. Confirmed by Vlad directly, 2026-09-09.
- **Scope is narrow, YouTube only.** This does not change entheogen.expert's brand-wide positioning (`brands/entheogen-expert/positioning.md`: "especially Russian-speaking audiences underserved by nuanced material") or the general project's Russian-language objective (`projects/entheogen-expert/PROJECT.md`) for Telegram, DMs, TikTok, or any other surface.

## Context

Vlad asked for help planning YouTube content for the channel using the VELIZHANIN/Hunt-ladder growth methodology, prompted by a Google Trends pull showing English-language search interest (psychedelics, mental health treatment, psychedelic integration, coaching), and asked whether to rename the channel (currently "Entheogenic Renaissance") toward a personal-brand "coach" positioning.

A channel and keyword audit (vidIQ, connected account `vladislav@onespecies.earth`) found, before any decision was made:

- `projects/entheogenic-renaissance/PROJECT.md` status was `maintain`, priority `secondary`, `not_metrics: [weekly-output-volume]`.
- `projects/entheogen-expert/PROJECT.md` objective names Russian-language explicitly and lists `viral-views` as a non-metric.
- `state/NOW.md` "do not start" list covers new channels without an explicit replacement decision; "Maintain: entheogen.expert content/nurture at bounded effort."

These were surfaced explicitly, with the exact conflicting language quoted, before proceeding — per the standing pattern for live-instruction-vs-guardrail conflicts. Vlad confirmed both the audience-language change and the scale change directly when asked (via structured question, not implied).

## Alternatives

- **Light repurposing test only** (1-2 videos pulled from existing episodes, no status change) — considered, rejected: Vlad explicitly chose "real scaled push" over this when asked directly.
- **Russian-first**, matching the brand's stated positioning — considered, rejected: Vlad explicitly chose English when asked directly, on volume/competition grounds.

## Rationale

Vlad's live instruction is rank 1 in the authority order except the three non-negotiables in `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md` — none of which this touches (no clinical/treatment claims are being approved, no O!Sapiens brand-vocabulary bleed, no MDR issue).

The channel's own data supports the direction, not just the instruction: the best-performing video in the channel's 110-video history ("Unlock Your Psychedelic Journey: Mastering Integration with the MAPS Guide," 575 views, breakout score 95.8 — roughly 5x the channel's next-best) already sits in the "psychedelic integration" lane. vidIQ keyword research (2026-09-09) shows that lane at medium volume (~3,534 est. monthly searches) and low competition (21.3/100) in English — a real, evidenced opportunity already validated by the channel's own history, not a cold guess.

## Consequences

- `projects/entheogenic-renaissance/PROJECT.md` status/priority/metrics updated to reflect active production (this decision).
- `state/NOW.md` "Maintain" section gets a carve-out sentence, mirroring the existing `osapiens_expert` Instagram carve-out pattern (`decisions/active/2026-09-04-osapiens-russian-instagram-reels-channel.md`), pointing here.
- Does **not** touch `projects/entheogen-expert/PROJECT.md`, `brands/entheogen-expert/positioning.md`, or `brands/entheogen-expert/audience.md` — those remain accurate for the brand outside this one channel.

## Evidence and assumptions

**Verified, 2026-09-09, live pulls:**
- Channel stats, video-level performance, and traffic-source/retention data (vidIQ, connected account).
- Keyword volume/competition for "psychedelic coach," "psychedelic integration," "mental health treatment" (vidIQ).
- Competitor/positioning scan of English-language psychedelic-education and integration-coaching channels (vidIQ channel search).

**Still open, not resolved by this decision:**
- **Final channel name/identity.** Vlad's instinct was a "[Name] Psychedelic Coach"-style rename; no channel in the competitive set uses that exact pattern (successful analogs use either a pure personal name — Zoey Arielle, 152K subs — or a distinct brand name — Third Wave, DoubleBlind — with the coaching credential stated in the bio/description, not the title). "Psychedelic coach" itself has ~0 measured search volume. Options were presented; not yet chosen.
- **Existing library hygiene**, unrelated to the name question but surfaced by the same audit:
  - One live video ("You're Not Broken — You're Out of Alignment. Meet O!Sapiens #mentalhealth #lifespan #healthspan") promotes O!Sapiens on this channel, in tension with brand separation even though it doesn't literally violate the one-directional non-negotiable wording.
  - Several existing titles use vocabulary `audience-segmentation.md`'s language bank flags as off-brand ("healing," "medicine," e.g. "Real story of healing from narcissism and HIV... #healing #medicine").
  - One title states "Certified by UC Berkeley!" — an unconfirmed credential per `identity/credentials.md` and `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`, live and public.
  - None of this is fixed by this decision — flagged for cleanup regardless of the scale decision.
- **Targeting clinical-adjacent keywords remains out of scope.** This decision does not approve targeting "mental health treatment" or similar clinical-territory search terms. `audience-segmentation.md`'s Segment 9 ("The Wrong Room") and `ethical-boundaries.md` still apply in full to content targeting on this channel, regardless of the English/reach shift — chasing that keyword space was evaluated and rejected on both strategic and ethical grounds during this same audit.

## Revisit trigger

If the channel name is finalized (update this decision or file a follow-up), if 90 days of English-language production shows no meaningful movement on subscribers/views, or if content targeting drifts toward clinical/treatment claims.
