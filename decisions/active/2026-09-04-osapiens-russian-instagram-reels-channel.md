# Activate Russian Instagram (osapiens_expert) as a Sapiens OS short-form content channel

## Decision

- **`osapiens_expert` Russian Instagram is now an active O!Sapiens/Sapiens OS content channel**, publishing short-form video (Reels) using the Velizhanin short-form methodology: causal/relatable-problem hook (причастная проблема) as the primary attention mechanic for cold audiences, normalization, a concrete "magic pill" step, VISP hook formula, Hunt's ladder for pillar targeting.
- **This channel carries general-audience Sapiens OS positioning, not Executive Biology.** Entry point is the free self-assessment (`osapiens.expert/rustest`) and the two course tiers (Быстрый старт, 1900₽; Полная перезагрузка, 9900₽) confirmed in `osapiens-russian-instagram-repackage.md` (2026-09-03, outside this repo at `~/Claude/output/`). Not the €350 Executive Performance Diagnostic, not the executive/founder audience.
- **This narrows, not reverses, `state/NOW.md`'s prior framing.** The line "Instagram is a possible supporting surface for [entheogen.expert], not an O!Sapiens acquisition channel" was written without this carve-out in view. English-language O!Sapiens (Executive Biology, LinkedIn-first) remains explicitly out of scope for Instagram — nothing here authorizes Reels/TikTok-style content on that brand's channels.

## Context

Across a same-day session (2026-09-04, outside this repo), Vlad gave a direct, repeated live instruction to build Reels scripts for his Russian Instagram using the content-marketing methodology of Nikolay Velizhanin (a Russian short-form growth coach), from two webinar transcripts he supplied. Earlier work that same day (`~/Claude/output/2026-09-03-osapiens-reels-hook-engine-ru.md` and its companion repackage doc) had already flagged the channel-authorization question as open, quoting this exact `state/NOW.md` line. When the conflict was surfaced back to Vlad mid-session, he responded directly: "перепиши решение с учетом новых вводных" (rewrite the decision given the new inputs) — an explicit instruction to resolve the open item, not to abandon the channel.

A `reels-script` skill (Velizhanin methodology, general/FANATIC scope) also exists at the Claude Code layer with its own gate excluding O!Sapiens/Sapiens OS by default, citing this same NOW.md guardrail. That skill's default exclusion is correct for the English/Executive Biology brand and is now explicitly overridden for the Russian Sapiens OS account only, per this decision.

## Alternatives

Leaving the guardrail as-is and treating the reels scripts already drafted as an unauthorized one-off test, to be shelved pending a separate strategy review — rejected, because Vlad gave a direct instruction to record this as a decision rather than leave it open.

## Rationale

Vlad's live instruction is rank 1 in the authority order (per `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`, and not one of its three non-negotiables). The original guardrail's intent — do not let O!Sapiens drift into an unplanned second acquisition channel without a decision — is satisfied by making the decision explicit here, not by refusing it.

## Consequences

- `state/NOW.md` and `state/active-projects.md` updated to reflect the new channel and point here.
- The reels-script skill's exclusion of O!Sapiens/Sapiens OS stays correct as a *default*; this decision is the explicit override for this one surface (Russian Instagram, general-audience Sapiens OS positioning) and should not be read as opening TikTok, Reels, or any short-form surface for English-language O!Sapiens/Executive Biology.
- No production capacity, filming cadence, or headcount has been allocated. Nothing has been filmed as of this decision. Do not treat drafted scripts as evidence of traction.
- Same brand-separation and clinical-scope rules apply as everywhere else in O!Sapiens/Sapiens OS material (no "ментальное здоровье," no outcome guarantees, no entheogen-brand vocabulary).

## Evidence and assumptions

No performance evidence yet (zero reels published as of 2026-09-04). This is a channel-authorization decision, not a validated-channel decision. Treat reach, saves, DM reply rate, test completions, and course conversions from actual published reels as the evidence that should trigger the next review, not assumed from the LinkedIn/Executive Biology motion.

## Revisit trigger

After the first 10-15 reels are published: review reach, saves, DM/comment reply volume, test completions, and whether this is displacing time from LinkedIn outreach (the primary O!Sapiens pipeline per `state/NOW.md`'s primary-channel framing). If it is displacing LinkedIn activity without evidence of its own, revisit.
