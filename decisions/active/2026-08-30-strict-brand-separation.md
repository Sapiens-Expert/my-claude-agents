# Maintain strict public brand separation

## Decision

Keep O!Sapiens/Executive Healthspan and entheogen.expert separate in public positioning, vocabulary, content, funnels, CTAs, and proof.

## Why

The audiences, trust conditions, safety context, and buying motives differ. Shared intellectual foundations do not justify public brand blending.

## Exception

Private client-initiated discussion with consent, scope clarity, and referral boundaries.
