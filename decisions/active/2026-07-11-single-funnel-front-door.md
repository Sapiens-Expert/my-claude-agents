# Use one funnel front door

## Decision

Use one primary O!Sapiens path: qualified relationship to fit call to paid Executive Performance Diagnostic to Performance Reset.

## Why

One front door reduces offer confusion, creates a measurable conversion chain, and prevents courses, communities, or content assets from becoming competing funnels.

## Revisit when

Reliable stage data shows a structural problem or a new front door has stronger validated economics.
