# Reduce LinkedIn comment volume

## Decision

Use approximately five highly targeted comments per working day instead of optimizing for high comment volume.

## Why

Later operating analysis identified diminishing returns from volume and prioritized relevance, relationship value, and qualified conversations.

## Previous approach

Higher-volume engagement, including plans in the range of 25 to 35 comments per week or more.

## Revisit when

Comment-to-profile, conversation, and qualified-relationship efficiency materially improves or the target audience changes.
