# Narrow carve-out: Sapiens OS framework as content structure on Entheogenic Renaissance YouTube

## Decision

The Sapiens OS behavior-change framework (name and structure, `frameworks/sapiens-os/overview.md`) may be used directly, by name, as the organizing structure for content on the Entheogenic Renaissance YouTube channel. Confirmed by Vlad directly, 2026-09-09, twice — once after a general bidirectional-brand-transfer risk was raised, and again after `decisions/active/2026-08-30-strict-brand-separation.md` was quoted to him directly, specifically its line "shared intellectual foundations do not justify public brand blending."

**This is a narrow amendment to DEC-2026-0830-01, not a reversal of it.** DEC-2026-0830-01 remains active and continues to govern everything it always governed, with this one named exception:

- **In scope (permitted):** the Sapiens OS framework name and structure appearing as content vocabulary on the Entheogenic Renaissance YouTube channel specifically.
- **Out of scope (DEC-2026-0830-01 still applies in full):** funnels, CTAs, proof, and audience crossover between O!Sapiens/Executive Healthspan and entheogen.expert. This decision does not permit directing viewers between the two funnels, does not permit O!Sapiens/executive-healthspan material to carry entheogen.expert vocabulary or positioning (non-negotiable #2, `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`, untouched), and does not extend to any entheogen.expert surface other than this YouTube channel unless separately confirmed.

## Context

During niche/strategy planning for the Entheogenic Renaissance YouTube channel, Vlad proposed merging psychedelics content with "mental health practices based on Sapiens OS framework for self-development." This was flagged before proceeding, twice, with increasing specificity:

1. First pass: general risk that public, English-indexed content linking Sapiens OS to psychedelics could damage the O!Sapiens sales motion (Priority #1, `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`), argued via `frameworks/branding/portfolio-decision.md` step 6 (bidirectional transfer). Vlad confirmed the override.
2. Second pass: `decisions/active/2026-08-30-strict-brand-separation.md` was located and quoted verbatim — a canonical, 10-day-old decision whose stated rationale ("shared intellectual foundations do not justify public brand blending") directly rejects the reasoning Vlad had given. Vlad confirmed the override again, against the specific document.

## Alternatives

- **Reuse the methodology under a new, entheogen-native name** (no shared brand vocabulary) — presented as the recommended option in both rounds. Rejected by Vlad both times in favor of using "Sapiens OS" directly.
- **Full reversal of DEC-2026-0830-01** — not what was confirmed. Vlad's instruction was about the framework/content layer specifically; nothing in the conversation touched funnels, CTAs, cross-audience acquisition, or O!Sapiens-side material. Scoped narrowly per the standing pattern for these overrides (`~/.claude/projects/-Users-macathome-Claude-Code/memory/feedback_guardrail-conflicts.md`: "narrow the override to exactly what was confirmed").

## Rationale

Vlad's live instruction is rank 1 in the authority order except the three non-negotiables in `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`. This does not touch any of them on a literal reading: no clinical/treatment claim is being made or approved, no EU MDR issue, and non-negotiable #2's literal text is one-directional (entheogen vocabulary must not appear in O!Sapiens/Sapiens OS material) — this is the reverse direction, which DEC-2026-0830-01 covers but the CLAUDE.md non-negotiable text does not.

No independent business rationale for the override was recorded beyond Vlad's direct confirmation. This is noted, not filled in with an invented justification.

## Consequences

- `brands/entheogen-expert/brand-separation.md` gets a carve-out pointer to this decision.
- `projects/entheogen-expert/PROJECT.md`'s "Constraint: strict separation from O!Sapiens" line gets a carve-out pointer.
- `projects/entheogenic-renaissance/PROJECT.md` gets a pointer noting the framework source.
- `decisions/active/2026-08-30-strict-brand-separation.md` is left unedited (provenance) — it remains the governing default for everything outside this one named exception.

## Evidence and assumptions

**Verified:** the exact text and date of DEC-2026-0830-01, confirmed by direct read before this decision was written.

**Unknown / not addressed by this decision:**
- Whether this changes if the YouTube channel's audience or content later crosses into funnel/CTA territory (e.g., a psychedelics viewer being offered an O!Sapiens diagnostic, or vice versa) — that would need separate, explicit confirmation; DEC-2026-0830-01 still prohibits it.
- Whether Vlad wants this same carve-out to extend to other entheogen.expert surfaces (Telegram, DMs, TikTok) — not asked, not assumed.
- The actual downside if this plays out as feared (an O!Sapiens prospect finds the psychedelics content via the shared framework name) has not been pressure-tested; this decision accepts that risk on Vlad's confirmation rather than resolving it.

## Revisit trigger

If a prospective O!Sapiens client, investor, or partner is found to have encountered the entheogen.expert content via the shared "Sapiens OS" name; if funnel/CTA crossover is later proposed; or if this carve-out is reconsidered.
