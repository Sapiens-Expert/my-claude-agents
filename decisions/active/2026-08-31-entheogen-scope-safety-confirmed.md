# Confirm entheogen.expert scope on suicidal ideation, medication screening, and facilitation

## Decision

- **Suicidal ideation: does not deal with these cases, period.** Confirmed by Vlad directly. Matches VladOS's prior position (`brands/entheogen-expert/audience-segmentation.md` Segment 9, "The Wrong Room") — the pre-VladOS material's "co-management alongside clinical care" framing was wrong, superseded.
- **Medication/interaction screening: not something he does, under any circumstance.** Confirmed by Vlad directly. Matches VladOS's prior position (`ethical-boundaries.md`) — the pre-VladOS material's claim that he personally assesses SSRI/MAOI/opioid interactions was wrong, superseded.
- **Facilitation: conditionally permitted, not excluded.** He facilitates when it takes place in a legal jurisdiction/environment — never underground or illegal. **This lands between the two prior sources, not on either side of them:** the pre-VladOS material described facilitation (accompanying clients to retreats) with no legal qualifier; VladOS's `coaching.md` previously excluded facilitation entirely as a blanket rule. Both were incomplete. `ethical-boundaries.md` and `coaching.md` updated to state the conditional directly.

## Context

Part of a 2026-08-30/31 cross-check between pre-VladOS knowledge and VladOS that surfaced entheogen.expert scope/safety contradictions — the highest error-cost category (legal/medical/regulatory scope). See `~/.claude/CLAUDE.md`'s non-negotiables section for how this was flagged before resolution.

## Alternatives

Treating VladOS's pre-existing position as simply "correct" on all three points and closing the question without asking — rejected, since the facilitation question turned out to have a real middle position neither source stated, which a same-side guess would have missed entirely.

## Rationale

Vlad's live instruction is rank 1 in the authority order. On two of three points it confirms VladOS's already-more-conservative position; on the third it corrects VladOS in the direction of allowing more than VladOS previously stated, but with a specific condition (legality of jurisdiction/environment) that must not be dropped in future summaries of this policy.

## Consequences

- `brands/entheogen-expert/ethical-boundaries.md` and `projects/entheogen-expert/coaching.md` updated.
- Content, DM responses, and referral protocol involving facilitation should now say "legal environments only," not "no facilitation" — a blanket exclusion is no longer accurate.
- `~/Claude/knowledge/entheogenic/` (pre-VladOS) remains unreconciled and lower-priority; this decision does not require editing it.

## Evidence and assumptions

**Still open, not addressed by this decision:** the Russian disclaimer text (`voice/entheogen-russian/disclaimer.md`) and the English `disclaimers.md` remain generic — the pre-VladOS material named specific contraindications (schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, family history) that neither current disclaimer states. Whether to add those specifics back in is a separate, still-unresolved question.

## Revisit trigger

If the disclaimer-contraindication question is resolved, or if the legal-environment condition on facilitation needs jurisdiction-specific detail.
