# Delay HealthSpan scale until validation

## Decision

Prioritize coaching cash flow and outcome instrumentation before substantial HealthSpan build or active fundraising. Continue only bounded validation work.

## Why

Coaching can generate cash, method proof, ICP learning, and outcome data. The app has extensive narrative and planning but insufficient demonstrated demand.

## Revisit when

Deposits, prototype use, pilot commitments, advisors, retention signals, or another predefined validation gate is achieved.
