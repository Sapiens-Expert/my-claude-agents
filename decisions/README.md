# Decisions

Material choices with rationale and revisit conditions. This is append-only strategic memory: do not erase major decisions. `active/` governs current work; `superseded/` preserves history and points to the replacing decision. Recommendations are not active decisions until the owner confirms them.

Decision filenames begin with the effective date. Every record has a stable ID, status, area, supersession metadata, rationale, and revisit trigger.
