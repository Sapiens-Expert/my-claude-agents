# FANATIC team and operations

Synthesized from five internal calls (2026-06-26, 2026-07-16, 2026-07-20, 2026-08-20, and an undated Vlad/Katya exit-terms call). Raw transcripts: `../../sources/transcripts/fanatic/`. This file answers several items `PROJECT.md` and `state/open-questions.md` list as unresolved — named owner, current finances, and team decisions — while leaving genuinely open items open.

Transcript quality across all five calls is low-fidelity (heavy Russian/Ukrainian code-switching, ASR noise) — treat granular specifics as `likely, not confirmed` even where stated plainly below.

## Ownership and role — resolves "named owner"

**Vlad holds 49% ownership (partner) and the Managing Director role at FANATIC.** Mostly delegated day-to-day, stays involved in specific processes: compensation structure, key client accounts, and — per the 2026-06-26 call — the operational/financial back end generally (accounting, resolving operational issues, financial planning, marketing and new-market strategy). LinkedIn's public title ("Partner") is a simplification of this, not a conflict with it.

**Sensitive, not public:** the 49% figure is real equity information, not published anywhere (not on LinkedIn, not elsewhere). FANATIC's accountant is Vlad's own sister (surname Andreeva) — stated directly by Vlad on the 2026-06-26 call. Both facts are for internal reasoning only; do not surface in LinkedIn copy, investor material, or any public-facing content without Vlad raising them himself.

- **Anton Averyanov** — client-facing; owns Portal, FutureVision, and personally stays on accounts where clients expect him by name (VkusVill, Azbuka Vkusa). Moving toward new-product development and new-client acquisition generally.
- **Arina Mokrousova** — projects/quality lead; escalates to Anton when needed; also owns HR/people design (grade and track system, individual comp tracks).
- **Zhenya** — reports to Vlad, owns the operational/commercial layer (proposals, contract support, project handoff). Under her: **Lera** (client-facing coordination) and **Anya** (accounting-adjacent). Surnames not captured in the calls.

- **Lola** — 3-month probation passed (per Arina). Formal in-house employment, promised in her original offer, delayed to around 2026-12-01 for cashflow reasons unrelated to performance; not yet told as of 08-20.

**Correction worth flagging for anyone reading the raw transcripts:** the Zoom label "FANATIC Agency" is a shared/generic account, not one fixed person — it's Anton on the 06-26 call, Zhenya on the two August calls. Identify the speaker from call content, not the label.

## Katya's departure — resolves the timeline

Katya resigned as of 2026-07-16 (new employer, corroborated but not cleanly confirmed as Sberbank — no severance beyond an unused-vacation payout and a percentage tied to closing the T-Bank project). A later two-person call between Vlad and Katya confirmed her **actual last day as September 11**, later than the two-to-three-week notice first estimated on 07-16. She kept running live projects (Pioneer, T-Bank supervision) through that date — which is why she still reads as an active, invested team member in the 08-20 call. Not a contradiction between calls, just a longer notice period than first expected.

Her exit bonus (the T-Bank-completion percentage) couldn't be paid as a lump sum given cashflow, so Vlad proposed a self-employment services contract over 6–7 months, paid faster if cashflow allows — structured through Vlad's personal ИП rather than FANATIC's ООО, his stated reasoning being that paying a just-terminated employee through the company would be a compliance red flag. **Sensitive, same tier as the ownership/accountant facts above** — an internal financial-structuring detail, not for public material. Her first handoff priority before leaving: the Nova Cosmetics client project (interview research with girls aged 13–16 about cosmetics/beauty habits).

## Diana's departure

Left FANATIC entirely, confirmed by 2026-08-20, after roughly five years' tenure. She had pushed hard and specifically on compensation for months before the exit (see below), repeatedly asking for concrete numbers that never landed. By 07-16, a colleague described her as already fully checked out, not completing remaining duties even in her final week.

## Cashflow — resolves "current finances" (as of 2026-08-20, likely stale by the time this is read)

Genuinely tight, confirmed first-hand: Vlad stated on the 08-20 call that at least two people on that call — plausibly including himself — weren't currently drawing pay from FANATIC. Specifics from that call: roughly 300,000 (currency not confirmed in transcript, presumed RUB) short that month for freelance costs plus taxes, waiting on an incoming payment from FutureVision/Pioneer to close it; T-Bank owed ~800,000, due early September; an ИП payment due the 25th. Team's own read: not quite a cashflow gap yet, but real risk of falling into one — partly because Diana's severance (~300,000) wasn't budgeted for. Roughly 6 million RUB in back pay was also referenced (07-20 call) as owed to the team from the old bonus system, expected to be substantially covered once Magnit's payment lands — not mentioned again by 08-20, so unclear if resolved or superseded by the tighter near-term picture.

**Still genuinely open:** Vlad's own income/commitment level, and whether this cashflow picture has moved since 08-20 — `state/open-questions.md` is right to keep this open.

## Compensation-system redesign — started 06-26, unresolved by 08-20

Anton proposed raising base pay and decoupling bonus from first-sale billing toward retention/upsell and internal contribution instead. Vlad's diagnosis: the existing system actively rewards isolated work against the team's own stated collaborative goals. Diana pushed hard for concrete numbers across two months and got only deferrals. The 07-20 call shows the same redesign still in provisional "v1" form. Not mentioned on 08-20 — unclear if shelved or handled elsewhere.

## Marketing — a self-diagnosed structural gap (07-16)

No functioning top-of-funnel marketing per Arina's own assessment: the Telegram channel only reaches people already in FANATIC's orbit; published articles build reputation, not leads. LinkedIn access was broken for roughly two months at that point — account locked after being accessed from Spain, flagged as suspicious activity; password recovered but the account-level block wasn't. LinkedIn and a founder-community platform ("Стратосфера"/Stratosfera) were the only two channels Anton named as reliable lead sources. A related gap: nobody had clear ownership of the marketing reporting line (Yulia was meant to report under Vlad alongside Anton; drifted after an undiscussed disagreement over a site redesign). Resolution status of either the LinkedIn block or the Yulia reporting-line meeting: unknown.
