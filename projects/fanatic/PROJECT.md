# FANATIC Performance & Growth System

- Status: Designed; authorization and current readiness unknown
- Objective: move from artifact-led agency delivery toward repeatable consulting quality, client impact, ownership, and organizational learning.

## Recommended first stage

Run a limited 90-day Quality & Growth Pilot on a small number of manageable projects. Assess financial, management, product, people, and capacity readiness first. Assign leads/reviewers by actual readiness, define quality at kickoff, protect limited reviews, give evidence-based feedback, and capture reusable learning.

Do not introduce bonus complexity before payment reliability, financial readiness, trust, and evidence quality exist. Historical obligations and performance recognition must remain separate.
