# Corporate workshops

- Status: Near-term O!Sapiens opportunity
- Candidate offer: “Performance Without Collapse.”
- Objective: paid delivery, authority, and qualified executive introductions using a repeatable workshop core.
- Needed: one-page offer, buyer list, pricing, deck, follow-up path, privacy rule, and outcome measure.

## Repeatable delivery system

Use the [workshop architecture](../../frameworks/consulting/workshop-architecture.md) and [delivery SOP](../../sops/coaching/workshop-design-and-delivery.md). The reusable core should include:

- a sponsor contract and participant research brief;
- one observable business/behavior outcome;
- an agenda that produces a decision, plan, or practiced capability;
- an evidence-calibrated Executive Biology module;
- participant application to real work without eliciting private health disclosure;
- named follow-up and outcome measurement;
- a legitimate bridge to a diagnostic conversation, never a disguised sales trap.

Do not claim workshop effectiveness from attendance, enjoyment, or the size of the source library. Establish repeatability through paid delivery, transfer evidence, sponsor acceptance, and qualified follow-up.
