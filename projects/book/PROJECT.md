# Book

- Status: Unscoped opportunity
- Gate: define audience, thesis, business role, source material, format, and what current priority it replaces before starting.

## Activation evidence

Before drafting chapters, confirm:

- a bounded reader problem and credible promise;
- why a book is the right product rather than articles, course, workshop, or diagnostic;
- a differentiated thesis supported by the completed deep-extraction program and Vlad's own evidence/IP;
- audience proof through repeated questions, qualified demand, or tested chapter/essay material;
- distribution/business role, rights/privacy position, maintenance implications, and opportunity cost;
- a chapter architecture in which every chapter advances the promised reader outcome.

If activated, use the [editorial-review SOP](../../sops/content/editorial-review.md). Story frameworks are optional diagnostic tools; the book must not turn client material into recognizable narrative or convert personal health experience into general efficacy claims.
