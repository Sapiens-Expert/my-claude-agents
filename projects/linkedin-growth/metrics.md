# Metrics

## Outcome and funnel

Qualified conversations, fit calls, diagnostics, coaching containers, revenue, referrals, stage conversion, cycle time, source attribution, and founder hours.

## Relationship and content diagnostics

Target-profile replies/comments, repeated relevant interactions, DMs initiated by target people, saves, profile views from relevant roles, and content-to-conversation movement.

Impressions, views, followers, connection count, reaction totals, generic engagement rate, and Social Selling Index are diagnostic signals rather than business outcomes. Never optimize a proxy when it degrades audience fit, trust, or conversion.
