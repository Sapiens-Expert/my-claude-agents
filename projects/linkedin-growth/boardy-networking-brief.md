# Boardy Networking Brief — O!Sapiens

## Purpose

Use Boardy to create relevant, double-opt-in introductions that lead to:

1. guest appearances by Vlad on established podcasts with the right business audience;
2. conversations with potential corporate buyers, especially HR, People, and L&D leaders in small and medium-sized companies;
3. relationships with credible partners who can bring O!Sapiens into founder and leadership communities.

This is a relationship-building brief, not a mass lead-generation campaign. Quality, timing, and mutual relevance matter more than introduction volume.

## Important operating rule

Ask Boardy to run the three lanes below as **separate matching requests**, in this order:

1. podcast guest-placement opportunities;
2. corporate buyers;
3. ecosystem partners.

Do not combine all three audiences in one broad request. Each introduction should have one clear reason for the conversation and one low-pressure next step.

## Authoritative media facts — do not reinterpret

- **Oh! Mental Health is not a podcast and does not exist. Never mention or invent it.**
- **Sapiens OS Healthspan is my newsletter. It is not a podcast.**
- I want to appear as a **guest on other people’s established podcasts**.
- I am **not currently recruiting or inviting guests to my own podcast**.
- For this lane, match me only with a verified current podcast host, producer, guest booker, or other person who can credibly invite or book me as a guest.
- Someone who merely wants to appear on podcasts is not a match.

## How to describe me

### Short version

I’m Vlad Andreev, founder of O!Sapiens and an executive performance coach with a background in commercial strategy, negotiation, and leadership development. I help founders and senior leaders understand the biological patterns affecting their energy, recovery, judgment, and behavior under sustained pressure—and turn those insights into practical changes.

### One-sentence positioning

O!Sapiens helps founders and senior leaders protect decision quality and sustainable performance when the cost of delivering has begun to rise.

### What makes the approach distinctive

- Executive context rather than generic wellness advice.
- Mechanism-based explanation translated into practical action.
- Attention to energy, sleep, stress load, recovery, decision quality, and behavior as an interconnected system.
- A non-medical, non-diagnostic approach focused on observable performance patterns.
- Commercial and organizational experience alongside coaching and behavior-change work.

### Language to avoid

Do not describe me as a doctor, neuroscientist, therapist, psychedelic therapist, healer, biohacker, or corporate wellness provider.

Do not use unverified numerical claims such as years of experience, executives served, workshops delivered, or coaching hours until those claims have been checked against source records.

## What I can offer

### For individuals

- A 90-minute Executive Performance Diagnostic.
- A written, practical protocol based on the patterns identified.
- A 90-day Performance Reset for founders and senior executives who need deeper implementation support.

### For organizations

- A focused workshop or leadership session provisionally framed as **Decision Quality Under Load**.
- The session examines how sustained pressure affects attention, judgment, recovery, and leadership behavior—and what leaders can change in practice.
- Possible formats include a leadership-team workshop, founder cohort session, executive offsite module, or pilot for a wider L&D program.

The corporate offer should be introduced as a focused performance and leadership intervention, not as a generic employee-wellness benefit.

# Lane 1 — Podcast Guest Placements

## Outcome sought

Warm introductions to English-language podcast hosts, producers, or guest bookers who could invite Vlad to appear as a guest. The purpose is to put O!Sapiens in front of founders, senior operators, leadership professionals, and people responsible for organizational performance.

This is one-directional: **Vlad is seeking guest appearances. He is not offering guests a place on a podcast of his own.**

## Ideal hosts and shows

Prioritize active interview podcasts covering one or more of the following:

- founder and executive performance;
- leadership under pressure;
- decision-making and judgment;
- sustainable high performance;
- organizational behavior and the future of work;
- sleep, stress, energy, and recovery in a serious business context;
- behavior change and applied science;
- founder health, healthspan, or longevity without biohacking hype;
- leadership development, executive coaching, or company culture.

Prefer shows that:

- publish consistently and have released an episode recently;
- interview practitioners and operators, not only celebrities or academics;
- serve founders, executives, senior managers, HR/L&D professionals, or investors who support founders;
- allow nuanced, educational conversations rather than promotional sound bites;
- have a clear guest format and evidence that the host prepares for interviews.

Before proposing an introduction, verify that the person currently hosts, produces, or books guests for an active podcast. Do not infer this from phrases such as “open to podcast opportunities,” previous guest appearances, or general interest in podcasting.

Audience relevance is more important than follower count.

## Strong episode angles

1. **Decision quality declines before performance visibly breaks**  
   How sustained load alters attention, judgment, patience, and leadership behavior while the executive still appears productive.

2. **The hidden biological debt of leadership**  
   Why the same workload can begin to cost more—and why discipline alone does not solve the problem.

3. **The founder as a load-bearing component of the company**  
   How a founder’s recovery and regulation affect decisions, relationships, and organizational stability.

4. **Why systems beat willpower in executive performance**  
   Translating physiology and behavior change into routines that work in demanding operating environments.

5. **Still delivering, no longer recovering**  
   Recognizing the stage between normal stress and visible collapse, when intervention is both possible and valuable.

## Value to the host

- A differentiated subject that connects biology to concrete leadership and business consequences.
- Practical frameworks listeners can use without purchasing products or following extreme protocols.
- A guest comfortable discussing commercial realities, executive behavior, negotiation, and organizational pressure—not only personal wellbeing.
- A calm, evidence-aware position that avoids diagnosis, miracle claims, and performance-guru language.

## The ask

A warm, double-opt-in introduction to explore whether one of the proposed angles would make a valuable episode. The first conversation is editorial and relational, not a sales pitch.

## Podcast non-fits for this lane

- Shows centered on recreational drug culture or psychedelic services.
- Pure consumer wellness, supplement, fitness, or biohacking shows with no executive context.
- Pay-to-play interview mills presented as earned editorial opportunities.
- Inactive shows or shows whose audience has little connection to leadership, business, or behavior change.
- People seeking podcast appearances for themselves who do not currently host, produce, or book an active show.

## Copy-and-paste request for Boardy

> I’m looking for warm introductions to verified hosts, producers, or guest bookers of active English-language podcasts whose audiences include founders, senior operators, or leadership and people-development professionals. I want to appear as a guest on their shows. I am not inviting guests to a podcast of my own. My topic is the biology of executive performance: how sustained pressure affects energy, recovery, attention, judgment, and leadership behavior, and how leaders can respond without relying on generic wellness advice or biohacking. I can offer practical episode angles such as “Decision Quality Under Load” and “Still Delivering, No Longer Recovering.” Please prioritize editorial fit and mutual relevance over audience size. Confirm that the person currently represents an active podcast before suggesting them. The ask is a short exploratory conversation about inviting me as a guest, with no expectation or sales pitch. My newsletter is called Sapiens OS Healthspan; it is not a podcast. Oh! Mental Health is not one of my projects and must never be referenced.

# Lane 2 — Corporate Buyers

## Outcome sought

Conversations with people who own leadership-development outcomes and could commission a focused workshop, leadership-team session, or pilot engagement.

## Ideal buyer profiles

### Primary buyers

- Head or Director of Learning & Development.
- Head or Director of People / People Operations.
- Chief People Officer in a small or medium-sized company.
- Leadership Development or Talent Development lead.
- HR Director with responsibility for management capability or executive wellbeing.

### Secondary buyers

- Founder, CEO, or COO in a company without a senior L&D function.
- Chief of Staff responsible for leadership effectiveness or executive offsites.
- Head of Talent at a founder-led or high-growth company.

## Company fit

Prioritize:

- approximately 30–500 employees;
- founder-led, privately held, or scaling companies;
- technology, professional services, consulting, creative, knowledge-work, and other cognitively demanding businesses;
- leadership teams operating through growth, restructuring, international expansion, sustained delivery pressure, or a demanding transition;
- organizations that value practical, confidential, evidence-aware leadership development.

Geographic preference: the UK and Europe, especially Spain, the Netherlands, DACH, and the Nordics, plus selective English-speaking organizations elsewhere where remote delivery is practical.

## Relevant timing signals

- Planning an executive offsite or leadership-development calendar.
- Sustained pressure is affecting leadership energy, judgment, communication, or retention.
- A recent growth phase, reorganization, acquisition, funding event, or senior leadership transition.
- Concern about burnout exists, but the organization wants something more useful than generic resilience training.
- Managers are technically capable but are struggling with recovery, attention, or behavior under load.

Boardy should not infer personal health problems from someone’s LinkedIn profile. These are organizational context signals, not diagnoses.

## Buyer problem

The company’s leaders are still functioning, but performance is becoming more expensive: recovery takes longer, judgment becomes less consistent, communication deteriorates under load, and ordinary leadership demands consume more capacity than before.

## Proposed first offer

**Decision Quality Under Load** — a focused leadership session on how sustained pressure changes attention, judgment, recovery, and leadership behavior, with practical ways to protect performance.

The initial conversation should test the buyer’s current context before proposing a format. Do not force a workshop pitch where there is no active need.

## Value to the buyer

- A concrete bridge between wellbeing and business performance.
- Language leaders can use without disclosing private health information.
- Practical interventions grounded in mechanisms and observable behavior.
- A format suitable for senior people who are skeptical of generic wellness content.
- Potential follow-on diagnostic or coaching support where appropriate and voluntarily chosen.

## The ask

A 25-minute exploratory conversation about how the organization currently supports decision quality and sustainable leadership under sustained pressure, and whether a pilot workshop or leadership session would be useful.

## Corporate non-fits

- Buyers looking only for mass-market wellness perks, entertainment, or a motivational keynote.
- Organizations requesting medical diagnosis, treatment, or access to employees’ private health data.
- Procurement processes with no internal sponsor or defined leadership problem.
- Introductions based only on title, with no plausible current relevance.

## Copy-and-paste request for Boardy

> I’m looking for warm introductions to Heads of L&D, People Directors, Chief People Officers, or equivalent leadership-development buyers in founder-led or scaling companies of roughly 30–500 employees. The strongest fit is a knowledge-work organization under sustained delivery pressure, growth, restructuring, or leadership transition. I help senior leaders understand and change the biological and behavioral patterns affecting energy, recovery, attention, judgment, and leadership behavior. A practical entry point is a focused session called “Decision Quality Under Load.” Please introduce me only where there is a plausible current need. The ask is a 25-minute exploratory conversation, not an immediate sale.

# Lane 3 — Ecosystem and Referral Partners

## Outcome sought

Build a small network of partners who already convene, advise, or support founders and leadership teams and for whom O!Sapiens would add a complementary capability.

## Ideal partners

- Executive coaches who do not specialize in physiology, recovery, or applied behavior change.
- Leadership-development consultancies and facilitators.
- Executive-offsite designers and strategy facilitators.
- Founder communities, peer networks, and membership organizations.
- VC, accelerator, and family-office platform or portfolio-support leaders.
- Boutique HR and talent advisers serving founders and leadership teams.
- Evidence-led occupational health or executive-health providers where professional boundaries are clear.

## Good partnership shapes

- A guest session for an existing founder or leadership community.
- A complementary module inside a leadership or offsite program.
- A co-hosted educational event or roundtable.
- Reciprocal referrals where client fit and consent are clear.
- An expert contribution to a newsletter, podcast, research project, or learning series.
- A small pilot used to test audience demand before building a larger collaboration.

## Partner value

O!Sapiens gives partners a credible way to address the biological layer of leadership performance without pretending to provide clinical care and without reducing the subject to generic wellness advice.

## The ask

A 30-minute fit conversation to compare audiences, client problems, and complementary capabilities. If there is a genuine fit, identify one small collaboration to test rather than designing a large partnership immediately.

## Partner non-fits

- People seeking access to the audience without offering reciprocal value.
- Product affiliates or supplement sellers wanting endorsement.
- Unlicensed providers making medical, therapeutic, or psychedelic-treatment claims.
- Broad “let’s collaborate” conversations with no shared audience or concrete experiment.

## Copy-and-paste request for Boardy

> I’m looking for a small number of credible ecosystem partners who already support founders or senior leadership teams: executive coaches, leadership consultancies, offsite facilitators, founder-community leaders, VC or accelerator platform teams, and selective executive-health providers. My work adds the biological and behavior-change layer to leadership performance without crossing into medical treatment or generic corporate wellness. I’m interested in a guest session, complementary program module, co-hosted roundtable, or carefully handled referrals. The ask is a 30-minute fit conversation followed, if relevant, by one small collaboration experiment.

# Instructions for Every Introduction

Ask Boardy to use these rules:

- Confirm mutual relevance and obtain consent from both parties before sharing contact information.
- Explain why the match makes sense now; do not send a generic biography.
- Keep the introduction short and specific.
- Use one ask only.
- Make it easy for either party to decline.
- Do not mention or infer anyone’s private health circumstances.
- Do not share confidential client information.
- Do not represent O!Sapiens as medical care.
- Do not offer discounts, commissions, affiliate arrangements, or exclusivity unless I have approved them directly.

## Suggested introduction format

> [Name], meet Vlad Andreev, founder of O!Sapiens and writer of the Sapiens OS Healthspan newsletter. Vlad works with founders and senior leaders on the biological and behavioral patterns that affect decision quality and sustainable performance under sustained pressure. I thought of connecting you because [specific, current reason based on the other person’s work]. Vlad would like to explore appearing as a guest on [verified active podcast name] to discuss [specific episode angle]. This is only an introduction if it feels useful to both of you—no obligation either way.

# First 60-Day Test

## Activity targets

- Podcast lane: 8 relevant introductions, 4 host conversations, and 2 confirmed or recorded appearances.
- Buyer lane: 10 relevant introductions, 5 exploratory conversations, 2 concrete proposals, and 1 paid pilot or qualified active opportunity.
- Partner lane: 6 relevant introductions, 3 fit conversations, and 1 small collaboration or referral experiment.

These are learning targets, not quotas for Boardy. Do not lower the match standard to reach them.

## Quality questions after every introduction

1. Was the person genuinely in the target audience?
2. Was there a credible reason to speak now?
3. Did the introduction accurately explain O!Sapiens?
4. Did the conversation uncover a concrete audience, buyer, or partnership need?
5. What wording or targeting signal should be changed before the next introduction?

## Commercial measures

Track:

- qualified conversations;
- podcast invitations and episodes published;
- workshop or pilot opportunities;
- proposals sent;
- paid engagements;
- referral relationships created;
- diagnostic inquiries attributable to introductions.

Do not judge the test primarily by LinkedIn follower growth, impressions, or raw introduction volume.
