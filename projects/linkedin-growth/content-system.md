# Content system

## Recurring forms

- Mechanism or precise reframe.
- Consented, de-identified case pattern.
- Personal data or professional observation with explicit limits.
- Research translation into an executive consequence.
- Diagnostic distinction that helps the reader see a costly pattern accurately.

Use one core idea, concrete language, an evidence level appropriate to the claim, and a restrained CTA. Content demonstrates judgment and creates recognition; direct conversations carry qualification and selling.

Measure qualified conversations and bookings attributable to content. Use saves, target-profile comments, profile views, and follows as diagnostic leading signals, not goals.
