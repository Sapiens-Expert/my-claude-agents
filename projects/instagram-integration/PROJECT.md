## Objective

Generate qualified, coaching-fit inbound conversations for entheogen.expert by pairing Instagram (qualification/conversion — older skew, functions as a landing page) with TikTok (discovery/reps — high volume, tests hooks cheaply). One integrated audience and evidence base serves both platforms; see `../tiktok-psychedelic-education/PROJECT.md` for the TikTok-specific execution, which shares this project's audience map and platform-risk register.

## Audience

Ten-segment map, full detail at `../../brands/entheogen-expert/audience-segmentation.md`. Core paying client: "the Stalled" (understood something real, three weeks later nothing changed) — Tier 1 commercial priority along with "the Buyer" (explicit support-seeker) and "the Re-doser" (repeat/escalation, highest disposable budget). Absolute no-sell segment: "the Wrong Room" (clinical, not coaching — any acute-risk signal ends the commercial conversation permanently, no exceptions).

## Content production method

New reel ideas for this account route through `/entheogen-shortform` first, which runs the non-negotiables, the idea-bank check, and the 12 platform-risk rules before anything gets drafted. Since 2026-09-04/06, `/entheogen-shortform` can draw structural options from the general-purpose `/reels-script` skill (`~/.claude/skills/reels-script/`) — the awareness-ladder for placing a topic and deciding Short-vs-longer-form, the 14-device opening catalog, and the VISP hook formula (Выгода/Интрига/Срочность/Причастность — Benefit/Intrigue/**Urgency**/Relatability; earlier notes and drafts calling this "VIZP" with a fourth letter of "Precision" were a transcription error, corrected 2026-09-04 — treat any VIZP reference as stale). This is a source of structural options only. It does not import `/reels-script`'s code-word CTA, "tragedization," or reaction-jacking tactics into entheogen content, and it does not touch the 7-pillar system, the no-CTA-ever rule on Pillar 3 (referral/difficult-experience) content, or any platform-risk rule — those stay governed entirely by `strategy.md` and `/entheogen-shortform`.

One drift worth reconciling, not yet fixed: `strategy.md` numbers "Difficult experiences and referral literacy" as Pillar 3, while `content/idea-bank/instagram-entheogen-content-pillars.md` numbers the same pillar (same name, same 8 ideas, same permanently-CTA-free rule) as Pillar 4 — the two canonical docs disagree on pillar numbering.
