# Topic map

The 280-page Digital Vlad corpus is a voice-and-topic archive, not a clinical evidence source.

## Core clusters

- Psychedelic science: mechanisms, neuroplasticity, placebo/context effects, uncertainty, and research literacy.
- Preparation and safety: set and setting, contraindications, interactions, sitter/facilitator competence, legality, and harm reduction.
- Difficult experiences: acute distress, “bad trips,” containment, meaning-making, and referral.
- Integration: insight–action gap, micro-behaviors, relationships, community, and longitudinal change.
- Mental health and trauma: non-shaming language, limits of explanatory models, medication nuance, addiction, and spiritual bypassing.
- Consciousness and meaning: philosophy, spirituality, ritual, skepticism, and the boundary between evidence and interpretation.
- Public systems: drug policy, stigma, access, ethics, commercialization, and cultural context.
- Adjacent foundations: sleep, nutrition, movement, stress regulation, habits, burnout, and social connection.

## Editorial invariant

Entheogens are tools, not saviors. No episode or derivative asset should turn an interview, personal account, or AI synthesis into a general medical claim.
