# Arkaya Living — Thailand

New project, no prior VladOS coverage. `arkayaliving.com` — wellness/longevity residential community in southern Thailand. Wesley owns the land and runs development; Vlad is described (by Wesley, on a recorded call, to a third party) as "one of my partners... related to wellness" — but no equity, revenue share, scope, or time commitment has been defined anywhere on record. This is not an O!Sapiens surface and not entheogen.expert-branded, but carries real medicine-adjacency risk (see below) — treat as its own brand context, firewalled in both directions.

## Current state

Pre-capital. Land secured (~5,000 sq m, Sichon Valley, Nakhon Si Thammarat province, southern Thailand — bought during COVID), master planning in progress, founding-membership applications open. Site markets 30-year residential leases (usage rights, not ownership) at $165,240, "Phase One" defined on the public site as 10 private wellness residences targeting mid-2027 completion. A separate, smaller "phase one" was described on the intro call (restaurant + lobby + one show unit, ~$150K) to fund workshops and pre-sell remaining units — **these are two different things sharing the same name; which is being sold to whom is unresolved.**

An investor with a property-management company has proposed injecting capital in exchange for management exclusivity, with an eye toward scaling to further Thailand/global locations, and has proposed de-risking by funding phase one only.

## Medicine-adjacency risk — a live firewall concern, not hypothetical

The intro call includes explicit psychedelic content: Vlad and the prospect (Martin) met at psychedelic-retreat education in the US roughly three years ago; discussion of whether a competing Portugal project "works with medicine"; Portugal's decriminalization; Vlad's own Ibogaine ceremony in South Africa, referenced on the call. **None of this appears on the Arkaya public site, which is correct — keep it that way.** If Arkaya ever markets medicine work directly, it becomes an entheogen.expert-adjacent surface and must never be cross-linked to O!Sapiens, LinkedIn, or investor material, per the standing brand-separation decision (`../../decisions/active/2026-08-30-strict-brand-separation.md`).

## People

Self-described as not having prioritized marketing — ran paid ads once or twice that produced substantial unfollowed-up leads.
- **Vlad** — wellness/programming partner, undefined terms.
- **Martin** — prospect met via this intro call, not a participant. 67, Switzerland-based, independent executive coach/trainer. Met Vlad at psychedelic-retreat education roughly three years ago.

## Prospect research (the actual value of the 2026-08-17 call)

Martin is not a near-term buyer — his capital and attention were committed through at least end of August 2026 to a competing Portugal project (already paid $10,000 for a membership slot there), and his Asia window is 4–6 months out. But he specified, unprompted, exactly what an affluent, health-focused, community-seeking prospect in his demographic wants and can't find in Asia: a "Plan B destination" to live 2–4 months a year (not primarily an investment — he has passive income and isn't return-driven); yoga, breathwork, healthy food; the ability to contribute his own talents/offerings under his own name rather than the organization's; genuine community-based infrastructure (the thing he says every Asian project he researched lacked); and no single nationality dominating the member base (his one live doubt about the Portugal alternative, which he described as "already a little bit too American"). **Treat this as free ICP research, not a live deal** — he offered to send a written wish-list of priorities, worth actively requesting if it hasn't arrived.

## Errors to fix before further prospect conversations

1. **Location was misstated to a prospect on this call.** Vlad said "Don Sac" (Don Sak, Surat Thani province) — the land is actually in Sichon, Nakhon Si Thammarat province, a different province.
2. **The public site shows two different monthly lease figures for the same product** — $459 and $499. $459 is the arithmetically correct one ($165,240 ÷ 360 months); $499 is wrong and live on a public sales page.
3. **Vlad's role has no defined scope, equity, or compensation anywhere in the record**, despite being described as a partner to a third party on a recorded call.
