# Assessment

Assess performance goal, symptoms/constraints in client language, sleep, recovery, energy, movement, nutrition, load, environment, readiness, and medical/referral needs. Output a prioritized coaching hypothesis and small behavior plan—not a diagnosis.

Do not use quick quizzes to recommend “the ideal program” for depression, suicidality, sleep disorders, or other clinical presentations. Screening and risk questions require validated instruments where appropriate, informed use, interpretation limits, and a defined referral/escalation pathway.
