# Evidence, privacy, and case use

## Source quarantine

Expanded O!Sapiens audience files contain identifiable or potentially re-identifiable historical client narratives, mental-health symptoms, relationship details, and suicidal ideation. They are private operational evidence only. Do not copy them into content, prompts sent to third parties, public case studies, testimonials, sales decks, or model-training datasets.

## Case-use gate

1. Confirm a legitimate purpose and minimum necessary data.
2. Separate service consent from optional marketing/research consent.
3. Obtain specific, informed, revocable permission for wording, channels, identity level, and duration.
4. Remove or generalize identifiers and test re-identification risk.
5. Verify outcomes, dates, denominator/context, non-response, and attribution limits.
6. Exclude crisis, suicidality, trauma, diagnosis, medication, family, sexual, or similarly sensitive detail unless an exceptional documented ethical/legal review authorizes it.
7. Let the client review and withdraw before publication.
8. Store the approval and schedule review/removal.

## Safety escalation

Current suicidal thoughts, intent, plan, inability to stay safe, or another acute risk is not a coaching-content opportunity. Follow the referral/crisis procedure and local emergency pathways; do not continue routine coaching as if the risk were merely a behavior-change barrier.
