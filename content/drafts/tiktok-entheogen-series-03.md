## Read this before posting

Three things about the underlying data that shaped this batch:

**The Tier 1 ranking is over-read.** The five top performers span 782–882 views — noise at this sample size. The real signal is roughly 800 vs. roughly 250 vs. suppressed. Treat Identity, Distinction, Curiosity Gap, Micro-Tool, and Before/After as effectively tied for first, not a ranked list.

**Hook type and topic are confounded.** The four hook types that landed in Tier 2 also carried the four most insider-dependent topics (bypassing, self-awareness as a trap, fear before deep work, numbness). Can't separate weak-hook from cold-topic from this data alone. Script S03-04 is designed specifically to separate them — see its experiment card.

**Suppression attribution is inference, not confirmation.** A specific prior script (S01-10) is the most likely trigger for a suppression event and the timing fits, but it isn't proven. If another suppression happens with none of the previously-flagged words present, the cause was something else.

**One thing outranks this entire batch.** 800-view videos producing 5 followers is a profile-conversion problem, not a content problem. **The bio was flagged as still pending at the time this batch was written — confirm it's been fixed before treating any of these results as informative.** Twelve more good videos into an unconverted profile produces twelve more good videos and roughly five more followers.

# Identity-based (4)

## S03-01 — The crash comes after

**Hook:** If you're calm in crises and wrecked after, listen. **Overlay:** THE CRASH COMES LATER

**Script:** If you're calm in crises and wrecked after, listen. During the emergency you were fine. Sharp, even. Then it ended. And you fell apart. People find this confusing. It's backwards, apparently. Here's one mechanism. Under high demand, your system holds the line. It suppresses what would slow you down. Tiredness, grief, fear. All of it gets deferred. Not cancelled. Deferred. When demand drops, the suppression stops. Everything you postponed arrives at once. So the crash isn't weakness after the fact. It's the bill for the performance. This isn't the whole story. Sleep, load, and health all matter too. Practical version. Stop scheduling recovery for the day after the deadline. Protect the week after. That's when it lands. Save this for the week after the next big push.

**Description:** Holding it together during the crisis and falling apart after is a sequence, not a contradiction. Save this for the week after the next big push. #executivecoaching #burnout #highachievers #recovery #innerwork

**Experiment card:** Type: Identity · Topic: delayed emotional processing (Tier 2) · new topic, proven hook type. Hypothesis: the strongest hook type applied to an untested Tier 2 cluster reaches Tier 1 performance. Primary metric: views, then saves. Success: 700+ views, saves above batch median. Failure: under 400 views (cluster too narrow).

## S03-02 — The one nobody checks on

**Hook:** If nobody ever worries about you, listen. **Overlay:** THE RELIABLE ONE

**Script:** If nobody ever worries about you, listen. You're the stable one. The one who handles it. People bring you their problems. Nobody brings you concern. That's not an accident. It's a role. Here's how it forms. You were competent early. People noticed. They stopped checking. Reliability removes you from other people's monitoring. It's efficient for everyone. Including you, for a while. The cost shows up later. You develop no practice at being the one who needs something. So when you do, you have no idea how to say it. And nobody around you is watching for signs. For some people this stays manageable for decades. For others it doesn't. Practical version. Pick one person. Tell them one true thing this week. Not a crisis. Just something real. That's the whole exercise. Follow if you're the one nobody checks on.

**Description:** Being the reliable one removes you from everyone else's monitoring. It works until it doesn't. Follow if you're the one nobody checks on. #executivecoaching #leadership #founders #loneliness #innerwork

**Experiment card:** Type: Identity · Topic: leadership isolation (Tier 1) · direct iteration on S02-03 (882 views). Hypothesis: the winning cluster has more than one video in it. Primary metric: follows. Success: follows at or above S02-03. Failure: under 500 views (S02-03 was the specific idea, not the territory).

## S03-03 — Decision debt

**Hook:** If you decide all day and can't pick dinner, listen. **Overlay:** DECISION DEBT

**Script:** If you decide all day and can't pick dinner, listen. Forty calls in a day. Real ones. Money, people, direction. Then someone asks where to eat and you go blank. That contrast confuses people. It shouldn't. Here's one mechanism. Decision quality behaves like a resource that depletes across a day. The big ones early get your best judgment. By evening you're running on the remainder. So the trivial choices fail first. They're just last in line. This is one explanation, not the only one. Sleep debt and chronic load do similar things. But the pattern is worth noticing. Practical version. Move the decisions that matter into your first four hours. Automate or delegate the ones that don't. Same dinner, same clothes, same route. Free the capacity for what needs it. Save this for the next time 6pm feels impossible.

**Description:** Handling forty real decisions and then failing at dinner is a sequence problem, not a character one. Save this for the next time 6pm feels impossible. #executivecoaching #decisionfatigue #leadership #burnout #depthwork

**Experiment card:** Type: Identity · Topic: decision-making under pressure (Tier 2) · new. Hypothesis: a concrete, universally recognizable moment (blanking on dinner) outperforms abstract framing of the same idea. Primary metric: views and completion. Success: 700+ views. Failure: under 400 (decision fatigue too well-covered elsewhere).

## S03-04 — The ten-year maybe (the clean test)

**Hook:** If you've been meaning to do the work for years, listen. **Overlay:** THE TEN-YEAR MAYBE

**Script:** If you've been meaning to do the work for years, listen. You've read about it. Bookmarked things. Talked to one person once. And never started. You call it not the right time. Sometimes that's accurate. Here's the part that's worth checking. Waiting can look like caution when it's actually a request. A request for certainty that the process will go well. That certainty doesn't exist and won't arrive. So the waiting has no natural end. Now the honest other side. Some waiting is correct. Real instability, health issues, a bad year. Those are reasons, not avoidance. A professional can help you tell the difference. Practical version. Write down what would have to be true for you to start. Look at the list. Is it conditions, or is it a guarantee? Conditions can be met. Guarantees can't. Comment how many years you've been at this.

**Description:** Waiting for the right time and waiting for a guarantee look identical from the inside. One of them ends. Comment how many years you've been at this. #personalgrowth #fearofchange #transformation #healingjourney #innerwork

**Experiment card — this is the confound test:** Type: Identity · Topic: readiness and fear (Tier 3, held). Hypothesis: S02-07 underperformed because of its hook type, not its topic — same territory, Tier 1 hook type, no insider vocabulary. Primary metric: views, compared directly against S02-07. Success: 600+ views — Tier 3 topics are recoverable with better hooks and can return to rotation. Failure: under 350 — the topic itself doesn't travel cold, and Tier 3 stays parked until there's a warm audience.

# Distinction (3)

## S03-05 — Rest and distraction

**Hook:** Rest and distraction aren't the same thing. **Overlay:** THE FAKE OFF SWITCH

**Script:** Rest and distraction aren't the same thing. Most people have replaced one with the other and haven't noticed. Here's the difference. Distraction moves your attention somewhere else. The scroll, the show, the second screen during the first screen. Your attention relocates. Your load doesn't drop. Rest lowers the demand on the system. Less input. Fewer decisions. Nothing to track. That's why four hours of scrolling leaves you flatter than when you started. You weren't resting. You were being occupied. For some people distraction genuinely helps. It's not useless. It's just not the thing you keep expecting it to be. Practical version. One twenty-minute block this week with no input at all. No phone, no podcast, no productive walking. It will feel worse before it feels like anything. Save this for the next time an evening disappears.

**Description:** Four hours of scrolling and four hours of rest do different things to the same nervous system. Save this for the next time an evening disappears. #burnout #rest #nervoussystem #habits #innerwork

**Experiment card:** Type: Distinction · Topic: rest versus avoidance (Tier 1) · new topic in a proven cluster. Hypothesis: Distinction plus a very common behavior produces the highest save rate in the batch. Primary metric: saves. Success: top 3 in batch for saves. Failure: saves below batch median (practical step too vague to save for).

## S03-06 — Wanting out and wanting change

**Hook:** Wanting out and wanting change aren't the same thing. **Overlay:** EXIT OR DIRECTION

**Script:** Wanting out and wanting change aren't the same thing. They feel identical at 11pm on a Tuesday. They aren't. Here's the test. Picture the thing you'd leave for. In detail. Not "somewhere quieter." What would Monday actually look like? If the picture fills in, that's direction. If it stays blank, that's relief you're after. Both are legitimate. They need different responses. Direction means planning. Relief means your current load is unsurvivable as configured. People mix these up and make expensive moves. They quit the job when the problem was the hours. Or they fix the hours when the problem was the direction. This isn't a rule. Some people need the exit to find the direction. Practical version. Before any big move, write the alternative in specifics. The specificity of that answer tells you which problem you have. Save this for the next 11pm.

**Description:** The fantasy of leaving and a genuine change of direction feel the same at night. One of them has details. Save this for the next 11pm. #executivecoaching #careertransition #leadership #meaningcrisis #depthwork

**Experiment card:** Type: Distinction · Topic: success misalignment (Tier 2) · new. Hypothesis: Tier 2's strongest cluster converts to Tier 1 performance when framed as a distinction rather than a state. Primary metric: views and shares. Success: 700+ views. Failure: under 400 (career-exit content too saturated on the platform).

## S03-07 — Support and treatment

**Hook:** Support and treatment aren't the same thing. **Overlay:** TWO DIFFERENT JOBS

**Script:** Support and treatment aren't the same thing. People spend a year with the wrong one because nobody explained the difference. Treatment addresses a condition. Depression, trauma, anxiety disorders, anything clinical. It requires a licensed professional. That licensing exists for a reason. Support helps a functioning person move. Structure, accountability, reflection, follow-through on a decision. That's a different job with different training behind it. Here's the sorting question. Are you stuck, or are you unwell? Stuck means you know roughly what you want and can't get traction. Unwell means the symptoms are running the week. If you're not sure, that's normal. Start with the clinical assessment. A good professional will tell you if you're in the wrong room. And anyone who claims their approach covers both is telling you something useful about themselves. Save this before you hire anyone.

**Description:** Hiring the right professional for the wrong job costs a year. The sorting question takes a minute. Save this before you hire anyone. #mentalhealth #therapy #executivecoaching #healingjourney #depthwork

**Experiment card:** Type: Distinction · Topic: professional confusion (Tier 2) · iterates S01-12 with a wider, non-insider frame. Hypothesis: removing the post-experience framing widens reach without losing the qualified viewer. Primary metric: saves and profile visits. Success: 600+ views with above-median profile visits (this hook attracts people actively looking to hire). Failure: high views, low profile visits (reads as general education, not a signal about Vlad specifically).

# Curiosity gap (3)

## S03-08 — Sick on the first day off

**Hook:** Why do you get sick the first day of vacation? **Overlay:** THE HOLIDAY CRASH

**Script:** Why do you get sick the first day of vacation? Eleven months without missing a day. Then you land in a nice place and immediately fall apart. People joke about this. It happens too often to be a joke. Here's the leading explanation. Sustained demand keeps you mobilized. Mobilized systems postpone the things that would take you offline. Recovery, inflammation, the full weight of fatigue. When demand drops, that postponement ends. Everything that was queued arrives while you're finally still. Worth being precise here. This is a plausible pattern, not a settled finding. Some of it is also just travel, sleep disruption, and crowded airports. Practical version. Don't fly on day one. Build a buffer. Your system needs a landing strip, not a cliff. And if this happens every single time, that's information about your baseline. Save this before your next holiday.

**Description:** Eleven months of holding it together, then day one of the break. The timing is not a coincidence. Save this before your next holiday. #burnout #recovery #stress #highachievers #innerwork

**Experiment card:** Type: Curiosity Gap · Topic: delayed processing plus sleep and stress (Tier 1 adjacent) · direct iteration on S02-05 (807 views). Hypothesis: the "why does this specific universal thing happen" format is the most repeatable structure in the account. Primary metric: views. Success: 800+ views (confirms the format, not just the topic). Failure: under 500 (the earlier topic was strong, not the format).

## S03-09 — Sunday evening

**Hook:** Why is Sunday evening worse than Monday morning? **Overlay:** THE SUNDAY GAP

**Script:** Why is Sunday evening worse than Monday morning? Monday you're in it. Busy, moving, no time to feel anything. Sunday at six you have nothing to do but forecast. Here's the mechanism. Anticipation and execution use different parts of your attention. Executing narrows you. Anticipating opens the whole picture. So Sunday shows you the shape of the week and the shape of the life. Both at once, with no task to hide behind. Now the useful distinction. Mild Sunday reluctance is normal. Most people have it. It means nothing. Consistent Sunday dread that survives a good weekend is different. That one is usually pointing at direction, not workload. Rest fixes workload. It doesn't fix direction. Practical version. Next Sunday, ask one question. Is this about the tasks or about the destination? Tasks are fixable this week. Destination takes longer, and knowing which is which saves you months. Save this for Sunday.

**Description:** Sunday evening gives you the one thing the week doesn't: nothing to hide behind. Save this for Sunday. #burnout #meaningcrisis #executivecoaching #leadership #depthwork

**Experiment card:** Type: Curiosity Gap · Topic: sleep and anxiety mechanisms (Tier 1) · new topic, proven cluster and format. Hypothesis: Sunday is the single most universal timestamp available to this audience. Primary metric: views and shares. Success: 800+ views. Failure: under 500 (Sunday dread too saturated by other creators).

## S03-10 — When the ideas stop

**Hook:** Why do your best ideas stop when you need them most? **Overlay:** WHEN THE IDEAS STOP

**Script:** Why do your best ideas stop when you need them most? The quarter that matters. The pitch. The turnaround. Exactly when you need range, you get nothing. Here's one mechanism. Under pressure, attention narrows toward the threat. That narrowing is useful. It's how you don't miss the obvious. But original thinking needs the opposite condition. It needs slack. Wandering. Inputs that aren't the problem. Pressure removes precisely the state that generates range. So you push harder into the exact conditions that block it. This is one factor among several. Sleep and load matter as much. It's not the only reason a project stalls. Practical version. When the ideas stop, change the input, not the effort. Twenty minutes, different room, no phone, nothing productive. This feels irresponsible during a crunch. That's the point. Follow if your best ideas stopped arriving.

**Description:** Pressure narrows attention, and narrow attention is the wrong condition for range. Pushing harder makes it worse. Follow if your best ideas stopped arriving. #creativity #burnout #executivecoaching #highachievers #innerwork

**Experiment card:** Type: Curiosity Gap · Topic: creative block as a stress response (Tier 2) · new. Hypothesis: creative-block framing pulls in founders and creatives who don't self-identify with burnout language. Primary metric: follows and audience composition in comments. Success: 600+ views with comments from a visibly different segment than the burnout videos. Failure: strong views with no follows (useful content that doesn't signal what Vlad does).

# Micro-tool (1)

## S03-11 — Rest or change

**Hook:** Three questions when you can't tell what you need. **Overlay:** REST OR CHANGE

**Script:** Three questions when you can't tell what you need. You're depleted. You don't know if you need a break or a different life. These separate them. Question one. Does a genuinely good week fix it? Not a good day. A full week with sleep and no fires. If yes, you're depleted. If it comes back by Wednesday, keep going. Question two. Is it the work or the direction? Same job, half the hours. Would that solve it? If yes, it's load. If no, it's direction. Question three. When did you last look forward to something? Not relief from something. Toward something. If you can't find one in six months, that's worth naming out loud. Three questions. Two minutes. This isn't a diagnostic. If the answers are dark, talk to a professional. It's a way to stop guessing. Save this for the next time you can't tell.

**Description:** Depleted and misaligned feel the same from the inside. Three questions separate them in two minutes. Save this for the next time you can't tell. #burnout #executivecoaching #meaningcrisis #highachievers #innerwork

**Experiment card:** Type: Micro-Tool · Topic: burnout distinctions plus success misalignment (Tier 1 and 2) · iterates S02-08 (793 views). Hypothesis: a tool applied to the highest-pain decision in the audience produces the batch's top save rate. Primary metric: saves. Success: highest saves in the batch. Failure: views in line with batch but saves below median (the "three questions" format has stopped earning saves).

# Before/after awareness (1)

## S03-12 — Before you bring it home

**Hook:** Before you tell your partner, read this. **Overlay:** BEFORE YOU BRING IT HOME

**Script:** Before you tell your partner, read this. Something shifted for you. A decision, a realization, a change you can feel. You want to bring it home tonight. Here's what usually goes wrong. You've been processing this for months. Quietly, mostly in your head. They get the conclusion in one sentence over dinner. You've had the whole journey. They get the destination with no map. So their reaction reads as resistance. It's usually just speed. Three things that help. One. Share what you're noticing, not what you've concluded. "I've been feeling" lands very differently from "I've decided." Two. Give them the timeline. Tell them how long you've been sitting with it. Three. Expect a delay. They need weeks too. You had months. This isn't about softening the truth. It's about not confusing pace with disagreement. Send this to your partner before you talk.

**Description:** You had months with it. They get one sentence at dinner. Their reaction is usually pace, not resistance. Send this to your partner before you talk. #marriage #relationships #communication #personalgrowth #innerwork

**Experiment card:** Type: Before/After · Topic: disclosure after personal change (Tier 1) · iterates S02-09 (782 views), moved from workplace to home. Hypothesis: the domestic version generates higher shares than the workplace version, because it has a specific person to send it to. Primary metric: shares. Success: highest shares in the batch. Failure: shares below S02-09 (workplace anxiety is the stronger driver in this audience).
